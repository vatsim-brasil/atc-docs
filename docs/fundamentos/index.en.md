# Fundamentals

VATSIM is a free online network where flight simulator pilots fly and virtual controllers provide air traffic services, in real time and by voice. VATSIM Brasil looks after Brazilian airspace on the network: standards, controller training and events.

This section is for newcomers. Choose your path:

    [:octicons-arrow-right-24: Controller path](controlador/index.en.md)

-   :material-airplane-takeoff:{ .lg .middle } **I want to fly with ATC**

    ---

!!! tip "You don't have to pick just one"
    Many controllers also fly, and the reverse is true. Flying with ATC is the best way to understand what pilots need to hear, and controlling shows why ATC asks for what it asks.

## Four ideas to get started

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-card-account-details-outline:{ .lg .middle } **CID**

    ---

    Your VATSIM account number. You use it to connect, as a pilot or a controller, and it is what appears on your profile and in the statistics.

-   :material-stairs-up:{ .lg .middle } **Rating**

    ---

    The controller's qualification. Each rating unlocks a set of positions, from Ground to Center, and is earned through study, training and assessment.

-   :material-headset:{ .lg .middle } **Position**

    ---

    The "seat" a controller occupies, such as `SBGR_TWR` or `SBBS_CTR`. The suffix tells you the type of service: `DEL`, `GND`, `TWR`, `APP` or `CTR`.

-   :material-arrow-collapse-down:{ .lg .middle } **Top-down**

    ---

    A position covers the empty positions below it. With no tower online, the aerodrome is served by APP or Center.

</div>
