# On Frequency

Talking on the radio is the scariest part of a first flight, and the part that improves most with practice. This page covers the basics of phraseology and has a **radio simulator**: a complete departure from Brasília (SBBR) to Guarulhos, from the gate to Control.

## Before you press the button

1. **Listen.** Wait for the frequency to be clear. Don't interrupt an exchange between the controller and another pilot.
2. **Think.** Know what you are going to say before you transmit. Write it down if you need to.
3. **Speak.** Who you are calling, who you are, what you want: *"Brasília Ground, GOL ONE FOUR ONE NINE, ready for taxi."*
4. **Read back.** Repeat the instructions you received and end with your callsign.

## The simulator

Choose a mode and advance message by message:

- **Follow along:** messages appear one by one, as if you were listening to the frequency.
- **Practise as pilot:** the pilot's lines are hidden. Say out loud what you would transmit, then check.
- **Practise as controller:** the same, with the ATC lines.

## What to always read back

A readback is repeating the instruction so the controller can confirm you understood. Always read back:

- route, departure and arrival clearances;
- **levels**, altitudes, headings and speeds;
- **runways**: enter, line up, take off, land, cross, hold short;
- **squawk code** and **frequencies**;
- altimeter setting.

Always end with your callsign. If the controller says *"read back correct"*, carry on; if they correct you, read back again.

## Words you will use

| Portuguese | English | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Afirmativo | Affirm | Yes |
| Negativo | Negative | No, or "not approved" |
| Ciente | Roger | I have received your message |
| Cumprirei | Wilco | I understand and will comply |
| Repita | Say again | Repeat your last message |
| Incapaz | Unable | I cannot comply |
| Prossiga | Go ahead | Send your message |
| Aguarde | Standby | Wait, I will call you back |

## Numbers and letters

On the radio, every number is said digit by digit: *GOL ONE FOUR ONE NINE*, *flight level THREE FOUR ZERO*. NINE is often pronounced *NINER*. The decimal point in a frequency is *DECIMAL*. Letters use the phonetic alphabet: **A** is *ALFA*, **B** is *BRAVO*, **C** is *CHARLIE*. In Portuguese, some digits change: **1** is *UNO* and **6** is *MEIA*.

!!! tip "It's OK to make mistakes"
    Every controller has heard a nervous first flight. If you freeze, take a breath and say *"say again"*. If you can't do what was asked, say *"unable"*. Nobody expects perfection; they expect safety and clarity.
