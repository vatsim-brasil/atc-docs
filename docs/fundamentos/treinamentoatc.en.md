# Starting ATC Training

## Introduction

Interested in becoming an Air Traffic Controller on the VATSIM Network, controlling one of the busiest airspaces in South America? Here is everything you need to know to get started.

## 📌 Requirements and Expectations

Becoming a controller requires:

* Commitment
* Discipline
* Self-study
* Willingness to keep learning

VATSIM follows a rating system that determines which positions you may control, similar to real-world ATC training.

!!! warning "Important"
    You must be a resident member of Vatsim Brasil before starting ATC training.

Check your status and how to join here: [Joining Vatbrz](https://atc.vatsim.com.br/fundamentos/inscricao/)

## 🧰 Software Used

Before observing or training, install the following programs:

- [Euroscope and Sector Files](https://atc.vatsim.com.br/fundamentos/softwares/euroscope/)
- [TrackAudio](https://atc.vatsim.com.br/fundamentos/softwares/trackaudio/)
- [vATIS](https://atc.vatsim.com.br//fundamentos/softwares/vatis/)

## 🎓 ATC Ratings on VATSIM

The VATSIM Brasil ATC rating system is divided into levels, each with its own responsibilities and permissions:

| Rating | Description                       |
|--------|-----------------------------------|
| S1     | Developing Controller             |
| S2     | Aerodrome Controller              |
| S3     | Terminal Controller               |
| C1     | Enroute Controller                |
| C3     | Senior Controller                 |
| I1     | Instructor                        |
| I3     | Senior Instructor                 |

### Requirements

#### S1 (Developing Controller)
- Pass a theory exam at the [Vatsim Brasil Training Center](<https://treinamento.vatsim.com.br>).
- Familiarization training with one of our mentors.
- The rating allows you to control all **_DEL and _GND** positions within the Vatsim Brasil jurisdiction.

#### S2 (Aerodrome Controller)
- Pass a theory exam at the [Vatsim Brasil Training Center](<https://treinamento.vatsim.com.br>).
- In-depth training with one of our mentors.
- Practical check.
- The rating allows you to control all **_TWR** positions within the Vatsim Brasil jurisdiction.

#### S3 (Terminal Controller)
- Pass a theory exam at the [Vatsim Brasil Training Center](<https://treinamento.vatsim.com.br>).
- A minimum of **10h** of connection time on **_TWR as an S2**.
- In-depth training with one of our mentors.
- A minimum of **10h** of solo trainee time as **_APP**.
- Practical check.
- The rating allows you to control all **_APP** positions within the Vatsim Brasil jurisdiction.

#### C1 (Enroute Controller)
- Pass a theory exam at the [Vatsim Brasil Training Center](<https://treinamento.vatsim.com.br>).
- A minimum of **10h** of connection time on **_APP as an S3**.
- In-depth training with one of our mentors.
- A minimum of **15h** of solo trainee time as **_CTR**.
- Practical check.
- The rating allows you to control all **_CTR** positions within the Vatsim Brasil jurisdiction.

#### I1 (Instructor)
- The requirements for the I1 rating can be found in section 4.1 of the [Division Policy - VATSIM Brasil](<https://vatsim.com.br/documentos/politica-de-divisao>).

#### C3 and I3 (Senior Controller and Senior Instructor)
The requirements for the C3 and I3 ratings can be found in sections 3.6 and 4.2, respectively, of the [Division Policy - VATSIM Brasil](<https://vatsim.com.br/documentos/politica-de-divisao>).

## 📖 Enrolling in S1 Training

To start your ATC training at Vatsim Brasil, follow these steps:

* Visit the Vatsim Brasil Training Center.
* Sign up and enroll in the S1 course.
* Complete the course and pass the theory exam.
* Once you have passed, open a ticket on the Vatsim Brasil Discord to schedule your first practical training session with an ATC mentor.

## 🛫 Practical Training

Practical training consists of one or more individual sessions with a certified ATC mentor. During these sessions, you will practice air traffic control in a simulated environment, applying the knowledge acquired in the theory course.

## 🧪 Rating Award

After completing all practical training sessions, the ATC mentor will assess your performance and determine whether you are ready to receive your S1 rating.

✅ If you are deemed ready, the mentor will forward your approval to the Vatsim Brasil training team, which will grant your rating in a timely manner.

## 🌐 Next Steps

After obtaining your S1 rating, you can continue your development as an ATC controller, pursuing more advanced ratings and taking part in events on the VATSIM network.
