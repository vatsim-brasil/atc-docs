# New SBPA page

The Porto Alegre page was rewritten from the current AIP AD 2 SBPA. It now summarizes the use of runway 11/29, explains the DCL, adds the Ground and Tower areas of responsibility and the ground flow with diagrams for runways 11 and 29. It also covers takeoff points and intersections, runway exits, the hotspot, taxi restrictions, HIRO, reduced runway separation minima (RRSM) and a table of aprons and airlines.
