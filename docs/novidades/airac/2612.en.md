# AIRAC 2612

The packages of all five FIRs were updated to AIRAC 2612. Update them with the **[Sector File Manager](https://github.com/VectorsATCGroup/euroscope-sector-file-manager)**, with EuroScope closed: the dashboard shows **Update** for every FIR with a new version.

| FIR | Highlights |
| --- | --- |
| [SBAO · Atlântico](#sbao) | New UZ33 routing and visibility center for SUEO_FSS |
| [SBAZ · Amazônica](#sbaz) | Navaids and fixes of Colombia, Peru and Venezuela; procedures at SBPV, SBSL, SBJI and SBCZ |
| [SBBS · Brasília](#sbbs) | New SIDs, STARs and approaches at Confins, Pampulha and Lagoa Santa; SNLG aerodrome |
| [SBCW · Curitiba](#sbcw) | Redrawn Rio CTRs, SBJR becomes a CTR, Cabo Frio FIZ; ground layouts for SBCH and SBZM |
| [SBRE · Recife](#sbre) | SID UTMAR2A at Fortaleza; sector SBBS_NS_CTR renamed to BSNS |

Airways UL795, UM654, UN741, UZ4, UZ6, UZ8, UZ23, UZ33, UZ35, UZ40, UZ72, Z2, Z3 and Z48 had their segments updated in every continental FIR, following the repositioned fixes. Every FIR also got the updated ICAO airport database (additions, removals and corrected names).

## SBAO · Atlântico FIR {#sbao}

### Navigation data

- Repositioned fixes AKSUG, CF001, CF002, CF003 and others, with precision adjustments to ANSEM, EGBIG, UGULO and VULAD.
- Airway UZ33 rerouted: it now goes through GATUR and SURTI instead of EDMUR, KORMU and OPGOL.
- Updated the segments of airways UL795, UM654, UN741, UZ4, UZ6, UZ8, UZ23, UZ35, UZ40, UZ72, Z2, Z3 and Z48, following the repositioned fixes.

### Sectors

- Added the visibility center of the SUEO_FSS position (Montevideo Oceanico).

## SBAZ · Amazônica FIR {#sbaz}

### Navigation data

- Added the navaids and fixes of the neighbouring countries north and west of the FIR (Colombia, Peru and Venezuela): 25 VORs (such as BOG, IQT and LET), 6 NDBs and about 1,000 fixes.
- Added the Tabatinga (TBT) and São Gabriel da Cachoeira (GBR) VORs.
- Repositioned fixes AKSUG, CF001, CF002, CF003, GILGO and FF27, with precision adjustments to other fixes.
- Porto Velho (SBPV): fix PV022 replaced by PV004.
- Updated the segments of airways UL795, UM654, UN741, UZ4, UZ6, UZ8, UZ23, UZ33, UZ35, UZ40, UZ72, Z2, Z3 and Z48.

### Procedures

- Porto Velho (SBPV): SID MUPEG1C (RWY 19) now goes through PV004 instead of PV022.
- São Luís (SBSL): the TEGEP transition of SIDs GERKO1A (RWY 24) and TEKIT1A (RWY 06) replaced by the USILA transition.
- Ji-Paraná (SBJI): transitions JI021, JI023 and JI027 of the RNAV 21 extended to JI018, and updated drawings of the RNAV 03/21 and the omnidirectional departures.
- Cruzeiro do Sul (SBCZ): updated drawing of the VOR/DME 10.

### VectorsProcsMap plugin

- Updated SIDs MUPEG1C (SBPV), GERKO1A and TEKIT1A (SBSL) and the SBJI omnidirectional departures.

## SBBS · Brasília FIR {#sbbs}

### Navigation data

- Renewed the fixes of the Belo Horizonte/Confins area (BH, CF, LS and LG series) and other areas: 208 fixes added and 112 obsolete fixes removed.
- Repositioned fixes AKSUG, CF001, CF002, CF003, GILGO, FF36, FS02C and FS20C, with precision adjustments to ANSEM, DIKOT, EGBIG, FF18, UGULO and VULAD.
- Added the Jaboticatubas/Serra do Cipó aerodrome (SNLG), with runway 07/25.
- Barra do Garças (SBBW): runway 07/25 heading updated from 071/251 to 072/252.
- Updated the segments of airways UL795, UM654, UN741, UZ4, UZ6, UZ8, UZ23, UZ33, UZ35, UZ40, UZ72, Z2, Z3 and Z48.

### Procedures

- Confins (SBCF): new SIDs. Added ARSEB1D, BIXEM1A, BUSUP1C, EKUBA1C, ENLUN1A, ISLEX1B, ISRUG1B, KUGUT1C, MUKAN1B, NEBUS1A and OPTIV1B; removed ARPUM1B, DOBTA1A, GEDAD1B, IRAXO2B, ISNUV2A, KUBEX3A, TORUX1B, TORUX2A and VASIX1A.
- Confins (SBCF): restructured STARs. Added APORT1A, BOGUP1A/1B, KUBLU1A/1B, KUGUT2A and KIMUS2B; removed ALONG1A/1B, ENSIG1A/1B, SINIX1A/1B, KUGUT1A and KIMUS1B.
- Confins (SBCF): new approaches ILS 16-X, ILS 16-Z, LOC 16-Z, LOC 34-Z, RNAV 16-X, RNAV 16-Z, RNAV 34-X and VOR/DME 34, replacing ILS 16-K, ILS 16-W, LOC 16-W, RNAV 16 and VOR/DME 34-Y. Updated drawings of the VOR/DME 16, ILS 34-X, ILS 34-Z and RNAV 34-Z.
- Pampulha (SBBH): new SIDs. Added ARMOM1A, ASBEG1A, NICAR1A, OPTUK1A and UTMEB1C (RWY 13) and EROGA1B, IVROD1B, SIBON1B and TRIVI1B (RWY 31); removed EGUSO2A, GEDAR2A, GEPNA3A, ILROT3A, IVSAM3A, KIMUS1A, POPNI1A, TIPLI2A and UTLIK1A.
- Pampulha (SBBH): new STARs ILMEP1A/1B, NELOD1A/1B and UMGIV1A/1B, replacing EQUIV1A/1B, OBLEB1B, OBLEB2A, UMDIR1B, UTBID1A/1B and UTNUL1A.
- Pampulha (SBBH): RNAV 13-A replaced by RNAV 13-Z, and updated LOC 13 (new TEDIP transition) and RNAV 31-A (GERBO and TIDPA transitions).
- Lagoa Santa (SBLS): removed SIDs ISNUV2A, KUBEX3A, TORUX2A and VASIX1A (the omnidirectional departures remain).
- Lagoa Santa (SBLS): RNAV 13 replaced by RNAV 13-A and RNAV 13-Z, NDB/DME 13 with the new ISPUT transition, and RNAV 31 with the new LS018 transition.
- Serra do Cipó (SNLG): added the RNAV 07 and RNAV 25 procedures.
- Viracopos (SBKP): STAR VERME1A replaced by EDRAT2A (RWY 15) and SID EGEVA1A removed.
- Pirassununga (SBYS): updated drawings of all SIDs and of the VOR/DME, VORTAC and RNAV approaches to runways 02C and 20C.

### Sectors

- Redrawn boundaries of the Belo Horizonte (SBBH) and Confins (SBCF) CTRs.
- Ribeirão Preto (SBRP): added the ATZ sector (up to 4,000 ft).

### VectorsProcsMap plugin

- Updated the Pampulha (SBBH) and Confins (SBCF) SIDs and STARs to the new AIRAC, removed the Lagoa Santa (SBLS) SIDs and updated the Barra do Garças (SBBW) omnidirectional departures.
- Added the Guarulhos (SBGR) and Congonhas (SBSP) procedures, and removed the Pirassununga (SBYS) ones, which moved to the SBCW package.

## SBCW · Curitiba FIR {#sbcw}

### Navigation data

- Renewed the fixes of the Belo Horizonte/Confins area (BH and CF series) and other areas: 249 fixes added and 110 obsolete fixes removed.
- Repositioned fixes AKSUG, CF001, CF002, CF003, GILGO, FF27, FF36, FS02C and FS20C, with precision adjustments to other fixes.
- Adjusted the runway thresholds of Barbacena (SBBQ, RWY 18/36) and Londrina (SBLO, RWY 13/31).
- Updated the segments of airways UL795, UM654, UN741, UZ4, UZ6, UZ8, UZ23, UZ33, UZ35, UZ40, UZ72, Z2, Z3 and Z48.

### Procedures

- Confins (SBCF): restructured STARs. Added APORT1A, BOGUP1A/1B, KUBLU1A/1B, KUGUT2A and KIMUS2B; removed ALONG1A/1B, ENSIG1A/1B, SINIX1A/1B, KUGUT1A and KIMUS1B. The transitions to the approaches were updated.
- Viracopos (SBKP): STAR VERME1A replaced by EDRAT2A (RWY 15) and SID EGEVA1A removed.
- Santos-Dumont (SBRJ): STARs MAKTI2A and SINAG1A updated, with SINAG now starting at ILPED. Updated drawings of SID PUMSO4B (RWY 02R) and of the RNAV 02R-T and 20L-U.
- Congonhas (SBSP): VOR/DME 35L and 35R procedures updated, now ending at SP200.
- Barbacena (SBBQ): updated drawings of SIDs OPRIS, TOKIM and UBKEB and of the NDB and RNAV 18/36 approaches.
- Corumbá (SBCR): updated drawings of SIDs FATAL, NEGRO and ISMOT and of the NDB 27 and RNAV 09/27 approaches.
- Foz do Iguaçu (SBFI): updated the initial segment of all SIDs (RWY 15 and 33).
- Jaguaruna (SBJA): updated drawings of SID IMBIX2A and of the RNAV 05/23.
- Updated the omnidirectional departures of SBBQ, SBCR, SBLO and SBPG and the drawings of the Londrina (SBLO) approaches.

### Sectors

- Jacarepaguá (SBJR): the sector changed from an ATZ (up to 1,500 ft) to a CTR (up to 2,000 ft).
- The Rio CTR sector (SBRJ) raised from 1,500 ft to 2,000 ft.
- Added the Cabo Frio FIZ sector (SBCB), split from the SBES sector.
- Redrawn boundaries of the Galeão (SBGL), Afonsos (SBAF), Jacarepaguá (SBJR) and Rio (SBRJ) CTRs, and added the Cabo Frio FIZ (SBCB) boundary.
- Added the Uruguayan aerodromes SUPC and SUNH to the Montevideo sectors, and the boundary lines between Paraguay (SGFA) and Bolivia (SLLF).
- Added the visibility center of the SUEO_FSS position (Montevideo Oceanico).

### Ground layouts

- Added the ground layouts of Chapecó (SBCH) and Zona da Mata (SBZM), with apron and stand names.
- Updated the Santos-Dumont (SBRJ) ground layout.

### ASR

- The radio station (R_TWR) ASRs of SBCH and SBZM now open centered on the aerodrome, with the ground layout and a rotated display.

### VectorsProcsMap plugin

- Added the Campo de Marte (SBMT) and Pirassununga (SBYS) procedures, and updated the SBBQ, SBCR, SBFI, SBJA, SBJD, SBKP, SBPG, SBRJ and SBSP procedures.

## SBRE · Recife FIR {#sbre}

### Navigation data

- Renewed the fixes of the Belo Horizonte/Confins area (BH and CF series) and other areas: 203 fixes added and 104 obsolete fixes removed.
- Repositioned fixes AKSUG, CF001, CF002, CF003 and FF36, with precision adjustments to ANSEM, DIKOT, EGBIG, FF18, UGULO and VULAD.
- Added the São Raimundo Nonato aerodrome (SWKQ, Serra da Capivara).
- Fortaleza (SBFZ): fix FZ048 replaced by FZ061.
- Airway UZ33 rerouted: it now goes through GATUR and SURTI instead of EDMUR, KORMU and OPGOL.
- Updated the segments of airways UL795, UM654, UN741, UZ4, UZ6, UZ8, UZ23, UZ35, UZ40, UZ72, Z2, Z3 and Z48, following the repositioned fixes.

### Procedures

- Fortaleza (SBFZ): SID UTMAR1A replaced by UTMAR2A (RWY 13), now starting at FZ061.
- Fortaleza (SBFZ): updated drawings of SIDs ERUGU1A, ESOPU1A, TOGAB1A and UGEPO1A (RWY 13).
- Mossoró (SBMS): updated drawings of the omnidirectional departure (RWY 05 and 23) and of the VOR/DME 23 and RNAV 23 arrivals.
- Updated drawings of the RNAV 20-Y at Una (SBTC) and the RNAV 20-C at Vitória (SBVT).

### Sectors

- Brasília Center NS sector (SBBS_NS_CTR): identifier changed from BSW to BSNS, including in sector ownership.

### VectorsProcsMap plugin

- SID UTMAR1A replaced by UTMAR2A at Fortaleza (SBFZ), updated SIDs ERUGU1A, ESOPU1A, TOGAB1A and UGEPO1A (RWY 13) and the Mossoró (SBMS) omnidirectional departures.
