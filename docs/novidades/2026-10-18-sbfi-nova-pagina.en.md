# New SBFI page

The Foz do Iguaçu page was rewritten from the current AIP AD 2 SBFI. It now summarizes the use of runway 15/33, which has no parallel taxiway, and explains the departure backtrack, the DCL run by Tower and the ground flow with diagrams for runways 15 and 33. It also covers runway exits, turnaround areas, the traffic patterns of the Foz TMA aerodromes and a table of aprons and airlines.
