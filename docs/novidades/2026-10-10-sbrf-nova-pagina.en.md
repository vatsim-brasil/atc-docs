# New SBRF page

The Recife page was rewritten from the current AIP AD 2 SBRF. It now explains the runway 18 preferential system, adds DCL with the `SBRF` code, the Ground and Tower areas of responsibility and the ground flow with diagrams for runways 18 and 36. It also covers the intersection takeoff points with their TORA, runway exits, taxi restrictions, HIRO and a table of aprons and airlines.
