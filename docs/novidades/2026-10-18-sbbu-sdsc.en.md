# New SBBU and SDSC pages

FIR Brasília now has pages for Bauru (SBBU) and São Carlos (SDSC), the two uncontrolled aerodromes from the SBBS package that were still missing. Each page has the general data, the transition altitude from the current charts, the runways, the package positions and the ROTAER rules that apply on VATSIM.
