# New SBRJ page

The Santos-Dumont page was rewritten from the current AIP AD 2 SBRJ. It now explains main runway 02R/20L and auxiliary runway 02L/20R, so close that every taxi to the main runway crosses the auxiliary one, and adds the DCL, the areas of responsibility and the ground flow with diagrams for the 02 and 20 configurations. It also covers runway exits, taxi restrictions, takeoff points and a table of aprons and airlines.
