# New SBCY page

The Cuiabá page was rewritten from the current AIP AD 2 SBCY. It now summarizes the use of runway 17/35, explains the DCL run by Ground, adds the Ground and Tower areas of responsibility and the ground flow with diagrams for runways 17 and 35, including the backtrack via A to depart from 17, which has no taxiway to the threshold. It also covers runway exits, the IMC restrictions, takeoff points and a table of aprons and airlines.
