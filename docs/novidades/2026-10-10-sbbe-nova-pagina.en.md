# New SBBE page

The Belém - Val de Cans page was rewritten from the current AIP AD 2 SBBE. It now summarizes the preferential system for runways 07/25 and 03/21, explains the DCL run by Ground, adds the Ground and Tower areas of responsibility and the ground flow with diagrams for the 07/03 and 25/21 configurations. It also covers the takeoff points, runway exits and turn pads, taxi restrictions, HIRO and a table of aprons and airlines. The transition altitude was corrected to 5000 ft.
