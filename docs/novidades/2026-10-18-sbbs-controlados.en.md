# New controlled aerodromes in FIR SBBS

FIR Brasília now has pages for the five controlled aerodromes from the SBBS package that were still missing: Ribeirão Preto (SBRP), Anápolis (SBAN), Uberlândia (SBUL), Uberaba (SBUR) and Pirassununga (SBYS). Each page has the general data, the transition altitude from the current charts, the runways, the ATC positions and the ROTAER rules that apply on VATSIM.
