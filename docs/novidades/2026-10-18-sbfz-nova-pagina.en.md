# New SBFZ page

The Fortaleza page was rewritten from the current AIP AD 2 SBFZ. It now summarizes the use of runway 13/31, explains the DCL run by Ground, adds the Ground and Tower areas of responsibility and the ground flow with diagrams for runways 13 and 31, including the runway crossing via D and G to threshold 31. It also covers runway exits, the hotspot next to threshold 13, takeoff points, HIRO and a table of aprons and airlines.
