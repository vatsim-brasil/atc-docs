# New SBBR page

The Brasília page was rewritten from the current AIP AD 2 SBBR. It now summarizes the preferential system and the sector split of runways 11L/29R and 11R/29L, the departure peak rules, the monitor handoffs without initial call, the DCL and the split Tower positions. It adds the areas of responsibility and the ground flow with diagrams for the 11 and 29 configurations, runway exits, hotspots, taxi restrictions, takeoff points, HIRO and a table of aprons and airlines.
