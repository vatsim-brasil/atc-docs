# New SBCT page

The Curitiba page was rewritten from the current AIP AD 2 SBCT. It now explains the 11/15 and 29/33 preferential system by aircraft type and adds DCL with the `SBCT` code, the Ground and Tower areas of responsibility and the ground flow with diagrams. It also covers the takeoff points with their TORA, the HIRO runway exits, taxi restrictions with the dedicated HEAVY flow and a table of aprons and airlines.
