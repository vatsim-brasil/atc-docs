# New SBNF page

The Navegantes page was rewritten from the current AIP AD 2 SBNF. It now explains operations without a parallel taxiway, entering via A or B and backtracking to the turn pads, and shows the ground flow with diagrams for runways 08 and 26. It also covers runway exits, the taxi and pushback restrictions in IMC, the northwest traffic pattern with its entry gates and the table of aprons and airlines.
