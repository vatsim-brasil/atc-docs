# New SBGO page

The Goiânia page was rewritten from the current AIP AD 2 SBGO. It now summarizes the use of runway 14/32 and the 10000 ft transition altitude from the IAC charts, adds the Ground and Tower areas of responsibility and the ground flow with diagrams for runways 14 and 32, via the A and J parallels. It also covers hot spot HS1 on TWY J, runway exits, taxi restrictions and the terminal and general aviation aprons.
