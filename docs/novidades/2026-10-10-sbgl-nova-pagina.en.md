# New SBGL page

The Galeão page was rewritten from the current AIP AD 2 SBGL. It now summarizes the runway scenarios (segregated, single runway and converging), adds DCL with the `SBGL` code, the Apron, Ground and Tower areas of responsibility and the ground flow with diagrams, including the routes for wingspans above 36 m. It also covers the takeoff points, runway exits, hotspots, 28/33 converging operations and a table of aprons and airlines.
