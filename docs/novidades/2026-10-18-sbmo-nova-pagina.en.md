# New SBMO page

The Maceió page was rewritten from the current AIP AD 2 SBMO. It now summarizes the use of runway 12/30 and the displaced threshold 30, explains the DCL run by Tower (the only position in the package) and adds the ground flow with diagrams for runways 12 and 30. It also covers runway exits, takeoff points and a table of aprons and airlines. The transition altitude is now 10000 ft, as in the AIP and the IAC charts.
