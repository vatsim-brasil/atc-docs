Official sources actually used in writing this manual. Every normative statement refers to one of these documents.

!!! warning "Precedence"
    1. The **current official aeronautical publication** prevails over this manual.
    2. The **VATSIM Brasil sector package** prevails regarding callsigns, frequencies and logons.
    3. The **current VATSIM policy** prevails regarding authorization, rating and endorsement.

    Support material for flight simulation. It does not replace any of the three sources above and must not be used for real-world air navigation.
