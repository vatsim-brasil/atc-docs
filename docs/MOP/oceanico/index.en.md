???+ warning "Attention!"
    All Atlântico FIR positions (`SBAO_FSS` and its split sectors) are designated **Tier 2** under the VATSIM GCAP and are grouped under a single VATSIM Brasil endorsement, identified as **Oceanic Positions**.

    Holding the required rating is not enough. To staff any of these positions you must, cumulatively, be on the division's ATC Roster, hold at least the **C1** rating and have received this specific endorsement.

    Contact the VATSIM Brasil Training Center to learn about the process for obtaining it.

## The manual

The Atlântico FIR is the only oceanic FIR in VATSIM Brasil. It is served by the **Atlântico ACC**, callsign **CENTRO ATLÂNTICO** or **ATLANTICO CENTER**, and covers the South Atlantic out to the 010° W meridian.

What changes here is not the size, it is the method: **there is no radar surveillance and no direct VHF**. The controller does not see the aircraft; they reconstruct it from what it reports. The whole operation follows from that.

This manual is the working reference for preparing, opening and operating the `SBAO_FSS` position. It is organized in the order in which things happen during a session.
