## :material-airplane: VFR circulation

!!! abstract "Visual navigation documents"
    - **Circular:** [AIC N 13/20 – Rotas Especiais de Aeronaves em Voo Visual na CTR de Ribeirão Preto](https://publicacoes.decea.mil.br/publicacao/aic-n-1320){ target="_blank" } (effective 23 APR 2020) · [:material-file-pdf-box: PDF](../../../files/aic/AIC-N-13-20.pdf){ target="_blank" }
    - **Visual chart – Aircraft routes (REA):** [REA CTR Ribeirão Preto](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/cartas/visuais/rea/REA_Ribeirao_Preto_aic_13_20.pdf){ target="_blank" }

    The documents are embedded in the **Visual Chart** and **VFR Circular (AIC)** tabs under **Useful Information**, with options to open them in a new tab or download them.

- A flight plan to the APP is mandatory for VFR flights to or across the TMA; AFIL flight plans are not accepted.
- SBRP: mandatory circuit entry points at Morro Piripau (east) and USP (west).
- SBAQ: arrivals and departures via the USINA NORTE and USINA SUL gates.
- SBYS: gliders from GND to FL100 on Saturdays, Sundays and holidays.

## :material-note-text-outline: Remarks

- The TMA is class A between FL145 and FL195 and class C between 5500 FT and FL145. The APP is split into eight sectors, and the Gavião Peixoto FIZ volume is excluded.
- In the Ribeirão CTR, ATS surveillance is provided by Academia APP.
- SBAQ (Araraquara) and SBGP (Gavião Peixoto) lie in class G FIZs, served by the local radio stations.
- SBYS is the Air Force Academy: civil aircraft may only operate with authorization from the Academy command. SBGP requires prior authorization from the aerodrome administrator.
