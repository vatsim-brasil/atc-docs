## :material-airplane: VFR circulation

- Flight plans filed by radiotelephony are not accepted.
- SBME: minimum circuit altitude of 600 FT for helicopters and 1500 FT for aeroplanes.
- AFIL flight plans are not accepted in the Aldeia ATZ and CTR.
- Helicopter routes chart: [CCV REH WJ1-Cabo Frio](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/cartas/visuais/reh/ccv-reh-wj1-cabo-frio_reh_20260319.pdf){ target="_blank" }
- There is no REA chart published for this TMA.

## :material-note-text-outline: Remarks

- Macaé concentrates offshore helicopter operations in the Campos Basin: several platform heliports are designated "Macaé" in the AIP (GEN 2.4).
- In the real world, the SBME tower operates from 0915 to 2100 UTC; outside those hours AFIS is provided on 119.200. Runways 05/23 and 06/24 are not used simultaneously, and jets do not use 06/24.
- The Aldeia 1 and 2 CTRs belong to São Pedro da Aldeia APP (`SBES_APP`, Aldeia Control). SBES is a Navy base; civil aircraft require authorization from the Naval Air Force Command.
- SBCP (Campos) and SBFS (São Tomé) lie in class G FIZs, served by the local radio stations.
