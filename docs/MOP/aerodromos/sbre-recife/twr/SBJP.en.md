## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Departures

- ACFT with a maximum takeoff weight above 20t, when occupying a parking stand on aprons 1 and 2, shall only start engines after the pushback operation has been completed.

### Aerodrome Regulations

- APP SVC in the CTR provided by Pessoa TWR.
- The AD may be used regularly by any ACFT compatible with RCD 4C or lower.
- The following classes and types of ACFT are restricted:
    - ACFT WO RDO EQPT;
    - GLD;
    - ACFT WO transponder or with a failure of this EQPT;
    - Powered ultralight FLT.
- The following air services are restricted:
    - Dropping of objects or spraying;
    - ACFT towing;
    - Parachute jumping;
    - Aerobatic FLT.

### Aprons and Taxiways

- OPS of ACFT with MTOW ABV 20t are subject to pushback when leaving the PRKG.
