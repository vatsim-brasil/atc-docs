## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Departures

- Pilots shall plan the TKOF so as to arrive at the holding point ready to perform it. If an immediate TKOF is not possible, inform the ATC unit in advance.
- Upon receiving TKOF clearance, the pilot is expected to begin the takeoff roll immediately (the expected reaction time is up to 10 seconds).
- TWR will not report the TKOF time to ACFT. The instruction regarding the FREQ of the next unit to be contacted AFT TKOF and, if necessary, any additional instructions will be issued together with the TKOF AUTH.
- Make the initial call to APP immediately after takeoff in order to obtain instructions to leave the departure runway centerline;
- The initial call to APP after takeoff shall be immediate and follow only this format: *FORTALEZA CONTROL [CALLSIGN]*. Do not include any additional information beyond this format.
- Turboprop or piston aircraft: expect vectors or a clearance direct to a published waypoint right after takeoff.
- Unless instructed otherwise, pilots are encouraged to take off from the following intersections in order to ensure the MROT:
    - *RWY 13:*
        * All aircraft
            * TWY `B`
            * Distance from Taxiway `B` to threshold 31: Available TORA: 2613 m
    - *RWY 31:*
        * All aircraft
            * TWY `H`
            * Distance from Taxiway `H` to threshold 13: Available TORA: 2687 m

### Approaches and Landings

- During LDG OPS, pilots will not report the landing gear COND to TWR, EXC in EMERG situations relating to its extension and/or locking.
- Pilots must vacate the runway at the fastest speed permitted by standard operating procedures and consistent with operational safety, allowing ATC to apply minimum separation on final approach.
    - *RWY 13:*
        * All aircraft
            * TWY `D`
            * Distance from Taxiway `D` to threshold 13: Available LDA: 2242 m

### Aerodrome Regulations

- Line-up must be immediate once cleared.
- The AD may be used regularly by any ACFT compatible with RCD 4E or lower.
- OBS VAC for joining or leaving the TFC circuit.
- The following classes and types of ACFT are restricted:
    - ACFT WO RDO EQPT;
    - GLD;
    - ACFT WO transponder or with a failure of this EQPT;
    - Powered ultralight FLT.
- The following air services are restricted:
    - Dropping of objects or spraying;
    - ACFT towing;
    - Parachute jumping;
    - Aerobatic FLT.

### Aprons and Taxiways

- Departure of jet and/or turboprop ACFT with a wingspan greater than 24M (exclusive) from aprons 2 and 3 only by pushback.
- Use of TWY `E` by ACFT RCD `C` TIL `E` leaving the general aviation terminal to enter or cross the RWY is prohibited.
- TWY `J` BTN THR 13 and TWY `E`: OPS of ACFT with a wingspan of 36M or more (inclusive) PRB.
