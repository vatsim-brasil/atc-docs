## :material-routes: Runways

### Runway system

SBMO has **one runway**, 12/30 (2602 × 45 m, asphalt). **E** runs along the south side of the runway from end to end, from **A** (threshold 12) to **D** (threshold 30). The aprons are to the south: apron 2 (TPS 2) to the west, next to threshold 12, and apron 1 (TPS 1) in the middle of the field.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-airplane:{ .lg .middle } **Runway `12`**

    ---

    ILS CAT I. Takeoff from the threshold, via A.

-   :material-airplane:{ .lg .middle } **Runway `30`**

    ---

    RNP and VOR. Threshold displaced 112 m. Takeoff from the start of the runway, via D.

</div>

!!! info "Runway selection"
    The AIP sets no preferential runway: the runway in use follows the wind.

### Runway data

| Runway | Dimensions | TORA | LDA | Approach | Remarks |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **12** | 2602 × 45 m | 2490 m | 2490 m | ILS CAT I | The last 112 m (before THR 30) are not part of the TORA[^ad13] |
| **30** | 2602 × 45 m | 2602 m | 2490 m | Non-precision | RNP and VOR. Threshold displaced 112 m[^ad13] |

- The AD accepts aircraft up to code **4E**[^ad20-1].
- 180° turns for aircraft the size of a **FOKKER 100, B737 or larger** only at the thresholds[^ad20-6].

## :material-headset: ATC Units

| Code | Abbr. | Callsign | Frequency | Remarks |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **SBMO_TWR** | `TMO` | Maceió Tower | **118.250** | `DCL` (code `SBMO`) |

- The VATSIM package only has **Tower**: there is no Ground, Clearance Delivery or voice ATIS. The AIP lists TWR 118.000 and 118.250 and D-ATIS only[^ad18].
- Above Tower, top-down: **SBXM_APP** (Maceió Control, 119.250), which controls the Maceió CTR and TMA.

### DCL

SBMO has **DCL** (departure clearance via datalink)[^ad18]. On the network, the station code is **`SBMO`**. There is no Clearance Delivery (DEL) position: **Tower** issues the clearances.

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Station code** | `SBMO` |
| **Who operates it** | SBMO_TWR. Without TWR, the position covering clearance top-down (APP or ACC) |
| **Controller tool** | TopSky DCL window, with your personal Hoppie ACARS code |
| **Pilot tool** | Aircraft ACARS or a Hoppie-compatible client, sending the clearance request to `SBMO` |

**How it works:**

1. The pilot sends the request (RCD) to `SBMO`, with the parking stand and the ATIS letter, if any.
2. The controller checks the flight plan and replies with the clearance: limit, runway, SID, transponder code and next frequency.
3. The pilot accepts (`WILCO`/`ACCEPT`). The clearance is then issued and **there is no voice readback**.
4. With the clearance accepted, the pilot calls Tower for start-up or pushback.

!!! tip "Controller setup"
    - Log in to the TopSky DCL with the code `SBMO` before opening the position.
    - Announce it in the controller information: `DCL AVBL LOGON SBMO`.
    - A faulty request (invalid plan, wrong SID) goes back as **REVERT TO VOICE**: the pilot calls Tower on frequency.
    - If the pilot does not accept within a reasonable time, consider the clearance not delivered and call them by voice.

[^ad18]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBMO 2.18](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip) and ROTAER.

## :material-map-marker-radius: Areas of responsibility

Since there is only a Tower position, **SBMO_TWR** controls everything: aprons, taxiways and runway 12/30. Neither the AIP nor the package has a Ground or Apron position.

- **Departure:** Tower issues the clearance (DCL or voice), approves pushback and taxi, and clears the takeoff on the same frequency.
- **Arrival:** Tower clears the landing and, once the aircraft has vacated, the taxi to the apron.
- **Pushback** is mandatory for aircraft above **40 t** leaving the stand[^ad20-2].

[^ad20-2]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBMO 2.20, item 2](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).

## :material-transit-connection-variant: Ground flow

!!! abstract "General rule"
    - **E** runs south of the runway, from **A** (threshold 12) to **D** (threshold 30).
    - **G** (west) and **F** (east) link E to apron 1; apron 2 faces E, between A and B.
    - **B** and **C** are rapid exits: **B** for runway 30 landings and **C** for runway 12 landings.
    - **K** leads to the engine run-up area, north of the runway.

=== "Runway 12"

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![Ground flow for runway 12: departures via E to A; landings vacating via C; aircraft rolling past C continue to D and backtrack via E](img/sbmo-fluxo-12.svg){ loading=lazy }
      <figcaption>Green: departures. Blue: landings. Orange dashed: landing rolling past C.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    - **Departures (12):** **E** west to **A** (threshold 12). From apron 1, leave via **G**.
    - **Landings (12):** vacate via **C** (rapid exit, ~1390 m) and continue via **E** and **F** to apron 1. Aircraft rolling past C go to the end, vacate via **D** and return via **E**.

    Example: `AZU4321, taxi to holding point runway 12 via G, E and A.`

=== "Runway 30"

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![Ground flow for runway 30: departures via E to D; landings vacating via B; aircraft rolling past B go to the end and vacate via A](img/sbmo-fluxo-30.svg){ loading=lazy }
      <figcaption>Green: departures. Blue: landings. Orange dashed: landing rolling past B.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    - **Departures (30):** **E** east to **D**. The takeoff starts at the beginning of the runway, before the displaced threshold (TORA 2602 m). From apron 1, leave via **F**.
    - **Landings (30):** vacate via **B** (rapid exit, ~1460 m) to apron 2, or continue via **E** and **G** to apron 1. Aircraft rolling past B go to the end and vacate via **A**.

    Example: `GLO1234, taxi to holding point runway 30 via F, E and D.`

### Runway exits

| Runway | Exits |
| :---: | :--- |
| **12** | **C** (~1390 m, rapid exit) or **D** (end) |
| **30** | **C** (~850 m, light aircraft), **B** (~1460 m, rapid exit) or **A** (end) |

<small>Approximate distance from the threshold to the exit, measured on the diagram. The SBMO AIP does not publish exits.</small>

## :material-clipboard-text-outline: Other procedures

- Observe the VAC for joining and leaving the traffic pattern[^ad22].
- On a visual approach, do not confuse the field with runway 14/32 of the **Alagoas Aero Club**, 7 NM to the southeast[^ad22].
- Not allowed at SBMO: aircraft without radio, without a transponder (or with it failed), gliders and powered ultralights. Parachute drops, aircraft towing and aerobatic flights are also prohibited[^ad20-1].
