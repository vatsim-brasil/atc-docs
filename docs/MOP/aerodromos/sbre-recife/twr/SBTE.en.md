## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Aerodrome Regulations

- The AD may be used regularly by any ACFT compatible with RCD 4C or lower.
- The HEL TFC circuit shall be flown only in the W SECT of the AD.

### Aprons and Taxiways

- ACFT with a wingspan greater than 24M entering or leaving stand `01` of the PRKG apron must use TWY `A`.
- ACFT leaving PSN 02 and 03 of the PRKG apron must use TWY `A`.
- Simultaneous operation of aircraft classified with reference code number 3 or 4 on runway 02/20 with any aircraft on taxiway `C` and on the aircraft parking apron is restricted when operations take place in Instrument Meteorological Conditions (IMC).
- RWY 02/20: simultaneous OPS of ACFT with a wingspan equal to or greater than 24M with OPS on TWY `C` or its extension onto the ACFT apron PRB when AD weather conditions allow only IFR FLT.

### Runways

- RWY 02/20: 180DEG turn AUTH for ACFT with a wingspan ABV 15M only at the THR.
- ACFT on APCH to RWY 02 or RWY 20: do not confuse with the APCH to RWY 02 or 20 of AD SNDR - TIMON/Domingos Rego, MA.
