## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Departures

- Pilots shall plan the TKOF so as to arrive at the holding point ready to perform it. If an immediate TKOF is not possible, inform the ATC unit in advance.
- Upon receiving TKOF clearance, the pilot is expected to begin the takeoff roll immediately (the expected reaction time is up to 10 seconds).
- Recife TWR will not report the TKOF time to ACFT. The instruction regarding the FREQ of the next unit to be contacted AFT TKOF and, if necessary, any additional instructions will be issued together with the TKOF AUTH.
- The initial call to APP after takeoff shall be immediate and follow only this format: *RECIFE CONTROL [CALLSIGN]*. Do not include any additional information beyond this format.
- Unless instructed otherwise, pilots are encouraged to take off from the following intersections in order to ensure the MROT:
    - *RWY 18:*
        * Turboprop aircraft
            * Taxiway `B`
            * Distance from Taxiway `B` to threshold 36: Available TORA: 1728 m
        * General Aviation
            * Taxiway `G` or `J`
            * Distance from Taxiway `G` to threshold 36: Available TORA: 1832 m
            * Distance from Taxiway `J` to threshold 36: Available TORA: 2300 m

### Approaches and Landings

- During LDG OPS, pilots will not report the landing gear COND to Recife TWR, EXC in EMERG situations relating to its extension and/or locking.
- Pilots must vacate the runway at the fastest speed permitted by standard operating procedures and consistent with operational safety, allowing ATC to apply minimum separation on final approach.
- Due to the lack of an engine run-up area, the section of TWY `M` between the RWY 18 HLDG point and TWY `B` will be used in exceptional cases.

### Aerodrome Regulations

- The airport may be used regularly by any aircraft compatible with reference code 4E or lower.
- The AD may be used regularly by any ACFT compatible with RCD 4E or lower.
- OBS VAC for joining or leaving the TFC circuit.
- The following classes and types of ACFT are restricted:
    - ACFT WO RDO EQPT;
    - GLD;
    - ACFT WO transponder or with a failure of this EQPT;
    - Powered ultralight FLT.
- The following air services are restricted:
    - Dropping of objects or spraying;
    - ACFT towing;
    - Parachute jumping;
    - Aerobatic FLT.

### Aprons and Taxiways

- OPR of ACFT weighing more than 40t is subject to a pushback operation when leaving the PRKG.
- TWY `N`: TFC OPR of ACFT with a wingspan of up to 24m (exclusive).
- TWY `G` and `K`: TFC OPR of ACFT with a wingspan of up to 36m.
- TWY `P`: TFC OPR of ACFT with a wingspan of up to 15m (exclusive).
- In Instrument Meteorological Conditions (IMC), simultaneous operations of ACFT with a wingspan greater than or equal to 36m on RWY 18/36 and on TWY `M`, `D` and `K` are prohibited.

### Runways

- 180DEG turns by ACFT the size of a FOKKER 100, BOEING 737 or larger only at the THR.
