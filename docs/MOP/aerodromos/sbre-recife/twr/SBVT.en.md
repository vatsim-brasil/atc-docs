## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Aerodrome Regulations

- The AD may be used regularly by any ACFT compatible with RCD 4D or lower.
- OBS VAC for entering and leaving the ATZ.
- The following classes and types of ACFT are restricted:
    - ACFT WO RDO EQPT;
    - GLD;
    - ACFT WO transponder or with a failure of this EQPT;
    - Powered ultralight FLT.
- The following air services are restricted:
    - Dropping of objects or spraying;
    - ACFT towing;
    - Parachute jumping;
    - Aerobatic FLT.

### Aprons and Taxiways

- Starting engines before pushback is prohibited until the ACFT reaches the taxi lane.
- TWY `A` and `B`: taxiing of CAT D ACT PRB.
- Aprons `2` and `3` BTN PSN 2 and 13: taxiing of CAT D ACFT PRB.

### Runways

- 180DEG turns in the middle of runway 06/24 are prohibited for ACFT above 12t.
- Backtrack on RWY 02 permitted only in the THR 20 turning area.
