## :material-routes: Runways

### Runway system

SBVT has **two runways that do not cross**: 02/20 (2058 m), to the east, next to the passenger terminal (apron 1), and 06/24 (1750 m), to the west, next to the old terminal and the cargo terminal (aprons 2 and 3). They come close to each other in the north, near thresholds 20 and 24. The AIP **defines no preferential runway**.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-airplane:{ .lg .middle } **Main: `02/20`**

    ---

    2058 × 45 m. RNP and VOR approaches. Serves apron 1 via TWY **F**.

-   :material-airplane:{ .lg .middle } **Secondary: `06/24`**

    ---

    1750 × 45 m. Has the aerodrome's **only ILS**, on 24. Serves aprons 2 and 3 via TWY **A**.

</div>

### Runway data

| Runway | Dimensions | TORA | LDA | Approach | Remarks |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **02** | 2058 × 45 m | 2058 m | 2058 m | RNP and VOR | |
| **20** | 2058 × 45 m | 2058 m | 2058 m | RNP | Turnaround area at threshold 20 |
| **06** | 1750 × 45 m | 1750 m | 1750 m | RNP | |
| **24** | 1750 × 45 m | 1750 m | 1750 m | ILS CAT I | Turnaround area at threshold 24 |

- **Backtrack** on 02 only up to the **threshold 20 turnaround area**[^ad20-6].
- On 06/24, 180° turns by aircraft above **12 t** only at the **threshold 24 turnaround area**, due to pavement disintegration[^ad20-6].
- Touch-and-go on training flights is **prohibited on 02/20**[^ad20-7].

[^ad20-7]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBVT 2.20, item 7](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).

## :material-map-marker-radius: Areas of responsibility

<figure markdown="span">
  ![Map of SBVT with the runway strips in yellow, under Tower, and the rest in blue, under Ground](img/sbvt-responsabilidade.svg){ loading=lazy }
  <figcaption>Yellow strips: Tower. Rest: Ground. The dashed line runs through the holding points. White circles: apron numbers.</figcaption>

| Position | Responsible for |
| :--- | :--- |
| **GND** | Aprons 1 to 4, TWY **A**, **F**, **N**, **D**, **E** and the links up to the **holding point** |
| **TWR** | Both runways and the taxiways inside the holding points |

- SBVT has **no Apron** position: Ground handles all aprons.
- **Departures:** Ground hands off to Tower **before the holding point**.
- **Arrivals:** once the aircraft vacates the runway, Tower hands off to Ground.
- Aircraft crossing 06/24 via **C/D**, or passing threshold 24 on the way to 20, need a Tower clearance.

## :material-transit-connection-variant: Ground flow

!!! abstract "General rule"
    - **TWY F** runs along the west side of 02/20, from apron 1 to threshold 24. **N** is the apron 1 taxiway; **G** and **H** lead to threshold 02.
    - **J**, **K**, **L** and **M** link F to 02/20. **M** reaches threshold 20 passing next to threshold 24.
    - **A** runs along the west side of 06/24 and links aprons 2 and 3 to the thresholds; **B** and **C** link A to the runway.
    - **D** crosses 06/24 and links the west side to F; **E** links D to F and K.

=== "Runway 02"

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![Ground flow on runway 02: departures via N, F and G to threshold 02; landings vacating via K and following F to apron 1; turboprops vacating via J](img/sbvt-fluxo-02.svg){ loading=lazy }
      <figcaption>Green: departures. Blue: landings. Dashed orange: short exit for turboprops.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    - **Departures (02):** **N**, **F** and **G** (or **H**) to threshold 02.
    - **Landings (02):** vacate via **K** and follow **F** to apron 1. Turboprops may vacate via **J**.
    - **Avoid M:** vacating via M after landing on 02 interferes with runway 06 operations (HS 7)[^adc].

    Example: `GLO1234, taxi to holding point runway 02 via N, F, G.`

=== "Runway 20"

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![Ground flow on runway 20: departures via F, passing threshold 24, and M to threshold 20; landings vacating via J and following F](img/sbvt-fluxo-20.svg){ loading=lazy }
      <figcaption>Green: departures. Blue: landings.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    - **Departures (20):** **F** northbound, passing threshold 24 with Tower clearance, and **M** to threshold 20.
    - **Landings (20):** vacate via **J** and follow **F** southbound to apron 1.

    Example: `AZU4321, taxi to holding point runway 20 via F, M, cross runway 06/24 at threshold 24.`

### Runway exits

| Runway | Exits |
| :---: | :--- |
| **02** | **J** (~740 m, turboprops), **K** (~1500 m) or **L** (~1550 m). Avoid **M** |
| **20** | **J** (~1310 m) or **G**/**H** at the end of the runway |
| **06** | **B** (~550 m), **C** or **D** (~890 m) or **F** at the end of the runway |
| **24** | **C** or **D** (~860 m) or **B** (~1200 m) |

<small>Approximate distance from the threshold to the exit, measured on the diagram.</small>

### Taxi restrictions

| Restriction | Where |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Code D prohibited** | TWY **A** and **B** and aprons 2 and 3 between stands 2 and 13[^ad20-6] |
| **Code E prohibited** | TWY **H**[^ad28] |
| **In IMC** | Code 4D prohibited on TWY **A** and on the apron 3 taxiway while a code 3 or 4 aircraft lands or departs on 06/24[^ad20-6] |
| **Pushback** | Engines only after pushback, once the aircraft passes the red line[^ad20-6]. Entry to the stand only with a marshaller[^ad20-1] |
| **At night** | Apron 4 and TWY **PP** closed[^ad28] |

!!! danger "Hotspots"
    - **HS 1** and **HS 2:** holding points of **G** and **H** (02/20). The Tower's view of the holding point is restricted: an aircraft may overrun it unnoticed.
    - **HS 3:** **D × F × J** intersection, access to apron 1. Converging flows and restricted visibility between aircraft on D and F.
    - **HS 4:** **D** in front of the fire station, with frequent crossings.
    - **HS 5:** **C × D × 06/24** intersection. Complex geometry and frequent runway crossings.
    - **HS 6:** **E × F × K** intersection. Several taxi flows converge.
    - **HS 7:** **M**, exit from 02. Landings on 02 vacating via M interfere with 06; the pavement also has restrictions.

[^ad28]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBVT 2.8](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).
[^adc]: SBVT ADC chart (AMDT 2610A1), hotspots HS 1 to HS 7.

## :material-clipboard-text-outline: Other procedures

- **Restricted** aircraft: no radio, gliders and no transponder (or transponder failure)[^ad20-1].
- **Restricted** air services: object dropping or spraying, aerial towing, parachute dropping and aerobatic flight[^ad20-1].
- Training flights near the aerodrome[^ad20-7].
- **Vulture** concentration in all sectors[^ad23].
- Follow the VAC to join and leave the traffic pattern[^ad22].
- Helicopters to offshore platforms: VFR preferably at **2500 ft** outbound and **1500 ft** inbound (3500 ft from the Macaé TMA); IFR at **5000 ft** outbound and **6000 ft** inbound[^ad22].
