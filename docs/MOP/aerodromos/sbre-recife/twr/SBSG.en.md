## :material-routes: Runways

### Runway system

SBSG has **one runway**, 12/30 (3000 × 60 m, asphalt). Taxiway **B** runs along the north side of the runway from end to end and reaches the thresholds through **B1** (12) and **B4** (30). The rapid exits **DD** and **CC** link the runway to B.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-airplane:{ .lg .middle } **Runway `12`**

    ---

    ILS CAT I, RNP, NAV APV and VOR. The only one with a precision approach. DD and CC are rapid exits for aircraft landing on 12.

-   :material-airplane:{ .lg .middle } **Runway `30`**

    ---

    Non-precision approaches only (RNP, NAV APV and VOR).

</div>

!!! info "Runway selection"
    The AIP sets no preferential runway and no tailwind limit: the runway in use follows the wind. In calm wind, **12** has the advantage of the ILS and the rapid exits.

### Runway data

| Runway | Dimensions | TORA | LDA | Approach | Remarks |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **12** | 3000 × 60 m | 3000 m | 3000 m | ILS CAT I | ALS and PAPI |
| **30** | 3000 × 60 m | 3000 m | 3000 m | Non-precision | PAPI |

- The AD accepts aircraft up to code **4E**[^ad20-1].

[^ad20-1]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBSG 2.20, item 1](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip). Declared distances: AD 2.13.

## :material-map-marker-radius: Areas of responsibility

<figure markdown="span">
  ![Map of SBSG with the runway strip in yellow, under Tower, and the rest in blue, under Ground](img/sbsg-responsabilidade.svg){ loading=lazy }
  <figcaption>Yellow strip: Tower. Rest: Ground. The dashed line runs through the holding points. White circles: apron numbers.</figcaption>
</figure>

| Position | Responsible for |
| :--- | :--- |
| **GND** | Aprons 1 to 3, TWY **APN 1**, **A**, **B**, **E**, **F** and the links up to the **holding point** |
| **TWR** | Runway 12/30 and the taxiways inside the holding points (**B1**, **B4**, **CC** and **DD**) |

- SBSG has **no Apron** position: Ground handles all aprons.
- Every movement on the apron, engine start, pushback and taxi out under own power need **ATC authorization**[^ad20-6].
- **Departures:** Ground hands off to Tower **before the holding point**.
- **Arrivals:** once the aircraft vacates the runway, Tower hands off to Ground.

## :material-transit-connection-variant: Ground flow

!!! abstract "General rule"
    - **TWY B** runs along the north side of the runway and reaches both thresholds: through **B1** on 12 and **B4** on 30.
    - **F** links apron 2 to B and **E** links apron 1. **A** runs between F and E, parallel to B.
    - **APN 1** runs in front of the three aprons and links the stands to F and E.
    - **DD** and **CC** are rapid exits for aircraft landing on 12. On 30 they need a tight turn.

=== "Runway 12"

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![Ground flow on runway 12: departures via F and B to B1; arrivals vacating via DD and returning via B and E to apron 1](img/sbsg-fluxo-12.svg){ loading=lazy }
      <figcaption>Green: departures. Blue: arrivals.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    - **Departures (12):** from the apron via **F** (or **E**), **B** westbound to **B1** (threshold 12).
    - **Arrivals (12):** vacate via **DD** (~1390 m) or **CC** (~1860 m) and return via **B** to **E** or **F**.

    Example: `GLO1234, taxi to holding point runway 12 via F, B, B1.`

=== "Runway 30"

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![Ground flow on runway 30: departures via E and B to B4; arrivals vacating via DD and continuing via B and F to apron 2](img/sbsg-fluxo-30.svg){ loading=lazy }
      <figcaption>Green: departures. Blue: arrivals.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    - **Departures (30):** from the apron via **E** (or **F**), **B** eastbound to **B4** (threshold 30).
    - **Arrivals (30):** vacate via **DD** (~1310 m) and continue via **B** to **F** or **E**. Aircraft that can't make the DD turn vacate at the end, via **B1**.

    Example: `AZU4321, taxi to holding point runway 30 via E, B, B4.`

### Runway exits

| Runway | Exits |
| :---: | :--- |
| **12** | **DD** (~1390 m, rapid), **CC** (~1860 m, rapid) or **B4** (end) |
| **30** | **CC** (~840 m), **DD** (~1310 m) or **B1** (end) |

<small>Approximate distance from the threshold to the exit, measured on the diagram. On 30, CC and DD need a tight turn.</small>

### Taxi restrictions

| Restriction | Where |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Apron 3** | General aviation only, maximum wingspan **24 m**[^ad20-6] |
| **Helicopters** | Direct approach to apron 3, then taxi to the stand assigned by Tower[^ad20-4] |
| **ATC authorization** | Any movement on the apron, engine start, pushback and taxi out under own power[^ad20-6] |

[^ad20-4]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBSG 2.20, item 4](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).
[^ad20-6]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBSG 2.20, items 1 and 6](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).
