## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Aprons and Taxiways

- ACFT with MTOW ABV 40T: pushback compulsory when leaving the PRKG.
- Apron 2 LTD to MAX 06 ACFT and a MAX stay of 03HR, SUBJ to AUTH from the local administration. Overnight stay WO AUTH PRB.

### Additional Information

- OBS concentration of birds, notably vultures, on RWY 13/31 and in the vicinity of THR 13 between 1100 and 1900 UTC. Vultures may also be present at the same times outside the airport site, near THR 31; they tend to disperse in thermal currents.
