## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Takeoffs

- In IMC, ACFT OPS on the apron TWY PRB while there are LDG or TKOF OPS of ACFT classified as RCD 2C.
- In IMC, ACFT *pushback* OPS PRB while there are LDG or TKOF OPS of ACFT classified as RCD 2C.
- In IMC, simultaneous OPS of the RWY and the apron TWY are restricted during LDG or TKOF OPS of ACFT classified as RCD 2C.

### Aerodrome Regulations

- ACFT in VFR OPS shall comply with the following procedures:
    1. Arriving TFC for RWY 03 shall fly a right-hand traffic pattern, and for RWY 21 a left-hand traffic pattern;
    2. TFC departing RWY 03 shall turn right, and from RWY 21 shall turn left;
    3. ACFT in the traffic pattern shall not cross the banks of the Oiapoque river.
- Observe the VAC for joining or leaving the traffic pattern.
