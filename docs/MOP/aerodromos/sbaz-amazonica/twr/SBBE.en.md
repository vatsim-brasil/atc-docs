## :material-routes: Runways

### Preferential runway system

SBBE has **two crossing runways**: 07/25 (2800 × 45 m), for jets, and 03/21 (1830 × 45 m), for turboprops and piston aircraft[^ad20-10]. Neither has a parallel taxiway: the TWY only reach the areas near thresholds 07 and 03, and the rest of 07/25 has three turn pads for backtracking.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-airplane:{ .lg .middle } **Preferential: `07` and `03`**

    ---

    07 for jets, with ILS CAT I. 03 for turboprops and pistons. The TWY lead from the aprons straight to these thresholds.

-   :material-airplane:{ .lg .middle } **Alternative: `25` and `21`**

    ---

    Departures need a backtrack. 25 has RNP and VOR; 21 has no instrument approach.

</div>

!!! warning "Preferential system"
    With {==**a tailwind component of up to 7 kt**==} and a **dry runway**, the runways in use are **07** and/or **03**[^ad20-10].

    With 07/03 in use, a request for 25/21 may be **denied** because of traffic or at ATC's discretion[^ad20-10].

### Runway data

| Runway | Dimensions | TORA | LDA | Approach | Remarks |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **07** | 2800 × 45 m | 2800 m | 2800 m | ILS CAT I | ALSF-1 and PAPI. Jets |
| **25** | 2800 × 45 m | 2800 m | 2800 m | Non-precision | PAPI. Jets |
| **03** | 1830 × 45 m | 1830 m | 1830 m | Non-precision | PAPI. Turboprops and pistons |
| **21** | 1830 × 45 m | 1830 m | 1830 m | Visual | PAPI. Turboprops and pistons |

- The AD accepts aircraft up to code **4E**[^ad20-1].
- 180° turns only on the **turn pads**, unless authorized by ATC, and never pivoting on the aircraft's axis[^ad20-6].

!!! danger "HS1: runway crossing"
    Runways 07/25 and 03/21 cross ~1370 m from threshold 07 and ~1250 m from threshold 03. Coordinate movements on both runways when they are in use at the same time (ADC chart).

[^ad20-1]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBBE 2.20, item 1](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).
[^ad20-6]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBBE 2.20, item 6](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).
[^ad20-10]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBBE 2.20, item 10](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip). Declared distances: AD 2.13.

## :material-headset: ATC Units

| Code | Abbr. | Callsign | Frequency | Remarks |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **SBBE_ATIS** | `ABE` | Belém Information | **127.600** | D-ATIS |
| **SBBE_GND** | `GBE` | Belém Ground | **121.900** | `DCL` (code `SBBE`) |
| **SBBE_TWR** | `TBE` | Belém Tower | **118.700** | |

### DCL

SBBE has **DCL** (departure clearance via datalink)[^ad18]. On the network, the station code is **`SBBE`**. There is no Clearance Delivery (DEL) position: Ground issues the clearances.

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Station code** | `SBBE` |
| **Who operates it** | SBBE_GND. Without GND, the position covering clearance top-down (TWR, APP or ACC) |
| **Controller tool** | TopSky DCL window, with your personal Hoppie ACARS code |
| **Pilot tool** | Aircraft ACARS or a Hoppie-compatible client, sending the clearance request to `SBBE` |

**How it works:**

1. The pilot sends the request (RCD) to `SBBE`, with the parking stand and ATIS letter.
2. The controller checks the flight plan and replies with the clearance: limit, runway, SID, transponder code, ATIS letter and next frequency.
3. The pilot accepts (`WILCO`/`ACCEPT`). The clearance is then issued and **there is no voice readback**.
4. With the clearance accepted, the pilot calls Ground for start-up or pushback.

!!! tip "Controller setup"
    - Log in to the TopSky DCL with the code `SBBE` before opening the position.
    - Announce it in the controller information: `DCL AVBL LOGON SBBE`.
    - A faulty request (invalid plan, wrong SID, no ATIS) goes back as **REVERT TO VOICE**: the pilot calls Ground on frequency.
    - If the pilot does not accept within a reasonable time, consider the clearance not delivered and call them by voice.

[^ad18]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBBE 2.18](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).

## :material-map-marker-radius: Areas of responsibility

<figure markdown="span">
  ![Map of SBBE with both runway strips in yellow, under Tower, and the rest in blue, under Ground](img/sbbe-responsabilidade.svg){ loading=lazy }
  <figcaption>Yellow strips: Tower. Rest: Ground. The dashed line runs through the holding points. White circles: apron numbers.</figcaption>
</figure>

| Position | Responsible for |
| :--- | :--- |
| **GND** | All aprons (1 to 6 and TAG) and the TWY up to the **holding point** |
| **TWR** | Runways 07/25 and 03/21 and the TWY inside the holding points |

- SBBE **has no Apron position** (APRON): Ground handles all aprons.
- **Departures:** Ground hands off to Tower **before the holding point**. Pilots reach the holding point ready for departure; if not, they tell Ground[^ad20-12].
- **Arrivals:** after vacating the runway, Tower hands off to Ground.
- A runway 07 landing that vacates at the intersection continues along runway 03/21 to TWY **A**: the aircraft stays with Tower until it leaves 03/21.

## :material-transit-connection-variant: Ground flow

!!! abstract "General rule"
    - The **apron 3 taxiway** links **C** and **D** (to 07) with **A** (to 03/21). The **apron 4 taxiway** links the terminal with **B** (threshold 03).
    - **E**, **G**, **J** and **K** link the north-side aprons (Air Force base and hangars) to 07/25; **F** leads to apron 6.
    - **H** and **L** link apron 5 to 03/21; **M** and **N** link the TAG (general aviation terminal) near threshold 21.
    - Jets may not manoeuvre with their tail towards the terminal: stands are left with **pushback**[^ad20-2].

=== "Runways 07 and 03"

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![Ground flow on runways 07 and 03: jets depart 07 from C; turboprops depart 03 from B; runway 07 landings vacate at the intersection and follow 03/21 to A; runway 03 landings vacate via A or H](img/sbbe-fluxo-07-03.svg){ loading=lazy }
      <figcaption>Green: departures. Dashed orange: full-length departure from 07, via D. Blue: landings.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    - **Departures (07), jets:** apron 3 taxiway and **C** (TORA 2475 m). Those who need the full length continue via **D** to the threshold and say so when requesting their clearance[^ad20-11].
    - **Departures (03), turboprops and pistons:** apron 4 taxiway and **B**.
    - **Landings (07):** vacate at the **intersection** with 03/21 (~1365 m) and follow 03/21 to **A**, or backtrack at the **1st turn pad** (~1805 m) and return to **C**[^ad20-13].
    - **Landings (03):** vacate via **A** for aprons 3 and 4, or via **H** for apron 5.

    Example: `AZU4321, taxi to holding point runway 07 via C.`

=== "Runways 25 and 21"

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![Ground flow on runways 25 and 21: departures enter via C or A and backtrack to the threshold; landings vacate via C or A](img/sbbe-fluxo-25-21.svg){ loading=lazy }
      <figcaption>Green: departures, with the backtrack drawn beside the runway. Blue: landings.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    - **Departures (25):** **C**, then **backtrack** along 07/25 (about 2500 m) to threshold 25, turning on the turn pad at the end of the runway.
    - **Departures (21):** **A**, then **backtrack** along 03/21 to threshold 21, crossing 07/25. From the TAG, via **N**.
    - **Landings (25):** vacate via **C** for aprons 3 and 4, or via **G**/**K** for the north-side aprons.
    - **Landings (21):** vacate via **A** for aprons 3 and 4, or via **H** for apron 5.

    Example: `GLO1234, taxi to holding point runway 25 via C. Enter and backtrack runway 25.`

### Runway exits

Pilots must vacate the runway **completely** before stopping, with the minimum runway occupancy time (MROT)[^ad20-11].

| Runway | Exits |
| :---: | :--- |
| **07** | Intersection with 03/21 (~1365 m), 1st turn pad (~1805 m), 2nd turn pad (~2470 m) or 3rd turn pad, at the end (~2750 m). The turn pads require a backtrack |
| **25** | **K** or **G** (~2000 m, north side), **C** (~2540 m) or **D**/**E** (end) |
| **03** | **A** (~470 m), **H** (~740 m), intersection with 07/25 (~1250 m) or **M**/**N** (end) |
| **21** | Intersection with 07/25 (~580 m), **H** (~1090 m), **A** (~1360 m) or **B** (end) |

<small>Approximate distance from the threshold to the exit. Runway 07: AIP; the others measured on the diagram.</small>

### Taxi restrictions

| Restriction | Where |
| :--- | :--- |
| **In IMC** | Code **3 and 4** aircraft may not operate on runway **03/21**[^ad20-11] |
| **In IMC** | No traffic on TWY **D** during landings and takeoffs of code **3 and 4** aircraft on **07/25**[^ad20-6] |
| **Width of 15 m or less** | TWY **H** (15 m), **L** (14 m), **M** and **N** (11 m): small general aviation only[^ad28] |

[^ad20-2]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBBE 2.20, item 2](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).
[^ad28]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBBE 2.8](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip) and ADC chart.

## :material-airplane-takeoff: Takeoff points

<figure markdown="span">
  ![Overview of SBBE with numbered aprons and the takeoff points marked with the available TORA](img/sbbe-visao-geral.svg){ loading=lazy }
  <figcaption>Takeoff points (purple) with the available TORA. Circled numbers: aprons.</figcaption>
</figure>

| Runway | Point | TORA | Used by |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **07** | **C** | 2475 m | Jets, unless instructed otherwise[^ad20-11] |
| **07** | **D** (threshold) | 2800 m | Those who need the full length |
| **03** | **B** (threshold) | 1830 m | Turboprops and pistons |
| **25** | Threshold (backtrack from **C**) | 2800 m | Jets |
| **21** | Threshold (backtrack from **A**, or via **N**) | 1830 m | Turboprops and pistons |

- Unless instructed otherwise, pilots state whether they can depart 07 from **C**[^ad20-11].
- Pilots reach the holding point **ready for departure**; if not, they tell Ground[^ad20-12].
- **Immediate** line-up when cleared, and the takeoff roll starts within **10 seconds** of the takeoff clearance[^ad20-12].
- After takeoff, call APP immediately with only `BELÉM CONTROL, [CALLSIGN]`, no additional information[^ad20-12].
- Turboprops and piston aircraft expect **vectors** or a **direct** to a waypoint right after takeoff[^ad20-12].

## :material-speedometer: HIRO

**HIRO** (high-intensity runway operations) cuts runway occupancy time to shorten the spacing on final, between departures and between landings and departures[^ad20-13].

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Activation** | Broadcast on the **ATIS**; only in visual conditions with a dry runway |
| **Approach speed** | Only aircraft able to keep **170 to 140 kt IAS** between the IF and the FAF |
| **Landing on 07** | Vacate the runway **completely** before stopping, at the **intersection** (1365 m) or the **1st turn pad** (1805 m), backtracking if needed. Code A, B and C aircraft that cannot do so tell APP or Tower |
| **Takeoff** | Request through DCL; jets depart 07 from **C** |

| Measurement | Distance |
| :--- | :---: |
| Threshold 07 to the intersection | 1365 m |
| Threshold 07 to the 1st / 2nd / 3rd turn pad | 1805 / 2470 / 2750 m |
| TWY C to the intersection | 1090 m |
| TWY C to the 1st / 2nd / 3rd turn pad | 1530 / 2195 / 2475 m |

[^ad20-11]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBBE 2.20, item 11](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).
[^ad20-12]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBBE 2.20, item 12](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).
[^ad20-13]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBBE 2.20, item 13](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).
