!!! warning "Preferential Runway Operation"
    In weather conditions with a {==**tailwind component less than or equal to 7KT**==}, the preferential runway configuration will be RWY 07 for jet aircraft and RWY 03 for turboprop and piston aircraft. This configuration will normally be used in preference to RWY 25 and 21, provided that the runway surface **is dry**.
    
    When the runway in use is RWY 07 or RWY 03 with a tailwind component, pilots requesting clearance to use RWY 25 or RWY 21 should expect that their landing or takeoff {==**may be delayed**==}.

## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Takeoffs

- Pilots shall plan the TKOF so as to arrive at the holding point ready to execute it. If an immediate TKOF is not possible, inform the ATC unit in advance.
- Upon receiving TKOF clearance, the pilot is expected to begin the takeoff roll immediately (the expected reaction time is up to 10 seconds).
- Belém TWR will not inform ACFT of the TKOF time. The instruction regarding the FREQ of the next unit to be contacted AFT TKOF and, if necessary, additional instructions will be issued together with the TKOF AUTH.
- Make the initial call to Belém APP immediately after takeoff in order to obtain instructions to leave the departure runway centreline;
- The initial call to APP after takeoff shall be immediate and follow only this format: *BELÉM CONTROL, [CALLSIGN]*. Do not include any additional information beyond this format.
- Turboprop or piston aircraft: expect vectors or a clearance direct to a published waypoint shortly after takeoff.
- Unless otherwise instructed, pilots are encouraged to take off from the following intersections in order to ensure the MROT:
    - *RWY 07:*
        * All aircraft
            * TWY `C`
            * Distance from TWY `B` to TWR 25: Available TORA: 2600 m
- ACFT TFC on TWY `D` PRB during LDG and TKOF OPS of ACFT with reference code 3 and 4 in IMC COND on RWY 07/25.

### Approaches and Landings

- During LDG OPS, pilots will not report the landing gear COND to Belém TWR, EXC in EMERG situations regarding its extension and/or locking.
- Pilots shall vacate the runway at the highest speed permitted by standard operating procedures and consistent with operational safety, allowing ATC to apply minimum separation on final approach.

### Aerodrome Regulations

- Line-up shall be immediate once cleared.
- The AD may be used regularly by any ACFT compatible with RCD 4E or lower.
- Observe the VAC for joining or leaving the TFC pattern.
- The following classes and types of ACFT are restricted:
    - ACFT WO EQPT RDO;
    - GLD;
    - ACFT WO transponder or with a failure of this EQPT;
    - FLT of powered ultralights.
- The following air services are restricted:
    - Dropping of objects or spraying;
    - ACFT towing;
    - Parachute jumping;
    - Aerobatic FLT.

### Aprons and Taxiways

- All aircraft departing from aprons `03` or `04` shall perform a pushback.

### Runways

- RWY 07/25 and RWY 03/21: Do not turn around outside the turning area, EXC if AUTH by the ATS unit, avoiding turns about the ACFT axis.
- In IMC COND, OPS of ACFT with reference code 3 and 4 PRB on RWY 03/21.
