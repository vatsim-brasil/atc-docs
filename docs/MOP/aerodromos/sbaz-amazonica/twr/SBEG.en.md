## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Approaches and Landings

- The aerodrome has only two TWY: `A`, connecting the apron to THR `11`, and `B`, connecting the apron to THR `29`. Pilots should plan the LDG to vacate the RWY at its end via one of the TWY, avoiding excessive braking and taxiing on the RWY.

### Aerodrome Regulations

- The AD may be used regularly by any ACFT compatible with RCD 4E or lower.
- Observe the VAC for joining or leaving the TFC pattern.
- The following classes and types of ACFT are restricted:
    - ACFT WO EQPT RDO;
    - GLD;
    - ACFT WO transponder or with a failure of this EQPT;
    - FLT of powered ultralights.
- The following air services are restricted:
    - Dropping of objects or spraying;
    - ACFT towing;
    - Parachute jumping;
    - Aerobatic FLT.
- Special Operations Authorizations: Boeing 747-8F.

### Aprons and Taxiways

- Beware of the service roads near the THR, which cannot be used as TWY.
