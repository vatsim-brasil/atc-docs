## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Aerodrome Regulations

- The TFC circuit shall be flown only in the north sector of the AD, with the following MNM heights:
    - Category H ACFT: 600FT above AD ELEV;
    - Category A and B ACFT: 1300FT above AD ELEV.
- ACFT shall comply with the AIC in force on visual circulation in the Rio de Janeiro TMA.
- Maintenance FLT permitted only for based ACFT, DLY 0900-2300.
- Training FLT and ANAC checks permitted only for based ACFT, DLY 1900-2300.

### Aprons and Taxiways

- Engine checks permitted only at the holding point.

### Runways

- RWY 09: first 260M CLSD for LDG and last 80M CLSD for TKOF.
- RWY 27: first 80M CLSD for LDG and last 260M CLSD for TKOF.

### Additional Information

- OBS bird concentration in the AD TFC circuit.
