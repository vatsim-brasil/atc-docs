## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Departures

- IFR DEP ACFT shall COOR with Catarina Radio 10 MIN BFR EOBT.
- IFR DEP ACFT shall wait at position No. 2 for the APP-SP clearance, which will be relayed by Catarina Radio.

### Aerodrome Regulations

- TGL OPS PRB EXC for ACFT based at SBJH.
- HEL PRB APCH DCT APN WO PPR from Catarina Radio.

### Aprons and Taxiways

- Entering the APN without marshaller signals is PRB. While moving on the APN, follow the marshaller signals.
- Engine start without the presence of the marshaller is PRB.
- ENG checks PPR from the local airport administration.

### Runways

- THR 12 displaced by 173M and THR 30 displaced by 172M (RESA provided by the displacement of the opposite THR).

### Additional Information

- Bird crossing over the AD: from SECT S to N DLY 0800-1000 and from SECT N to S DLY 1900-2100.
