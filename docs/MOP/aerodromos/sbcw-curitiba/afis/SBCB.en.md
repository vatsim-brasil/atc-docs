## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Aerodrome Regulations

- AD authorised for INTL air TFC of passengers and cargo.
- ANAC checks and training FLT PRB DLY 0900-1900.
- OBS VAC for joining or leaving the TFC circuit.
- OBS Brazilian Navy ACFT on training flights in the Aldeia CTR, over the sea and near the AD.

### Runways

- RWY 10/28: 180° turns PRB for medium and large ACFT; PROC AUTH only at the THR turn pads.
- THR 10 and THR 28 displaced by 90M (RESA provided by the displacement of the opposite THR). Last 90M of each RWY CLSD for LDG/TKOF.
- Grooving along the entire length of RWY 10/28.

### Additional Information

- OBS bird concentration along the RWY.
