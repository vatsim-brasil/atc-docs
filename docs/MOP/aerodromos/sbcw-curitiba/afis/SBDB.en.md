## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Aerodrome Regulations

- The AD may be used regularly by any ACFT compatible with reference code 3C or lower, LTD to ACFT with a MAX wingspan of 36M.
- The TFC circuit shall be flown in the east SECT of the AD.

### Aprons and Taxiways

- Guidance from the airport OPR for positioning on the apron is compulsory.
- MNTN strict compliance with the TAX markings (yellow line) when entering and leaving the main apron.
- If pushback EQPT is required, requesting it is the responsibility of the ACFT OPR.

### Runways

- Turns by ACFT with a wingspan equal to or ABV 20M only at the THR.
- Type of operation per threshold:
    - *THR 18:* VFR day/night and non-precision IFR day/night;
    - *THR 36:* VFR day/night and non-precision IFR day/night.

### Additional Information

- OBS bird movement in the manoeuvring area and bird concentration in the vicinity of the AD and in the movement area.
