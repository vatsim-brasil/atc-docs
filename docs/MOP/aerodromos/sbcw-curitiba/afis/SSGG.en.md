## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Aerodrome Regulations

- The AD may be used regularly by any ACFT compatible with reference code 2C or lower.
- Special OPS authorization: ATR 72.
- TFC circuit: MNM HGT of 1200 FT for fixed-wing ACFT and 600 FT for rotorcraft.
- Jet ACFT OPR: contact and COOR with Guarapuava AFIS are mandatory for night VFR OPS.

### Aprons and Taxiways

- Guidance from the airport OPR for positioning on the apron is mandatory. If pushback EQPT is required, requesting it is the responsibility of the ACFT OPR.
- Apron LTD to 1 PSN for ACFT with a MAX wingspan of 28M and 3 PSN for ACFT with a MAX wingspan of 12M.

### Additional Information

- Bird concentration near the downwind leg of RWY 08.
