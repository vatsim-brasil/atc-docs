!!! warning "Preferred Runway Operation"

    In weather conditions with a {==**tailwind component less than or equal to 7KT**==}, the preferred runway configuration for landing and takeoff will be the use of RWY 15 and RWY 10, respectively. This configuration will normally be used in preference to RWY 28 and RWY 33, provided that the runway surface **is dry**.
    
    When the runway system in use is RWY 15 and RWY 10 with a tailwind component, pilots requesting clearance to use the RWY 28 and RWY 33 system should expect that their landing or takeoff {==**may be delayed**==}.

## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Departures

- Pilots shall plan the TKOF so as to arrive at the holding point ready to perform it. If an immediate TKOF is not possible, inform the ATC unit in advance.
- Upon receiving TKOF clearance, the pilot is expected to begin the takeoff roll immediately (the expected reaction time is up to 10 seconds).
- Galeão TWR will not report the TKOF time to ACFT. The instruction regarding the FREQ of the next unit to be contacted AFT TKOF and, if necessary, any additional instructions will be issued together with the TKOF AUTH.
- All departures shall use a noise abatement procedure, in accordance with each aircraft's manual.
- TWY `B` between `F` and `G` exclusive to ACFT with a maximum wingspan of 36m.
???+ warning "Attention!"
    ACFT with a wingspan greater than 36m departing from RWY 33 must:
    - enter TWY `F`;
    - cross RWY 15/33;
    - vacate via TWY `J`;
    - continue to TWY `H`.
- `APRON 1` exclusive to ACFT with a maximum wingspan of 36m.
???+ warning "Attention!"
    ACFT with a wingspan greater than 36m departing from RWY 10 must use TWY `L3` to leave `APRON 2` and will proceed via `K`, `N` and `P`.
    ACFT with a wingspan of less than 36m departing from RWY 10 must use TWY `L1` to leave `APRON 2` and will proceed via `N` and `P`.
- Simultaneous operations may be conducted on the runway pair 28 and 33; these operations will be activated by Galeão Tower and will be exclusively segregated, with takeoffs from runway 33 and landings on runway 28.
- Pilots should begin the takeoff from the beginning of the runway, without the need to taxi to the displaced threshold.

### Approaches and Landings

- During LDG OPS, pilots will not report the landing gear COND to Galeão TWR, EXC in EMERG situations relating to its extension and/or locking.
- Operations will be conducted using specific approach charts, whose identification contains the word *Converging* (for example, *IAC ILS U (Converging) RWY 28*), and whose missed approach points are moved back from the threshold.
- The information {==*operações simultâneas em pistas convergentes em andamento / simultaneous operations on converging runways in progress*==} will be provided via ATIS when the traffic enters the TMA.
??? warning "Attention!"
    If the pilot determines that they are unable to fly the specific approach procedures for converging runway operations, they must inform APP on first contact.
- In the event of a go-around after the MAPT, the pilot must turn before the limiting radial published on the chart. If such a manoeuvre is not possible, inform APP/TWR.

### Aerodrome Regulations

- The AD may be used regularly by any ACFT compatible with RCD 4E or lower.
- OBS VAC for joining or leaving the TFC circuit.
- Touch-and-go training by CIVIL ACFT is permitted subject to traffic availability.
- The following classes and types of ACFT are restricted:
    * ACFT WO RDO EQPT;
    * GLD;
    * ACFT WO transponder or with a failure of this EQPT;
    * Powered ultralight FLT.
- The following air services are restricted:
    * Dropping of objects or spraying;
    * ACFT towing;
    * Parachute jumping;
    * Aerobatic FLT.
- This position shall be opened only during Vatsim Brasil events; day-to-day operation is not permitted.
- Pilots shall adjust their landing and takeoff so as to ensure the Minimum Runway Occupancy Time (MROT).
Galeão Tower may conduct the operations described above[^3] in **two** distinct situations, differing only in the cut-off point, provided the following restrictions are met:
[^3]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBGL](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip) 
=== "IMC"
    !!! info "In this case, the cut-off point is **3 nm**."
    1. Weather conditions are such that visibility is equal to or greater than the procedure minima table, and the ceiling is at least 100 FT above the procedure DH;
    2. The information on simultaneous operations on converging runways in progress is provided via ATIS;
    3. The specific instrument approach chart for this type of operation is in use.
=== "VMC"
    !!! info "In this case, the cut-off point is **1.4 nm**."
    1. Weather conditions are such that the ceiling is equal to or greater than 1500 FT and visibility is equal to or greater than 5000 m;
    2. The information on simultaneous operations on converging runways in progress is provided via ATIS;
    3. The specific instrument approach chart for this type of operation is in use.
??? question "And what is the cut-off point?"
    The cut-off point is the limit point that an aircraft on final for RWY 28 must not yet have passed for a takeoff from RWY 33 to be cleared. For example:
    - In IMC, as long as an ACFT on approach to RWY 28 has not passed 3 nm before the threshold, an ACFT may be cleared for TKOF from RWY 33. Once it has passed that point, no ACFT may be cleared for TKOF until the approaching aircraft has landed or gone around without having violated the procedure's limiting RDL.
    - In VMC, this limit point is reduced to 1.4 nm from the RWY 28 threshold.
In the event of a go-around after the MAPT on runway 28, separation from aircraft departing runway 33 may be reduced, so that, in VMC, the application of visual separation may be feasible. In this case, detailed essential traffic information must be provided to pilots as soon as possible.
=== "Information to be provided to the approaching aircraft"

    Examples of phraseology to be used by the controller:

    | Structure | Example |
    |-----------|---------|
    | (Aircraft callsign) turn right, for missed approach procedure, essential local traffic, (Type), commencing takeoff runway 33. | PTATC, turn right for missed approach procedure, essential local traffic, B737 commencing takeoff runway 33. |
    | (Aircraft callsign), turn right for missed approach procedure, essential local traffic, (Type), departing runway 33, passing runway midpoint. | PTATC, turn right for missed approach procedure, essential local traffic, B737 departing runway 33, passing runway midpoint. |
    | (Aircraft callsign), turn right for missed approach procedure, essential local traffic, (Type), departing runway 33, crossing threshold 15. | PTATC, turn right for missed approach procedure, essential local traffic, B737 departing runway 33, crossing threshold 15. |

=== "Information to be provided to the departing aircraft"

    Examples of phraseology to be used by the controller:

    | Structure | Example |
    |-----------|---------|
    | (Aircraft callsign) traffic, (Type), commencing go-around runway (28), caution essential local traffic, passing threshold 28. | PTATC, traffic, B737 commencing go-around runway 28, caution essential local traffic, passing threshold 28. |
    | (Aircraft callsign) traffic, (Type), commencing go-around runway (28), caution essential local traffic, passing runway midpoint. | PTATC, traffic, B737 commencing go-around runway 28, caution essential local traffic, passing runway midpoint. |

### Aprons and Taxiways

- The use of the transponder in *altitude reporting* mode (ALT RPTG) and ADS-B (if equipped) is required while on aprons, taxiways and runway.
- With RMP in service, ACFT DEST aprons `1`, `2`, `3` and `5` must establish CTC with Apron Control before entering.
??? info "Click here to see the Galeão Apron area of responsibility"
    ![Galeão Apron area of responsibility](SBGL-Apron.png){ loading=lazy }
- TWY `Y1`, `Y2`, `Y3` and `Y4` exclusive to ACFT with a maximum wingspan of 36m.
- `APRON 1` is intended for ACFT on international flights, commercial ACFT on domestic flights, business aircraft, government agency aircraft, air taxis and, in exceptional situations, helicopters transferring passengers to and from commercial flights.
- `APRON 5` is intended for domestic general aviation ACFT, commercial aviation ACFT for maintenance or extended stays, cargo aircraft, military transport aircraft; CAN aircraft; helicopters; aircraft diverted from other airports for technical or weather reasons, except those intended for `APRON 1`; and Rio/São Paulo shuttle aircraft, on an occasional basis.
- Due to the lack of an engine run-up area, the section of TWY `M` between TWY `S` and TWY `V` will be used in exceptional cases.
