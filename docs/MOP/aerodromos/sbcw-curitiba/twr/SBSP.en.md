## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Departures

- Pilots shall plan the TKOF so as to arrive at the holding point ready to perform it. If an immediate TKOF is not possible, inform the ATC unit in advance.
- Upon receiving TKOF clearance, the pilot is expected to begin the takeoff roll immediately (the expected reaction time is up to 10 seconds).
- São Paulo TWR will not report the TKOF time to ACFT. The instruction regarding the FREQ of the next unit to be contacted AFT TKOF and, if necessary, any additional instructions will be issued together with the TKOF AUTH. Except in exceptional situations, ACFT shall maintain a listening watch on the TWR FREQ until crossing the THR opposite to the DEP RWY.
- CAT A aircraft prohibited from LDG and TKOF OPS on RWY 17R/35L, EXC MIL ACFT and ACFT performing MEDEVAC.
- Section of RWY 17L/35R at its intersection with TWY B usable as a helicopter LDG and DEP area.

### Approaches and Landings

- During LDG OPS, pilots will not report the landing gear COND to São Paulo TWR, EXC in EMERG situations relating to its extension and/or locking.

### Aerodrome Regulations

- AD not usable as an alternate.
- OBS VAC for joining or leaving the TFC circuit.
- OBS AIC on the Circulation of ACFT on VFR flights in TMA-SP.[^1]
- Composite separation may be applied on the RWY 17 final APRX and/or the RWY 35 TKOF path at SBSP, between IFR ACFT and VFR HEL flying in the HEL control area. MIN composite separation to be applied: 2.5 nm x 500 FT.
- The following air services are restricted:
    * Visual flight plans of fixed-wing ACFT with DEST or origin at this AD;
    * Training FLT;
    * Cargo FLT, EXC flights dedicated exclusively to the transport of bank pouches.
[^1]: [AIC-N 20/21](https://publicacoes.decea.mil.br/publicacao/AIC-N-2021)

### Aprons and Taxiways

- Due to TFC flow requirements in TMA SP and/or at the AD, the start-up and taxi sequence will be given by São Paulo GNDC.
- ACFT movement on TWY `S` is subject to a 15-knot speed restriction due to its narrow width and the proximity of moving obstacles on the parallel service road.
- TWY `O` limited to OPS of ACFT with a maximum wingspan of 24 m.
