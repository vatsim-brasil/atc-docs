## :material-routes: Runways

### Main and auxiliary runway

SBSP has **two parallel runways** about 220 m apart: **17R/35L**, the main runway, to the west next to the terminal, and **17L/35R**, the auxiliary runway, to the east next to the general aviation hangars. All thresholds are displaced.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-airplane:{ .lg .middle } **Main: `17R` / `35L`**

    ---

    Code 4C (A320, B737). ILS CAT I at both thresholds and EMAS at both ends. Scheduled flights.

-   :material-airplane-landing:{ .lg .middle } **Auxiliary: `17L` / `35R`**

    ---

    Code 2B; code 3C only in VMC. RNP and VOR. Turboprops, piston aircraft and general aviation.

</div>

!!! warning "Who uses each runway"
    - On **17R/35L**, landings and takeoffs are **prohibited** for wingspans **below 14.5 m** (except in IMC CAT I and aeromedical flights) and for **turboprop or piston** aircraft (except RBAC 121 and aeromedical flights)[^ad12].
    - Only aircraft able to use **both runways** may operate at SBSP, except scheduled domestic passenger flights and flights carrying patients and organs[^ad20-1].
    - A scheduled passenger flight that **cannot operate on the auxiliary runway** tells Tower **on first contact**[^ad18].
    - On **17L/35R**, **code 3** aircraft only in **VMC**. **Category A** is prohibited Monday to Friday, **1000–1300** and **2200–0000 UTC**, except MEDEVAC, military and RBAC 121/135[^ad20-1].

The AIP sets no preferential runway: the configuration (17 or 35) follows the wind and applies to both runways.

## :material-headset: ATC Units

| Code | Abbr. | Callsign | Frequency | Remarks |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **SBSP_ATIS** | `ASP` | São Paulo Information | **127.650** | D-ATIS |
| **SBSP_DEL** | `DSP` | São Paulo Clearance | **120.600** | `DCL` (code `SBSP`) |
| **SBSP_GND** | `GSP` | São Paulo Ground | **121.900** | |
| **SBSP_TWR** | `TSP` | São Paulo Tower | **127.150** | 118.050 is the spare |
| **SBSP_H_TWR** | `HSP` | Helicopter Control | **118.350** | Helicopter control area |

- **Ground** sets the start-up and taxi sequence according to the TMA and AD flow[^ad22].
- Tower does not give the takeoff time. The aircraft stays on Tower's frequency until **crossing the opposite threshold**[^ad18].
- At high-density times, landings and takeoffs use the **minimum runway occupancy time** possible[^ad22].

### DCL

SBSP has **DCL** (departure clearance via datalink)[^ad18]. On the network, the station code is **`SBSP`**.

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Station code** | `SBSP` |
| **Who operates it** | SBSP_DEL. Without DEL, the position covering clearance top-down (GND, TWR, APP or ACC) |
| **Controller tool** | TopSky DCL window, with your personal Hoppie ACARS code |
| **Pilot tool** | Aircraft ACARS or a Hoppie-compatible client, sending the clearance request to `SBSP` |

**How it works:**

1. The pilot sends the request (RCD) to `SBSP`, with the parking stand and ATIS letter.
2. The controller checks the flight plan and replies with the clearance: limit, runway, SID, transponder code, ATIS letter and next frequency.
3. The pilot accepts (`WILCO`/`ACCEPT`). The clearance is then issued and **there is no voice readback**.
4. With the clearance accepted, the pilot calls Ground for start-up or pushback.

!!! tip "Controller setup"
    - Log in to the TopSky DCL with the code `SBSP` before opening the position.
    - Announce it in the controller information: `DCL AVBL LOGON SBSP`.
    - A faulty request (invalid plan, wrong SID, no ATIS) goes back as **REVERT TO VOICE**: the pilot calls Clearance on frequency.
    - If the pilot does not accept within a reasonable time, consider the clearance not delivered and call them by voice.

[^ad18]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBSP 2.18](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).

<figure markdown="span">
  ![Map of SBSP with both runway strips in yellow, under Tower, and the rest in blue, under Ground](img/sbsp-responsabilidade.svg){ loading=lazy }
  <figcaption>Yellow strips: Tower. Rest: Ground. The dashed line runs through the holding points. White circles: apron numbers.</figcaption>

| Position | Responsible for |
| :--- | :--- |
| **GND** | Aprons 1, 2 and 3 and the TWY up to the **holding point** |
| **TWR** | Both runways and the TWY inside the holding points, including **A**, **B**, **C** and **D** between the runways |
| **Helicopter Control** | VFR helicopters in the helicopter control area |

- SBSP **has no Apron position** (APRON): Ground handles all aprons.
- The only links between the runways are **A**, **B**, **C** and **D**, all inside the strips: aircraft crossing from one side to the other stay with **Tower**.
- **Arrivals:** after vacating the runway, Tower hands off to Ground.

## :material-transit-connection-variant: Ground flow

!!! abstract "General rule"
    - **West side (main runway):** **M** and **N** run in front of apron 3. **E** and **F** lead to threshold 17R; **H** links the middle of the apron with the runway; **L** continues south to **I**, **J** and **K** (threshold 35L).
    - **East side (auxiliary runway):** **S** runs in front of aprons 1 and 2; **O** and **P** reach 17L, **Q** the middle and **R** 35R.
    - **A**, **B**, **C** and **D** link the two runways.
    - The main apron is left with **pushback**[^ad20-1].

=== "Runways 17R and 17L"

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![Ground flow on runways 17: 17R departures via N, M and E; 17R landings vacate via I and return along L and H; general aviation on 17L via S and O](img/sbsp-fluxo-17.svg){ loading=lazy }
      <figcaption>Green: departures on the main runway. Dashed orange: auxiliary runway. Blue: landings.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    - **Departures (17R):** **N** or **M** northbound and **E** to the threshold.
    - **Landings (17R):** vacate via **I** (rapid exit) or **J**/**K** at the end, and return along **L** and **H** to apron 3. Aircraft needing less runway vacate via **H**.
    - **Auxiliary runway (17L):** from aprons 1 and 2 via **S** and **O**.

    Example: `GLO1234, taxi to holding point runway 17R via N and E.`

=== "Runways 35L and 35R"

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![Ground flow on runways 35: 35L departures via N, H, L and K; 35L landings vacate via F and return along M; general aviation on 35R via S and R](img/sbsp-fluxo-35.svg){ loading=lazy }
      <figcaption>Green: departures on the main runway. Dashed orange: auxiliary runway. Blue: landings.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    - **Departures (35L):** **N**, **H**, **L** and **K** (or **J**) to the threshold.
    - **Landings (35L):** vacate via **F** or, at the end, via **E**, and return along **M**. Aircraft stopping early vacate via **H**.
    - **Auxiliary runway (35R):** from aprons 1 and 2 via **S** and **R**.

    Example: `AZU4321, taxi to holding point runway 35L via N, H, L and K.`

### Hotspots

| Hotspot | Where | Watch out |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **HS1** | Threshold 17R, on **E** | Risk of runway incursion |
| **HS2** | **F**, next to **E** | Jet blast from aircraft on E |
| **HS3** | Where **H**, **L**, **M**, **N**, **W** and **X** meet | Risk of ground collision |

### Taxi restrictions

| Restriction | Where |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Maximum wingspan 24 m** | TWY **O** and **S**[^ad20-6] |
| **TWY O in IMC** | No taxiing of code letter **B** during landings and takeoffs of code **1 or 2** on 17L/35R; O may not be used during code **3** operations on 17L/35R[^ad20-6] |
| **Maximum speed 15 kt** | TWY **N**, due to its closeness to M and the apron 3 safety line[^ad20-6] |

[^ad20-6]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBSP 2.20, item 6](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip) and ADC chart.

## :material-helicopter: Helicopters

- Helicopters land and take off **only on the runways**; never on TWY, aprons or hangars[^ad20-8]. The section of 17L/35R at the intersection with **B** can be used for helicopter landings and departures[^ad20-2].
- On the runway 17 final and the runway 35 departure path, **composite separation** may be applied between IFR aeroplanes and VFR helicopters in the helicopter control area: minimum **2.5 NM × 500 ft**[^ad22].

[^ad20-2]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBSP 2.20, item 2](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).
[^ad20-8]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBSP 2.20, item 8](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).

## :material-clipboard-text-outline: Other procedures

- **VFR** flight plans of aeroplanes to or from SBSP are not accepted, except military aircraft or when navigation aids are unavailable[^ad22].
- APP may vector for a **visual approach** with visibility of 5 km or more and ceiling of 1000 ft or more; the plan stays IFR until the pilot reports the runway in sight[^ad22].
- The aerodrome **cannot be used as an alternate**[^ad20-1].
- Observe the VAC for joining and leaving the traffic pattern[^ad23].
