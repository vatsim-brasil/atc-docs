## :material-routes: Runways

### Main and auxiliary runway

SBFL has **two runways**: **14/32** (2400 × 45 m, asphalt), the main runway, and **03/21** (1320 × 45 m, concrete), the auxiliary runway. They meet next to thresholds **14** and **21**. **D** runs along 14/32 on the south side, between the runway and the new terminal (apron 1).

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-airplane:{ .lg .middle } **Main: `14` / `32`**

    ---

    Jets and scheduled flights. ILS CAT I on 14; RNP and VOR on 32.

-   :material-airplane-landing:{ .lg .middle } **Auxiliary: `03` / `21`**

    ---

    VFR only. **No jets**[^ad20-1]. Threshold 21 displaced by 140 m.

</div>

!!! warning "HS2: thresholds 14 and 21 side by side"
    Thresholds 14 and 21 are very close and share the TWY **A** holding points. Confirm the correct runway with the pilot before clearing the line-up.

The AIP sets no preferential runway: the runway in use follows the wind.

### Runway data

| Runway | Dimensions | TORA | LDA | Approach | Remarks |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **14** | 2400 × 45 m | 2400 m | 2400 m | ILS CAT I | ALSF-1 |
| **32** | 2400 × 45 m | 2400 m | 2400 m | Non-precision | RNP and VOR |
| **03** | 1320 × 45 m | 1180 m | 1180 m | Visual | First 180 m closed (RESA) |
| **21** | 1320 × 45 m | 1320 m | 1180 m | Visual | Displaced threshold (140 m) |

- The AD accepts aircraft up to code **4C**. The **B757-200** operates under special procedures[^ad20-1].
- On 03/21, **VFR** operations only, and **jet aircraft are prohibited**[^ad20-1].

[^ad20-1]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBFL 2.20, item 1](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip). Declared distances: AD 2.13.

### DCL

SBFL has **DCL** (departure clearance via datalink)[^ad18]. On the network, the station code is **`SBFL`**. There is no Clearance Delivery (DEL) position: the ADC chart ties the DCL to the **Tower** frequency.

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Station code** | `SBFL` |
| **Who operates it** | SBFL_TWR. Without TWR, the position covering clearance top-down (APP or ACC) |
| **Controller tool** | TopSky DCL window, with your personal Hoppie ACARS code |
| **Pilot tool** | Aircraft ACARS or a Hoppie-compatible client, sending the clearance request to `SBFL` |

**How it works:**

1. The pilot sends the request (RCD) to `SBFL`, with the parking stand and ATIS letter.
2. The controller checks the flight plan and replies with the clearance: limit, runway, SID, transponder code, ATIS letter and next frequency.
3. The pilot accepts (`WILCO`/`ACCEPT`). The clearance is then issued and **there is no voice readback**.
4. With the clearance accepted, the pilot calls Ground for start-up or pushback.

!!! tip "Controller setup"
    - Log in to the TopSky DCL with the code `SBFL` before opening the position.
    - Announce it in the controller information: `DCL AVBL LOGON SBFL`.
    - A faulty request (invalid plan, wrong SID, no ATIS) goes back as **REVERT TO VOICE**: the pilot calls Tower on frequency.
    - If the pilot does not accept within a reasonable time, consider the clearance not delivered and call them by voice.

[^ad18]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBFL 2.18](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip) and ADC chart.

  ![Map of SBFL with both runway strips in yellow, under Tower, and the rest in blue, under Ground](img/sbfl-responsabilidade.svg){ loading=lazy }
  <figcaption>Yellow strips: Tower. Rest: Ground. The dashed line runs through the holding points. White circles: apron numbers.</figcaption>

| Position | Responsible for |
| :--- | :--- |
| **GND** | Aprons 1 to 4 and the TWY up to the **holding point** |
| **TWR** | Both runways and the TWY inside the holding points |

- SBFL **has no Apron position** (APRON): Ground handles all aprons.
- Aprons **2, 3 and 4** are **north** of 14/32. To reach **D** and threshold 32, aircraft **cross the runway** via **B**, with Tower's clearance.
- **Arrivals:** after vacating the runway, Tower hands off to Ground.

## :material-transit-connection-variant: Ground flow

!!! abstract "General rule"
    - **D** runs along 14/32 on the south side, from threshold 14 (via **F**) to threshold 32. **D1** to **D4** are intermediate holding positions on D.
    - **G** and **H** link D with apron 1; **Y1**, **Y2** and **Y3** run on apron 1. **G** also links D with the runway.
    - To the north, **A** links apron 3 with threshold 21 and **B** links aprons 3 and 4 with 14/32; **Y4** and **L** lead to apron 2.
    - **C** leads to the military apron, crossing 03/21 (HS4).

=== "Runway 14"

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![Ground flow on runway 14: departures via G, D and F to the threshold; landings roll to the end and return along D; aprons 3 and 4 via B, crossing the runway](img/sbfl-fluxo-14.svg){ loading=lazy }
      <figcaption>Green: departures. Dashed orange: aprons north of the runway. Blue: landings.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    - **Departures (14):** from apron 1 via **G**, **D** and **F** to the threshold.
    - **Landings (14):** vacate via **G** (~750 m, light aircraft) or roll to the end and return along **D**.
    - **Aprons 3 and 4:** via **B**, crossing the runway, to D.

    Example: `AZU4321, taxi to holding point runway 14 via G, D and F.`

=== "Runway 32"

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![Ground flow on runway 32: departures via G and D to threshold 32; landings vacate via G to apron 1](img/sbfl-fluxo-32.svg){ loading=lazy }
      <figcaption>Green: departures. Dashed orange: aprons north of the runway. Blue: landings.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    - **Departures (32):** **G** and **D** to threshold 32.
    - **Landings (32):** vacate via **G** (~1650 m) and continue to apron 1; those rolling further vacate via **F**, at the end.
    - **Aprons 3 and 4:** via **B**, crossing the runway.

    Example: `GLO1234, taxi to holding point runway 32 via G and D.`

### Runway exits

| Runway | Exits |
| :---: | :--- |
| **14** | **B** (~330 m, north side), **G** (~750 m) or **D** (end) |
| **32** | **G** (~1650 m), **B** (~2070 m, north side) or **F** (end) |

<small>Approximate distance from the threshold to the exit, measured on the diagram. The SBFL AIP publishes no exits.</small>

### Hotspots

| Hotspot | Where | Watch out |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **HS1** | **A**, next to threshold 21 | Risk of runway incursion. TWY A allows wingspans up to 36 m |
| **HS2** | Thresholds **14** and **21** | Wrong-runway departure risk: the thresholds are close and share the A holding points |
| **HS3** | **F** and **D** at 03/21 | Risk of runway incursion |
| **HS4** | **C** crossing 03/21 | Risk of runway incursion |

### Taxi restrictions

| Restriction | Where |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Maximum wingspan 36 m** | TWY **A** and **H** and apron 1 (ADC/PDC charts) |
| **Wingspan of 36 m or more** | Restrictions on some TWY under own power; Tower coordinates with the airport[^ad20-6] |
| **Maximum speed 8 kt** | TWY **Y2** on apron 1, for code C aircraft[^ad20-6] |

[^ad20-6]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBFL 2.20, item 6](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip) and ADC and PDC charts.
