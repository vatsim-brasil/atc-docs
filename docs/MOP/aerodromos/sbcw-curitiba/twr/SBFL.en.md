## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Approaches and Landings

- During SR and SS, ATTN to the possibility of glare from the rising and setting sun, which may impair seeing and identifying the THR AUTH for LDG.

### Aerodrome Regulations

- The AD may be used regularly by any ACFT compatible with RCD 4C or lower.
- Special OPS AUTH: B757-200 OPS are allowed in accordance with the special PROC described in the MOPS approved by ANAC.
- OBS VAC for joining or leaving the TFC circuit.
- Observe the AFIL prohibition.
- Observe that approaches or landing sequences may be refused to ACFT due to airport congestion.
- School and training flights should preferably be carried out on RWY 03/21 and are subject to AD flow management.
- OBS ACFT on training flights near the AD.
- The following classes and types of ACFT are restricted:
    - ACFT WO RDO EQPT;
    - GLD;
    - ACFT WO transponder or with a failure of this EQPT;
    - Powered ultralight FLT.
- The following air services are restricted:
    - Dropping of objects or spraying;
    - ACFT towing;
    - Parachute jumping;
    - Aerobatic FLT.

### Aprons and Taxiways

- Engine start-up and pushback only after COOR with the airport administration through the apron marshal. Once released, the ACFT must call the control unit.
- ACFT on domestic or INTL TFC weighing more than 12 tonnes: pushback is compulsory when leaving the PRKG.
- Movement on TWY `Y2` of Apron 1 is subject to an 8KT speed restriction for code `C` ACFT.
- ACFT with a wingspan of 36m or more are restricted from using certain TWYs under their own power (ACN/PCN balance, jet blast, wingspan and length). TWR will coordinate the operations of these ACFT with the airport administration.
- A "FOLLOW ME" vehicle can be requested through TWR.

### Runways

- RWY 03/21: LDG/TKOF OPS of jet (turbojet) ACFT PRB.
- RWY 03/21 OPR VFR day/night.
- RWY 03 first 180M and RWY 21 last 180M CLSD for LDG/TKOF due to the provision of RESA.
- THR 21 displaced 140M.

### Additional Information

- Bird concentration along the sides and in the TFC circuit and SECT APCH RWY 14/32 and 03/21: southern lapwings, ibises and swallows. Higher incidence between 0900 and 1200 UTC and in the late afternoon.
