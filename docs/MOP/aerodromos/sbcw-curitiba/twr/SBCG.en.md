## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Departures

- VFR ACFT taking off from RWY 06: turning right BFR crossing the opposite THR is PRB.

### Approaches and Landings

- RWY 06: the ILS ICG and PAPI touchdown points do not coincide and are 40M apart.

### Aerodrome Regulations

- The AD may be used regularly by any ACFT compatible with RCD 4C or lower.
- OBS VAC for joining or leaving the traffic circuit.
- ACFT DEST Campo Grande Air Base must call Campo Grande OPS on FREQ 122.500 MHZ.
- Helicopter TFC circuit: HGT MNM 600FT (ALT MNM 2433FT).
- Filing flight plans by voice with TWR by ACFT DEP SBCG is PRB, EXC for ACFT covered by the AD emergency plan.
- The following classes and types of ACFT are restricted:
    - ACFT WO RDO EQPT;
    - GLD;
    - ACFT WO transponder or with a failure of this EQPT;
    - Powered ultralight FLT.
- The following air services are restricted:
    - Dropping of objects or spraying;
    - ACFT towing;
    - Parachute jumping;
    - Aerobatic FLT.

### Aprons and Taxiways

- ACFT parked on the MIL apron must call the Operational Duty Officer (OPO) for start-up instructions.
- Taxiing of MIL E-130 (Hercules) ACFT on the CIV apron road only with AD AUTH and COOR.
- Engine run-ups (TKOF power) for CAT A ACFT on the shared road giving access to the hangars, at the marked PSN.
- Engine run-ups (TKOF power) for CAT B, C and above ACFT on TWY `C`, PSN U6 and U7.
- Apron for small and medium general aviation ACFT kept outside hangars: tie-down required.

### Additional Information

- OBS ACFT on training flights near the AD.
- Bird concentration mainly in the APCH/TKOF SECT of RWY 06/24.
- Bird concentration at COORD 20 33 42.7S/054 39 33.8W, within a 5.4NM radius of the AD.
