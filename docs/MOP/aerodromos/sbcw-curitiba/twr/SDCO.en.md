## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Aerodrome Regulations

- Special VFR TKOF/LDG OPS at SDCO are PRB.
- OBS VAC and the AIC on Visual Circulation in the São Paulo TMA.

### Aprons and Taxiways

- ACFT are prohibited from entering or leaving the Private Area with engines running:
    - Exit: ACFT must be towed to the marked safety strip (holding position) and wait for the gate to open before starting engines;
    - Entry: engines must be shut down at the marked safety strip (holding position), wait for the gate to open, and the ACFT is towed from that point.
- Engine run-ups on the hangar aprons are PRB.

### Runways

- THR 01 displaced by 50M.
- THR 19 displaced by 100M.
