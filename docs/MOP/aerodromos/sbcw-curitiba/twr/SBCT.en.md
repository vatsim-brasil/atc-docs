## :material-routes: Runways

### Preferential runway system

SBCT has **two crossing runways**. 15/33 (2218 m) is the main runway; 11/29 (1798 m) is used by light aircraft. The runways are used **in pairs**: the **11/15** system or the **29/33** system[^ad20-10].

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-airplane:{ .lg .middle } **Main runway: `15` / `33`**

    ---

    2218 × 45 m. **All landings** and takeoffs by **ATRs and jets**. ILS CAT II on 15 and CAT I on 33.

-   :material-airplane-takeoff:{ .lg .middle } **Secondary runway: `11` / `29`**

    ---

    1798 × 45 m. Takeoffs by **light aircraft**, depending on the departure sector. Landings only on pilot or ATC request.

</div>

!!! warning "Preferential system: 11/15"
    With a {==**tailwind component of 7 kt or less**==} and a **dry runway**, the **11/15** system is used in preference to 29/33[^ad20-10].

    With the 11/15 system in use and a tailwind, pilots requesting the 29/33 system should expect a **delay** to their landing or takeoff[^ad20-10].

| System | Landings | ATR and jet takeoffs | Light aircraft takeoffs |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **11/15** (preferential) | **15** | **15** | **11** for the sector between CTB VOR radials **020 and 129**; **15** otherwise |
| **29/33** | **33** | **33** | **29** for the sector between CTB VOR radials **210 and 315**; **33** otherwise |

!!! info "Aircraft groups used on this page"
    | Group | Examples | In the AIP |
    | :--- | :--- | :--- |
    | **Light** | Piston, C208, King Air | Code 1 and 2 |
    | **ATR** | ATR 42 and ATR 72 | Code 3 |
    | **Jets** | A320, B737, E-Jets and larger | Code 4 |

    The AIP groups aircraft by the runway length they need for takeoff. If in doubt, the pilot says what they can comply with.

### Runway data

| Runway | Dimensions | TORA | LDA | Approach | Remarks |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **15** | 2218 × 45 m | 2218 m | 2218 m | ILS CAT II, RNP, VOR | No taxiway to the threshold: entry via C (see [Takeoff points](#takeoff-points)) |
| **33** | 2218 × 45 m | 2218 m | 2218 m | ILS CAT I, RNP, VOR | Entry via F, at the threshold |
| **11** | 1798 × 45 m | 1798 m | 1798 m | RNP, VOR | Entry via A, at the threshold |
| **29** | 1798 × 45 m | 1798 m | 1798 m | RNP, VOR | No taxiway to the threshold |

- 180° turns (*backtrack*) by aircraft with MTOW above **40 t** only **on the thresholds**, on both runways[^ad20-6].
- The aerodrome accepts aircraft up to wide-bodies such as the **B777** and **A330** (code 4E). The **B747-8F** operates under special procedures approved by ANAC[^ad20-1].

### DCL

SBCT has **DCL** (departure clearance via datalink)[^ad18]. On the network, the station code is **`SBCT`**.

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Station code** | `SBCT` |
| **Who operates it** | SBCT_DEL. Without DEL, whichever position covers clearance top-down (GND, TWR, APP or ACC) |
| **Controller tool** | TopSky DCL window, with your personal Hoppie ACARS code |
| **Pilot tool** | Aircraft ACARS or a Hoppie-compatible client, sending the clearance request to `SBCT` |

**How it works:**

1. The pilot sends the request (RCD) to `SBCT`, with the stand and the ATIS letter.
2. The controller checks the flight plan and replies with the clearance: limit, runway, SID, squawk, ATIS letter and next frequency.
3. The pilot accepts (`WILCO`/`ACCEPT`). The clearance is then delivered and **no voice readback is needed**.
4. Once the clearance is accepted, the pilot calls Ground directly for start-up or pushback.

!!! tip "Controller setup"
    - Log in to the TopSky DCL with the code `SBCT` before opening the position.
    - Announce it in the controller information: `DCL AVBL LOGON SBCT`.
    - A request with errors (invalid plan, wrong SID, no ATIS) is answered with **REVERT TO VOICE**: the pilot calls Clearance on frequency.
    - If the pilot does not accept within a reasonable time, treat the clearance as not delivered and call by voice.

!!! info "Takeoff from intersection C"
    On runway 15, **light aircraft and ATRs** take off from intersection **C**. Pilots unable to comply **say so when requesting taxi**[^ad20-13-2].

[^ad18]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBCT 2.18](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).

## :material-map-marker-radius: Areas of responsibility

<figure markdown="span">
  ![SBCT map with both runway strips in yellow, under Tower, and the rest in blue, under Ground](img/sbct-responsabilidade.svg){ loading=lazy }
  <figcaption>Yellow strips: Tower. Everything else: Ground. The dashed line runs through the runway holding points. Colored circles: handoff points and crossings, explained below. White circles: apron numbers.</figcaption>
</figure>

The split follows the **runway holding points**: everything inside the holding points of 15/33 and 11/29 belongs to Tower.

| Position | Responsible for |
| :--- | :--- |
| **GND** | Aprons 1 and 2, the hangar area, TWY **A**, **B**, **L** (apron 1 taxiway), **G**, **H**, **J**, **M** and **N** and the parts of **C**, **D**, **E** and **F** **up to the holding point** |
| **TWR** | Both runways, the **runway 11 threshold crossing** between A and C, the **15/33 crossing via E** and the taxiways inside the holding points |

**Handoff points** (circles on the map):

1. :material-numeric-1-circle:{ style="color:#12a150" } **Departure:** Ground hands the aircraft to Tower **before the holding point**: on **A**, for 11 and for 15 (the route to C crosses the runway 11 threshold), and on **F**, for 33.
2. :material-numeric-2-circle:{ style="color:#3d7fd6" } **Arrival:** after vacating, the pilot **does not call Ground until instructed by Tower**[^ad20-11]. Aircraft vacating 33 via C cross the runway 11 threshold still with Tower and only change to Ground on A (HEAVY or wingspan above 36 m: only after vacating via E).
3. :material-numeric-3-circle:{ style="color:#e0a100" } **Crossings:** the runway 11 threshold (between A and C) and 15/33 via E (towards D) only with Tower clearance.

[^ad20-11]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBCT 2.20, item 11](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).

## :material-transit-connection-variant: Ground flow

!!! abstract "General rule"
    - **TWY B** is the route between the aprons and the runways, parallel to 15/33. Eastbound it leads to **F** (runway 33 threshold); westbound, via **A**, to the runway 11 threshold and to **C**.
    - **G**, **H**, **J**, **M** and **N** link the apron 1 taxiway (**L**) to B. Apron 2 faces B directly.
    - Runway 15 **has no taxiway to its threshold**: the entry is via **C**, 1740 m from the runway 33 threshold.

=== "11/15 system (preferential)"

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![Ground flow in the 11/15 system: departures via B and A to C, with a backtrack for jets; landings on 15 vacating via E or F](img/sbct-fluxo-15.svg){ loading=lazy }
      <figcaption>Green: departures, from the apron exit to the runway. Dashed green: backtrack. Blue: arrivals. Dashed orange: HEAVY or wingspan above 36 m. Red bar: mandatory hold before crossing the runway 11 threshold.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    - **Departures (15):** taxi via **B** westbound, **A** and **C**, crossing the runway 11 threshold with Tower clearance. Light aircraft and ATRs take off from **C** (TORA 1740 m); jets enter via C and **backtracks** to the runway 15 threshold.
    - **HEAVY or wingspan above 36 m (15):** via **B** to **E**, enter the runway and **backtrack** to the runway 15 threshold.
    - **Departures (11):** light aircraft departing to the eastern sector, via **B** and **A** to the runway 11 threshold.
    - **Landings (15):** light aircraft vacate via **E** (1465 m from the threshold); ATRs and jets vacate via **F**, at the end of the runway[^ad20-13-1].

    Example: `GLO1234, taxi to holding point runway 15 via B, A, C.`

=== "29/33 system"

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![Ground flow in the 29/33 system: departures via B to F, runway 33 threshold; landings on 33 vacating via C or via runway 29 towards A](img/sbct-fluxo-33.svg){ loading=lazy }
      <figcaption>Green: departures, from the apron exit to the runway. Blue: arrivals. Dashed orange: HEAVY or wingspan above 36 m. Red bar: mandatory hold before crossing the runway 11 threshold.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    - **Departures (33):** taxi via **B** eastbound to **F**, at the threshold (TORA 2218 m).
    - **Departures (29):** light aircraft departing to the western sector. Runway 29 has no taxiway to its threshold: access is via **D**, crossing 15/33 via **E** with Tower clearance.
    - **Landings (33):** ATRs and jets vacate via **C** (1740 m from the threshold) and return via **A**, crossing the runway 11 threshold; light aircraft vacate **via runway 29**, towards **A** (1200 m from the runway 33 threshold)[^ad20-13-1].

    - **HEAVY or wingspan above 36 m (33):** vacate via **C** to the runway 11 threshold, **backtrack on 29** to the runway intersection, **backtrack on 33** and vacate via **E**. It stays with Tower until vacating via E.

    Example: `AZU4321, taxi to holding point runway 33 via B, F.`

### Runway exits

During HIRO, pilots must vacate via the exit below or tell APP or Tower as soon as possible if they cannot[^ad20-13-1].

| Runway | Light aircraft | ATRs and jets |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **15** | **E** (1465 m) | **F** (end of the runway) |
| **33** | **Runway 29**, towards A (1200 m) | **C** (1740 m) |

<small>Distance from the threshold to the exit. HEAVY aircraft or those with a wingspan above 36 m landing on 33 follow their own flow, described below.</small>

### Taxi restrictions

| Restriction | Where |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Maximum wingspan 36 m** | All of TWY **A** and TWY **B** between **E** and **A**[^ad20-6] |
| **IMC, wingspan of 36 m or more** | Prohibited on **B** and **E** while an ATR or jet is landing or taking off[^ad20-6] |
| **RVR below 350 m** | One aircraft at a time on the manoeuvring area. Stop bars are on **C**, **E** and **F**[^ad29] |
| **Wingspan above 24 m** | Leaving the stand only with **pushback**[^ad20-2] |

!!! warning "HEAVY or wingspan above 36 m"
    These aircraft use neither **A** nor **B** west of **E** and follow their own flow:

    - **Takeoff from 15:** enter via **E** and **backtrack** to the runway 15 threshold.
    - **Landing on 33:** vacate via **C**, **backtrack on 29** to the runway intersection, **backtrack on 33** and vacate via **E**.

[^ad20-13-1]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBCT 2.20, item 13.1](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).
[^ad20-2]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBCT 2.20, item 2](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).
[^ad29]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBCT 2.9](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip) and AD 2.20, item 6.

## :material-airplane-takeoff: Takeoff points

<figure markdown="span">
  ![SBCT overview with numbered aprons and the takeoff points marked with the available TORA](img/sbct-visao-geral.svg){ loading=lazy }
  <figcaption>Takeoff points (purple) with the available TORA. Circled numbers: aprons.</figcaption>
</figure>

| Runway | Point | TORA | Used by |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **15** | **C** | **1740 m** | Light aircraft and ATRs[^ad20-13-2] |
| **15** | Threshold, backtracking from C | 2218 m | Jets and anyone not accepting the intersection |
| **15** | Threshold, backtracking from E | 2218 m | HEAVY or wingspan above 36 m |
| **33** | **F** (threshold) | 2218 m | Everyone |
| **11** | **A** (threshold) | 1798 m | Light aircraft, sector between CTB VOR radials 020 and 129 |

- Pilots reach the holding point **ready for departure**; if not, they tell Tower before entering the runway[^ad20-12].
- Line up **immediately** when cleared, and start the takeoff roll within **10 seconds** of the takeoff clearance[^ad20-12].
- The next unit's frequency comes **with the takeoff clearance**. Tower **does not give** the takeoff time[^ad20-12].
- After takeoff, call APP immediately with only `CONTROLE CURITIBA, [CALLSIGN]`, with no additional information[^ad20-12].

[^ad20-13-2]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBCT 2.20, item 13.2](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).
[^ad20-12]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBCT 2.20, item 12](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).

## :material-speedometer: HIRO

**HIRO** (high-intensity runway operations) cuts runway occupancy time to shorten separation on final, between departures and between arrivals and departures[^ad20-13].

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Hours** | **1130–1530 UTC** and **2000–2300 UTC**. Tower may change them according to demand; the hours are broadcast on the **ATIS** |
| **Approach speed** | **170 to 150 kt IAS** between the IF and the FAF |
| **Landing** | Vacate the runway **completely** at the highest safe speed before slowing to taxi speed, via the planned exit (see [Runway exits](#runway-exits)) |
| **Takeoff** | On 15, light aircraft and ATRs take off from **C** |

[^ad20-13]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBCT 2.20, item 13](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).

## :material-clipboard-text-outline: Other procedures

### Approaches and landings

- Vacate the runway **completely** before stopping[^ad20-11].
- After vacating, **do not call Ground** until instructed by Tower[^ad20-11].
- Pilots **do not report** gear down, except in an emergency.

### Aerodrome regulations

- **Training** (circuits, touch-and-go, IFR procedure training) **prohibited** Monday to Friday, **1100–1500** and **1900–2200 UTC**. Outside those hours, it needs authorization from Curitiba APP coordinated with Tower[^ad20-1].
- **Restricted** aircraft: no radio, gliders, no transponder (or transponder failure) and powered ultralights[^ad20-1].
- **Restricted** air services: object dropping or spraying, aerial towing, parachute dropping and aerobatic flight[^ad20-1].
