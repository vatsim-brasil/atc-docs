## :material-routes: Runways

### Runway system

SBPA has **one runway**, 11/29 (3200 × 45 m, asphalt). All the TWY are in the **western third and the middle** of the runway: **D** runs along the north side only between **C** and **E**. East of E up to threshold 29 there are no TWY, just the **1st turn pad** (~2400 m from threshold 11) and the turn pad at the end of the runway.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-airplane:{ .lg .middle } **Runway `11`**

    ---

    ILS CAT I and CAT II (ALSF-2). Departure from the threshold, via A or J.

-   :material-airplane:{ .lg .middle } **Runway `29`**

    ---

    RNP and VOR. Departure from intersection E (TORA 1994 m).

</div>

!!! info "Runway selection"
    The AIP sets no preferential runway: the runway in use follows the wind.

### Runway data

| Runway | Dimensions | TORA | LDA | Approach | Remarks |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **11** | 3200 × 45 m | 3200 m | 3200 m | ILS CAT I/II | ALSF-2 |
| **29** | 3200 × 45 m | 3200 m | 3200 m | Non-precision | RNP and VOR |

- The AD accepts aircraft up to code **4E**[^ad20-1].
- On approach to 11, **do not confuse** the runway lights with those of Av. Sertório, parallel and 900 m to the right. On final for 29, do not confuse it with the approach to runway 31 at Canoas, to the right[^ad23].

[^ad20-1]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBPA 2.20, item 1](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip). Declared distances: AD 2.13.

### DCL

SBPA has **DCL** (departure clearance via datalink)[^ad18]. On the network, the station code is **`SBPA`**.

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Station code** | `SBPA` |
| **Who operates it** | SBPA_DEL. Without DEL, the position covering clearance top-down (GND, TWR, APP or ACC) |
| **Controller tool** | TopSky DCL window, with your personal Hoppie ACARS code |
| **Pilot tool** | Aircraft ACARS or a Hoppie-compatible client, sending the clearance request to `SBPA` |

**How it works:**

1. The pilot sends the request (RCD) to `SBPA`, with the parking stand and ATIS letter.
2. The controller checks the flight plan and replies with the clearance: limit, runway, SID, transponder code, ATIS letter and next frequency.
3. The pilot accepts (`WILCO`/`ACCEPT`). The clearance is then issued and **there is no voice readback**.
4. With the clearance accepted, the pilot calls Ground for start-up or pushback.

!!! tip "Controller setup"
    - Log in to the TopSky DCL with the code `SBPA` before opening the position.
    - Announce it in the controller information: `DCL AVBL LOGON SBPA`.
    - A faulty request (invalid plan, wrong SID, no ATIS) goes back as **REVERT TO VOICE**: the pilot calls Clearance on frequency.
    - If the pilot does not accept within a reasonable time, consider the clearance not delivered and call them by voice.

[^ad18]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBPA 2.18](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).

## :material-map-marker-radius: Areas of responsibility

<figure markdown="span">
  ![Map of SBPA with the runway strip in yellow, under Tower, and the rest in blue, under Ground](img/sbpa-responsabilidade.svg){ loading=lazy }
  <figcaption>Yellow strip: Tower. Rest: Ground. The dashed line runs through the holding points. White circles: apron numbers.</figcaption>
</figure>

| Position | Responsible for |
| :--- | :--- |
| **GND** | Aprons 1 to 4 and the TWY up to the **holding point** |
| **TWR** | Runway 11/29 and the TWY inside the holding points |

- SBPA **has no Apron position** (APRON): Ground handles all aprons.
- Aprons 3 and 4 (general aviation) are **south** of the runway: the way to the terminal crosses the runway via **K** and **C**, with Tower's clearance.
- **Departures:** Ground hands off to Tower **before the holding point**. Pilots reach the holding point ready for departure; if not, they tell Ground[^ad20-10].
- **Arrivals:** after vacating the runway, Tower hands off to Ground.

## :material-transit-connection-variant: Ground flow

!!! abstract "General rule"
    - **M3** and **M4** run in front of apron 1; **P** serves apron 2.
    - **B** and **J** (or **A**) lead to threshold 11; **C** links apron 1 with the runway and, on the other side, with **K** (apron 3).
    - **D** runs along the runway between **C** and **E**; **F** and **E** are the exits to apron 1.
    - **L** and **H** serve general aviation south of the runway. **L** is **closed** between **K** and **G** (glide slope antenna protection)[^ad28].
    - Aprons 1 and 2: wingspans above **16 m** leave with **pushback**; there is no stand for self-manoeuvring out[^ad20-3].

=== "Runway 11"

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![Ground flow on runway 11: departures via M3, B and J to the threshold; via C only during HIRO; landings vacate via F or E and return along D](img/sbpa-fluxo-11.svg){ loading=lazy }
      <figcaption>Green: departures. Dashed orange: departure from C (HIRO) and exit via E. Blue: landings.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    - **Departures (11):** **M3**, **B** and **J** (or **A**) to the threshold, preferably: that section is concrete[^ad20-10].
    - **Intersection C:** only with **HIRO** active, coordinated with Ground beforehand, and it may be refused according to Tower's planning[^ad20-10].
    - **Landings (11):** vacate via **F** (1502 m) or **E** (1994 m) and return along **D**. If a backtrack is needed, do it at the **1st turn pad**[^ad20-10].

    Example: `GLO1234, taxi to holding point runway 11 via M3, B and J.`

=== "Runway 29"

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![Ground flow on runway 29: departures via D to E; via F if agreed; landings vacate via C](img/sbpa-fluxo-29.svg){ loading=lazy }
      <figcaption>Green: departures. Dashed orange: departure from F. Blue: landings.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    - **Departures (29):** **M3**, **M4** and **D** to **E** (TORA 1994 m). Those unable to depart from E say so when requesting their clearance[^ad20-10].
    - **Other options:** **F** (TORA 1507 m) or the **1st turn pad** (TORA 2280 m), with a backtrack.
    - **Landings (29):** vacate via **C** (2627 m) and follow **M3** to apron 1[^ad20-10].

    Example: `AZU4321, taxi to holding point runway 29 via M3, M4, D and E.`

!!! warning "Entering at intersections C, E and F"
    These TWY have flexible pavement. When entering the runway from them, follow the **yellow line** and **do not pivot** on the aircraft's axis[^ad20-10].

### Runway exits

Pilots vacate the runway **completely** before slowing to taxi speed. Those needing another exit, a backtrack, or not configured for the exits below tell APP or Tower **on first contact**, preferably at the FAF[^ad20-10].

| Runway | Exits |
| :---: | :--- |
| **11** | **F** (1502 m), **E** (1994 m) or backtrack at the **1st turn pad** |
| **29** | **C** (2627 m) |

### Hotspots

| Hotspot | Where | Watch out |
| :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **HS1** | Intersection of **A** and **J**, next to threshold 11 | The layout needs attention: risk of runway incursion |

### Taxi restrictions

| Restriction | Where |
| :--- | :--- |
| **TWY L closed** | Between **K** and **G**[^ad28] |
| **Maximum speed 8 kt** | TWY **P**[^ad28] |
| **Wingspan up to 16 m** | Apron 3[^ad20-3] |

## :material-speedometer: HIRO and reduced separation

**HIRO** (high-intensity runway operations) applies when activated on the **ATIS**[^ad20-10].

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Approach speed** | Code A, B and C and scheduled flights: **140 to 170 kt IAS** between the IF and the FAF. ATC-assigned speeds are mandatory |
| **Landing** | Vacate the runway **completely** via the exit listed in the runway exits table |
| **Takeoff** | 11 from the threshold (C only if coordinated); 29 from **E** |
| **Separation on final** | Minimum **5 NM** surveillance separation[^ad20-11] |

!!! info "RRSM: reduced runway separation minima"
    Tower may apply reduced separation when all of the conditions hold and RRSM is on the **ATIS**: wake separation respected, visibility of **5 km** or more, ceiling of **1000 ft** or more, tailwind up to **4 kt** and an uncontaminated runway[^ad20-11].

    | Runway | Landing after landing | Landing after departure | Departure after departure |
    | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
    | **29** | Preceding aircraft passed **2600 m** from the threshold, moving and vacating via **C** without backtracking | Preceding aircraft passed **2600 m** (C) | Not permitted |
    | **11** | Not permitted | Preceding aircraft passed **2400 m** (1st turn pad) | Preceding aircraft passed **2400 m** (1st turn pad) |

    Example: `AZU4321, cleared to land runway 29, wind 350 degrees 11 knots. A320 ahead vacating the runway.`

[^ad20-10]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBPA 2.20, item 10](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).
[^ad20-11]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBPA 2.20, item 11](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).
