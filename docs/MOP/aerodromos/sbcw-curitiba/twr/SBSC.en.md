## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Approaches and Landings

- Land with caution due to F-5 arresting barriers positioned between the stopways and the THR of RWY 05/23.
- RAG RWY 05 and RWY 23: cable (hook) arresting only.

### Aerodrome Regulations

- OPS of civil ACFT only with prior AUTH from the BASC Commander.
- It is mandatory to report the estimated DEP/LDG time and contact the Duty Operations Officer (OPO) on FREQ 121.150 at least 15 MIN in advance, so that a RWY inspection can be carried out. Non-compliance may result in a go-around or airborne holding.
- Contact with OPS Santa Cruz on FREQ 121.150 is mandatory before engine start.
- When the D-TWR service is unavailable, only FIS and ALRS will be provided.
- OBS VAC for joining or leaving the traffic circuit.
- OBS the PROC set out in AIC 20/21.
- Overflight of area SBR 346 MOEDA above 500FT within the Santa Cruz CTR only with prior AUTH from TWR Santa Cruz.
- AUTH to cross the Santa Cruz CTR (outside the visual corridors) for ACFT on visual flights will only be granted under the following conditions:
    - Prior AUTH from APP Santa Cruz and/or TWR Santa Cruz;
    - No interference with the TFC circuit, DEP or LDG at SBSC;
    - No military operation at SBSC;
    - No interference with the Restinga restricted airspace (SBR 309);
    - No interference with the Marambaia restricted airspaces (SBR 313, SBR 314 and SBR 333);
    - No overflight of SDMR.
- State ACFT are not subject to the restrictions above; the crossing will be AUTH provided it does not endanger OPS at SBSC and does not interfere with the restricted airspaces.
- Filing flight plans by RTF is not AUTH.

### Runways

- DEP/LDG OPS preferably on RWY 23 due to wildlife risk (capybaras).
- RWY 05: LDG/TKOF OPS of fixed-wing ACFT AUTH BTN 01HR AFT SR and 01HR BFR SS.
- RWY 23: CLSD for night LDG/TKOF OPS of fixed-wing ACFT.
- RWY 23: daytime TGL OPS of ACFT not based at BASC only with AUTH from the BASC Commander.

### Additional Information

- Presence of animals in the vicinity of RWY 05/23.
