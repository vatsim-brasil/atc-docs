## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Departures

- BFR TKOF, contact TWR Taubaté to receive instructions, due to military manoeuvres.

### Aerodrome Regulations

- IFR and night VFR OPS AVBL only for MIL ACFT of Brazilian Army Aviation.
- OPS of non-based MIL ACFT, police or public agency ACFT only with prior AUTH. Other ACFT only in exceptional situations, with AUTH from the Commander of the Taubaté Aviation Base.
- Ultralights and gyrocopters must fly the circuit established for helicopters.
- OBS VAC for joining or leaving the traffic circuit.
- Flight plans are not accepted by radiotelephony.

### Runways

- THR 08 displaced by 400M due to obstacles (trees) on the APCH.

### Additional Information

- For contingency procedures and low-altitude VMC circulation near the AD, OBS the obstacles published on the VAC.
