## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Aerodrome Regulations

- CIV ACFT OPS only with CLR from the Commander of BACO (Canoas Air Base).
- LDG/TKOF OPS: CTC with Canoas OPS on FREQ 122.500 MHZ is compulsory.
- OBS VAC for joining or leaving the TFC circuit.

### Aprons and Taxiways

- TWY `C` (Charlie) CLSD due to ASPH degradation.

### Runways

- RWY 13/31 SWYs are equipped with arresting barriers for jet ACFT, activated by TWR. Operating limits: ACFT up to 15,870 kg at speeds up to 190KT, or ACFT up to 25,500 kg at speeds up to 135KT.

### Additional Information

- OBS bird concentration over the RWY between 1000FT and 2000FT ALT during SR and SS.
