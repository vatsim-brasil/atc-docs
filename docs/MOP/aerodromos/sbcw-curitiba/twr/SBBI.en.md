## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Aerodrome Regulations

- Type of operation: VFR day/night and IFR non-precision day/night.
- OBS VAC for joining or leaving the traffic circuit.
- OBS ACFT, gliders and ultralights on training flights near the AD.
- Touchdown of rotary-wing ACFT on unpaved areas is PRB.

### Aprons and Taxiways

- Engine run-ups on the Military Apron only with AUTH from the Commander of CINDACTA II.
- Engine run-ups only with prior AUTH from the concessionaire. Once authorized, the ACFT must wait for the marshaller before starting the engine run-up.
- Rotary-wing ACFT: beware of jet blast on the central grass area due to engine run-ups.
- Boarding and disembarking of passengers with the ACFT engine running on Apron 1 is PRB.

### Runways

- THR 18 displaced by 36M.
- THR 36 displaced by 45M.

### Additional Information

- Beware of the possibility of unmanned hot-air balloons (festa junina balloons) in the vicinity of the AD. Higher incidence in JUN, JUL, AUG, SEP and OCT.
- Bird concentration (southern lapwings, burrowing owls, swallows and ibises) in the SECT APCH circuit of RWY 18/36 and on the LT (adjacent grass areas) of RWY 18/36.
