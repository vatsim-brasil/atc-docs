## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Approaches and Landings

- Traffic circuit in the north sector is prohibited for civil ACFT.
- Precision approach radar (PAR) available for RWY 11 and 29 upon prior contact with the OPO, at least 1HR in advance.

### Aerodrome Regulations

- Civil ACFT OPS during HR 1100-2300; scheduled flights according to previously established times.
- TGL operations only with prior AUTH from the Operations Officer.
- MIL ACFT: contact the Duty Operations Officer (OPO) on FREQ 122.900.
- VAC for exclusive use of military ACFT.

### Aprons and Taxiways

- TWY `F` BTN TWY `C` and TWY `D`: OPS of ACFT with a wingspan of 21M or more PRB.
- Section of RWY 02/20 between RWY 11/29 and the TWY: taxiing of ACFT with an ACN of 14 or more (Hercules) is prohibited.
- Engine run-ups on the aprons are PRB. Only AUTH in the run-up area and at the THR.

### Runways

- RWY 02/20 exclusively for ARR and DEP of MIL ACFT.
- Stopways of RWY 11 and 29 are equipped with arresting barriers, activated by TWR.

### Additional Information

- OBS ACFT on training flights near the AD.
