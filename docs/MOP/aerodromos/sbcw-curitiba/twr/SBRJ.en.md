## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Departures

- Pilots shall plan the TKOF so as to arrive at the holding point ready to perform it. If an immediate TKOF is not possible, inform the ATC unit in advance.
- Upon receiving TKOF clearance, the pilot is expected to begin the takeoff roll immediately (the expected reaction time is up to 10 seconds).
- Rio TWR will not report the TKOF time to ACFT. The instruction regarding the FREQ of the next unit to be contacted AFT TKOF and, if necessary, any additional instructions will be issued together with the TKOF AUTH.

### Approaches and Landings

- During LDG OPS, pilots will not report the landing gear COND to Rio TWR, EXC in EMERG situations relating to its extension and/or locking.

### Aerodrome Regulations

- Primary instruction, elementary piloting, training and cargo flights are prohibited.
- OBS VAC for joining or leaving the TFC circuit.
- VMC climbs for ACFT bound SOUTH shall be made via BARRA (entrance of Guanabara Bay).
- Touch-and-go training by CIVIL ACFT is permitted subject to traffic availability.

### Aprons and Taxiways

- `PÁTIO DECEA 1` and `PÁTIO DECEA 2` AVBL only for MIL ACFT.
- `PÁTIO GEIV` exclusive to fixed-wing MIL ACFT, with a maximum wingspan of 20m and a maximum length of 21m.
