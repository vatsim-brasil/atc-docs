## :material-routes: Runways

### Main and auxiliary runway

SBRJ has **two very close parallel runways** (~76 m between centerlines) on the edge of Guanabara Bay: **02R/20L**, the main runway, to the east by the water, and **02L/20R**, the auxiliary runway, to the west by the aprons. **Every TWY linking the aprons with the main runway crosses the auxiliary runway.**

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-airplane:{ .lg .middle } **Main: `02R` / `20L`**

    ---

    1323 × 42 m, code 4C. RNP (several AR) and VOR approaches; no ILS. Jets and scheduled flights.

-   :material-airplane-landing:{ .lg .middle } **Auxiliary: `02L` / `20R`**

    ---

    1260 × 30 m, code 3C. General aviation and turboprops. Visual.

</div>

!!! warning "02L/20R is in the way"
    Taxiing between the aprons and 02R/20L always **crosses 02L/20R**: via **A** (threshold 20), **B**/**H** (middle), **C**/**G**, **D**/**F** and **E** (threshold 02). Every crossing needs Tower's clearance.

The AIP sets no preferential runway: the configuration (02 or 20) follows the wind and applies to both runways.

- Tower does not give the takeoff time; the next unit's frequency comes with the takeoff clearance[^ad23].

### DCL

SBRJ has **DCL** (departure clearance via datalink)[^ad18]. On the network, the station code is **`SBRJ`**.

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Station code** | `SBRJ` |
| **Who operates it** | SBRJ_DEL. Without DEL, the position covering clearance top-down (GND, TWR, APP or ACC) |
| **Controller tool** | TopSky DCL window, with your personal Hoppie ACARS code |
| **Pilot tool** | Aircraft ACARS or a Hoppie-compatible client, sending the clearance request to `SBRJ` |

**How it works:**

1. The pilot sends the request (RCD) to `SBRJ`, with the parking stand and ATIS letter.
2. The controller checks the flight plan and replies with the clearance: limit, runway, SID, transponder code, ATIS letter and next frequency.
3. The pilot accepts (`WILCO`/`ACCEPT`). The clearance is then issued and **there is no voice readback**.
4. With the clearance accepted, the pilot calls Ground for start-up or pushback.

!!! tip "Controller setup"
    - Log in to the TopSky DCL with the code `SBRJ` before opening the position.
    - Announce it in the controller information: `DCL AVBL LOGON SBRJ`.
    - A faulty request (invalid plan, wrong SID, no ATIS) goes back as **REVERT TO VOICE**: the pilot calls Clearance on frequency.
    - If the pilot does not accept within a reasonable time, consider the clearance not delivered and call them by voice.

[^ad18]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBRJ 2.18](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).

## :material-map-marker-radius: Areas of responsibility

<figure markdown="span">
  ![Map of SBRJ with both runways and the space between them in yellow, under Tower, and the aprons in blue, under Ground](img/sbrj-responsabilidade.svg){ loading=lazy }
  <figcaption>Yellow strip: Tower (both runways and the space between them). Rest: Ground. The red marks are the auxiliary runway holding points.</figcaption>
</figure>

| Position | Responsible for |
| :--- | :--- |
| **GND** | Aprons 1A, 1B, 2 and the military aprons, and the TWY up to the 02L/20R **holding point** |
| **TWR** | Both runways, the TWY between them and the 02L/20R crossings |

- SBRJ **has no Apron position** (APRON): Ground handles all aprons.
- Because the runways are so close, all holding points are **west of the auxiliary runway**. To reach the main runway, Ground hands the aircraft off to Tower there, and Tower clears the crossing.
- **Arrivals:** the aircraft vacates the main runway, crosses the auxiliary runway with Tower, and only then is handed off to Ground.

## :material-transit-connection-variant: Ground flow

!!! abstract "General rule"
    - **J** runs in front of the aprons, from the terminal (1A) to the military apron; **L** and **M** form a loop by the terminal and **K** links J with threshold 02.
    - **A** leads to thresholds 20; **D** and **E**, to thresholds 02.
    - **B**/**H** (middle), **C**/**G** and **F** cross both runways.
    - **Category C** operates **only via A**; if A is unavailable, everyone may use **A2**[^ad28].

=== "Runways 20L and 20R"

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![Ground flow on runways 20: 20L departures via J and A, crossing 20R; 20L landings vacate via G and cross the auxiliary runway via C; general aviation on 20R via A](img/sbrj-fluxo-20.svg){ loading=lazy }
      <figcaption>Green: departures on the main runway. Dashed orange: auxiliary runway. Blue: landings.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    - **Departures (20L):** **J** northbound and **A**, crossing 20R, to threshold 20L.
    - **Landings (20L):** vacate via **G** (~1000 m) or **F**/**E** at the end, cross the auxiliary runway via **C**/**D** and return along **K** and **J**.
    - **Auxiliary runway (20R):** general aviation via **J** and **A**.

    Example: `AZU4321, taxi to holding point runway 20L via J and A.`

=== "Runways 02R and 02L"

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![Ground flow on runways 02: 02R departures via J, K, D and E, crossing 02L; 02R landings vacate via H and cross the auxiliary runway via B; general aviation on 02L via D](img/sbrj-fluxo-02.svg){ loading=lazy }
      <figcaption>Green: departures on the main runway. Dashed orange: auxiliary runway. Blue: landings.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    - **Departures (02R):** **J**, **K**, **D** and **E**, crossing 02L, to threshold 02R.
    - **Landings (02R):** vacate via **H** (~920 m) or **A** at the end, cross the auxiliary runway via **B** and follow **J**.
    - **Auxiliary runway (02L):** general aviation via **K** and **D**.

    Example: `GLO1234, taxi to holding point runway 02R via J, K, D and E.`

## :material-clipboard-text-outline: Other procedures

- Observe the VAC for joining and leaving the traffic pattern[^ad22].
- VMC climbs to the **south** leave via **Barra** (the Guanabara Bay entrance)[^ad22].
- With IFR approaches to **02R** in progress, VFR departures to REA FOXTROT fly to Icaraí, then Piratininga Lagoon, and enter the REA via Portão Itaipu[^ad22].
- Changing from IFR to VFR after starting the STAR or approach is subject to Rio APP's clearance[^ad22].
- On approach to runways 02, watch out for **Sugarloaf** (1326 ft), 2 NM from threshold 02R on the extended centerline[^ad23].
