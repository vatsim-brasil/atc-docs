## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Aerodrome Regulations

- Military aerodrome. Civil aircraft only operate with prior authorization from COMAR VI.
- Main runway 06L/24R is 3300 x 45 m; runway 06R/24L is 2233 x 22.5 m.
- ILS CAT I on runway 24R only. PAR approaches are also available for runways 06L and 24R.
- The stopways of runways 06L and 24R have arresting barriers operated by the TWR.
- Foreign aircraft, civil or military, may not enter or overfly Sector 1 of the Anápolis TMA or CTR 1 without authorization.
