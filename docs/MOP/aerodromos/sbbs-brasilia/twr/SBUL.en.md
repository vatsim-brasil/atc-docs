## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Aerodrome Regulations

- Runway 04/22 is 2100 x 45 m. ILS on runway 04 only.
- Reference code C aircraft must start the takeoff from the threshold.
- 180° turns on the runway for aircraft with a wingspan of 24.57 m or more only at the thresholds.

### Aprons and Taxiways

- Taxi speed on the aprons is limited to 15 kt.
- Aircraft with an MTOW above 16 t leave the stand with push-back and only start engines on reaching the taxilane. Power-back is prohibited.
- In IMC, push-back on apron 1 and operations on the apron 1 taxiway and the "Norte" access way are prohibited while code 3 or 4 aircraft are landing or taking off.
- General aviation parking only for aircraft with a wingspan of up to 16.5 m.
