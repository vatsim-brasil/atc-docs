## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Takeoffs

- ACFT on the takeoff roll from threshold 14: do not confuse the extension of TWY `B` with the runway.

### Aerodrome Regulations

- The AD may be used regularly by any ACFT compatible with RCD 4C or lower.
- OBS VAC for joining or leaving the traffic circuit.
- The following classes and types of ACFT are restricted:
    - ACFT WO EQPT RDO;
    - GLD;
    - ACFT WO transponder or with a failure of this EQPT;
    - Powered ultralight FLT.
- The following air services are restricted:
    - Dropping of objects or spraying;
    - ACFT towing;
    - Parachute dropping;
    - Aerobatic FLT.
- North hangars access road: width 9.5m, under the responsibility of the ACFT OPR.
- South hangars access road: width 12m, under the responsibility of the ACFT OPR.

### Aprons and Taxiways

- Jet ACFT operating on Apron `2`: departure under own power PRB.
- ACFT on APCH to RWY 32: do not confuse with TWY `J`, to the right of the flight path.
