## :material-routes: Runways

### Runway system

SBGO has **one runway**, 14/32 (2286 × 45 m, asphalt). Two parallels run along it end to end: **A** on the apron 1 side (general aviation and hangars) and **J** on the apron 2 side (passenger terminal).

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-airplane:{ .lg .middle } **Runway `14`**

    ---

    RNP, VOR and NAV APV. Takeoff from the threshold, via F or B.

-   :material-airplane:{ .lg .middle } **Runway `32`**

    ---

    RNP, VOR and NAV APV. Takeoff from the threshold, via T or E.

</div>

!!! info "Runway selection"
    The AIP sets no preferential runway: the runway in use follows the wind.

### Runway data

| Runway | Dimensions | TORA | LDA | Approach | Remarks |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **14** | 2286 × 45 m | 2286 m | 2286 m | Non-precision | RNP, VOR and NAV APV |
| **32** | 2286 × 45 m | 2286 m | 2286 m | Non-precision | RNP, VOR and NAV APV |

- The AD accepts aircraft up to code **4C**[^ad20-1].
- **There is no ILS.**
- 180° turns for aircraft with an MTOW above **40 t** only at the **thresholds**[^ad20-6].
- When taking off from 14, **do not use** the extension of TWY **B** aligned with the runway as a landing and takeoff runway[^ad23].
- On final, **do not mistake** TWY **J** for the runway: it is on the left on final for 14 and on the right on final for 32 (hot spot **HS1**)[^ad23].

[^ad20-1]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBGO 2.20, item 1](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip). Declared distances: AD 2.13.
[^ad20-6]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBGO 2.20, item 6](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).
[^ad23]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBGO 2.23](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip) and ADC chart.

## :material-headset: ATC Units

| Code | Abbr. | Callsign | Frequency | Remarks |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **SBGO_ATIS** | `AGO` | Goiânia Information | **127.675** | |
| **SBGO_DEL** | `DGO` | Goiânia Clearance | **121.050** | Voice clearance |
| **SBGO_GND** | `GGO` | Goiânia Ground | **121.700** | |
| **SBGO_TWR** | `TGO` | Goiânia Tower | **118.700** | |

- SBGO **has no DCL**[^ad18]: clearance is given by voice on Clearance. Without DEL, the position covering clearance top-down (GND, TWR, APP or ACC) takes over.

## :material-map-marker-radius: Areas of responsibility

<figure markdown="span">
  ![SBGO map with the runway strip in yellow, under Tower, and the rest in blue, under Ground](img/sbgo-responsabilidade.svg){ loading=lazy }
  <figcaption>Yellow strip: Tower. Rest: Ground. The dashed line runs through the holding points. White circles: aprons.</figcaption>
</figure>

| Position | Responsible for |
| :--- | :--- |
| **GND** | Aprons 1 and 2, TWY **A**, **J**, **K**, **L** and the links up to the **holding point** |
| **TWR** | Runway 14/32 and the TWY inside the holding points |

- SBGO **has no Apron position** (APRON): Ground handles both aprons.
- The **Hangares 1** and **Hangares 2** taxiways are **outside air traffic control**: aircraft move under the operator's responsibility and call Ground before entering **A**[^ad28].
- **Departures:** Ground hands off to Tower **before the holding point**. **Arrivals:** after vacating the runway, Tower hands off to Ground.

## :material-transit-connection-variant: Ground flow

!!! abstract "General rule"
    - **J** runs along the runway on the terminal side, from **F** (threshold 14) to **T** (threshold 32).
    - **K** links apron 2 to the middle of J; **L** links apron 2 to J next to threshold 32.
    - **A** runs along the runway on the apron 1 side, from **B** (threshold 14) to **E** (threshold 32). **C** and **D** link A to the runway.
    - **G** and **H** are the angled exits between the runway and J: **H** for aircraft landing on 14, **G** for aircraft landing on 32.

=== "Runway 14"

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![Ground flow on runway 14: departures from apron 2 via K and J to F; from apron 1 via A to B; landings vacating via H and continuing via J and L to apron 2](img/sbgo-fluxo-14.svg){ loading=lazy }
      <figcaption>Green: departures from apron 2. Dashed orange: departures from apron 1 (general aviation). Blue: landings.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    - **Departures (14):** from apron 2, **K** and **J** to **F** (threshold 14). From apron 1, **A** to **B**.
    - **Landings (14):** vacate via **H** (~1530 m) and continue via **J** and **L** to apron 2. For apron 1, vacate via **D** (~1930 m).

    Example: `GLO1234, taxi to holding point runway 14 via K, J and F.`

=== "Runway 32"

    <figure markdown="span">
      ![Ground flow on runway 32: departures from apron 2 via L to T; from apron 1 via A to E; landings vacating via G and continuing via J and K to apron 2](img/sbgo-fluxo-32.svg){ loading=lazy }
      <figcaption>Green: departures from apron 2. Dashed orange: departures from apron 1 (general aviation). Blue: landings.</figcaption>
    </figure>

    - **Departures (32):** from apron 2, **L** to **T** (threshold 32). From apron 1, **A** to **E**.
    - **Landings (32):** vacate via **G** (~1700 m) and continue via **J** and **K** to apron 2. For apron 1, vacate via **C** (~1400 m).

    Example: `AZU4321, taxi to holding point runway 32 via L and T.`

### Runway exits

| Runway | Exits |
| :---: | :--- |
| **14** | **C** (~880 m), **H** (~1530 m), **D** (~1930 m) or **T**/**E** (end) |
| **32** | **D** (~350 m), **C** (~1400 m), **G** (~1700 m) or **F**/**B** (end) |

<small>Approximate distance from the threshold to the exit, measured on the diagram. The SBGO AIP publishes no exits.</small>

### Taxi restrictions

| Restriction | Where |
| :--- | :--- |
| **180° turns** | MTOW above 40 t only at the thresholds[^ad20-6] |
| **Pushback required** | Jets on scheduled flights at apron 2: leaving under own power is prohibited[^ad28] |
| **Pushback before engine start** | Apron 1, stands 3, 4 and 5 (in front of the TWR): code B jets and code C aircraft[^ad28] |
| **Outside ATC** | Hangares 1 (11 m wide) and Hangares 2 (12 m) taxiways[^ad28] |

[^ad28]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBGO 2.8](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip).

## :material-clipboard-text-outline: Other procedures

- Helicopters: landings and takeoffs directly at the Hangares 1 sector (north hangars) are prohibited. They land and take off on apron 1 or in the area next to the engine test sector and are towed to the hangar[^ad20-1].
- Aerobatic flights, parachute drops, dropping objects or spraying, and aircraft towing are prohibited[^ad20-1].

[^ad17]: [AIP Brasil, AD 2 SBGO 2.17](https://aisweb.decea.mil.br/?i=publicacoes&p=aip) and IAC charts.
