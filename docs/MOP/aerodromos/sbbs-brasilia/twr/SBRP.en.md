## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Aerodrome Regulations

- The Ribeirão CTR is controlled by Academia Approach (`SBXQ_APP`).
- Runway 18/36 is 2100 x 45 m. THR 18 is displaced 200 m and THR 36 is displaced 100 m.
- The last 90 m at each end are closed for landing and rejected takeoff because there is no RESA. Declared distances: RWY 18 TORA 2000 m / LDA 1810 m; RWY 36 TORA 1900 m / LDA 1910 m.
- There is no ILS. The approach procedures are RNP 18 and RNP 36.

### Aprons and Taxiways

- Aircraft the size of a FOKKER 100 or larger may only make 180° turns at the runway thresholds.

### Traffic Pattern

- Traffic pattern entry is made through the compulsory entry points:
    - East sector: Morro Piripau (21 07 43S / 047 40 47W);
    - West sector: USP (21 09 25S / 047 51 15W).
- Aircraft on training flights near the aerodrome make touch-and-goes at the aerodrome.
