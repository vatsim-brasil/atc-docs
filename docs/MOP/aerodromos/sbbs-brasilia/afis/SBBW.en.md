## :material-airplane-takeoff: Operations

### Aerodrome Regulations

- PRB OPS at the AD: boarding and disembarking of PAX by ACFT with engines and propellers running.
- PRB OPS of ATR-72 ACFT, compatible with RCD 3C, in IMC.
- The AD may be used regularly by any ACFT compatible with RCD 2C or lower.

### Runways

- RWY 16/34: ACFT above 10 tonnes shall only perform 180-degree turns in the turning areas.
