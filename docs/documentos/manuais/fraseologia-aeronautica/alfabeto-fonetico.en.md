#

When it is necessary to spell out proper names, service abbreviations and words of doubtful pronunciation in radiotelephony, the following phonetic alphabet is used:

!!! note "Note on the pronunciation of the phonetic alphabet"
    The pronunciation of the phonetic alphabet is the same for all languages.

| B | Bravo | 'braːvo | BRA <u>VOU</u> |
| C | Charlie | 'tʃɑːli or 'ʃɑːli | <u>CHAR</u> LI |
| D | Delta | 'deltɑ | <u>DEL</u> TA |
| E | Echo | 'eko | <u>E</u> COU |

| G | Golf | gʌlf | <u>GOLF</u> |

| L | Lima | 'liːmɑ | <u>LI</u> MA |
