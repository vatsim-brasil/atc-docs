## Overview

Welcome to the Aeronautical Phraseology Manual, a practical and objective guide developed to assist aviation professionals and enthusiasts in standardised radiotelephony communication.
This manual was written with a focus on clarity and objectivity, presenting the standards established by Brazilian and international aeronautical phraseology regulations.

## About this Manual

After reading this manual, you will notice that the phraseology has become leaner and clearer, and consequently more objective.

This manual was created in accordance with the topics listed in **MCA 100-16** [^1] and **MCA 100-21** [^2], which are the official DECEA documents covering aeronautical phraseology in Portuguese, English and Spanish.

Reading both manuals regularly is extremely important.

[^1]: [**MCA 100-16 - Fraseologia de Tráfego Aéreo (Air Traffic Phraseology)**](https://publicacoes.decea.mil.br/publicacao/MCA-100-16): establishes the air traffic phraseology standards, complementing the provisions of **ICA-100-12**, "Regras do Ar" (Rules of the Air).
[^2]: [**MCA 100-21 - Fraseologia de Tráfego Aéreo em Espanhol (Air Traffic Phraseology in Spanish)**](https://publicacoes.decea.mil.br/publicacao/MCA-100-21): establishes the air traffic phraseology standards in Spanish, complementing the provisions of **ICA-100-12**, "Regras do Ar" (Rules of the Air).

## Scope

The phraseology contained here covers only the basics of an instrument flight without adverse situations; should such situations occur, the specific regulations must be consulted.
