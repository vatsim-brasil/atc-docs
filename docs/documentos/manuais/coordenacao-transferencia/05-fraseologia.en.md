## How to use this page

The page has two parts. The first gives the **instructions to the pilot** that accompany a transfer, from Chapter IV of MCA 100-16. The second gives the **messages between units**, from Annex II of the same manual[^1]. Pilot transmissions are marked with `*`. The callsigns, frequencies and names are those used in the examples of the manual. Replace them with those of your position.

The messages between units appear in MCA 100-16 in Portuguese only. The English column is a rendering for reference, in standard ICAO coordination style.

## Messages between units

### General rules

- When answering, give the name of the unit and "go ahead" ("prossiga") or "stand by" ("aguarde"). On a direct line, the name may be omitted[^3].
- When there is no readback, acknowledge with "roger" ("recebido") or "understood" ("entendido")[^4].
- **Prepare the message before calling** and check that you are talking to the right addressee[^5].
- Use the phonetic alphabet to spell proper names, abbreviations and words whose pronunciation is doubtful[^6].
