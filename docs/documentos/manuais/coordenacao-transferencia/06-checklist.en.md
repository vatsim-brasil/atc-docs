# Quick Checklist

## When opening the position

- [ ] I know who is online **above, below and beside** my position
- [ ] I know which positions I will cover **top-down** (TMA diagram in the [Terminals](../../../MOP/terminais/index.en.md) section)
- [ ] I know the **transfer points** and the restrictions in the operations manual
- [ ] I agreed with whoever was covering on when to take over, and received the traffic through the tag
- [ ] I asked about holds, restrictions, emergencies and pending coordination
- [ ] I announced on the `ATC` channel that I opened the position

## Before each transfer

- [ ] The tag shows what I cleared: level, heading, speed and route
- [ ] What **does not fit on the tag** was passed in the chat
- [ ] The transfer started **sufficiently in advance** for the accepting controller to analyse it
- [ ] The aircraft is separated from other traffic at the transfer point
- [ ] If the next unit has no surveillance, **procedural** separation is already established

## During the transfer

- [ ] I transferred with `F4` and waited for **acceptance**
- [ ] Only after acceptance did I tell the pilot to **change frequency**
- [ ] No acceptance: I coordinated in the chat and kept the aircraft away from the boundary
- [ ] If the accepting controller requested a change, I applied it before handing over the traffic

## When receiving a transfer

- [ ] I accepted with `F3` or requested the necessary change
- [ ] I did not change the clearance **before the boundary** without the transferring controller's consent
- [ ] I confirmed contact and identification

## Pocket times

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Frequency change with ATS surveillance | Right **after acceptance** |
| Frequency change with procedural separation | **5 minutes** before the boundary |
| Arrival estimate from ACC to APP | At least **15 minutes** before |
| Expected landing time from APP to TWR | At least **15 minutes** before |
| Revision of the expected approach time | Variation of **5 minutes** or more |
| Revision of the time over the holding point | Difference of **3 minutes** or more |

## When closing the position

- [ ] I told my neighbours a few minutes in advance
- [ ] I transferred **each aircraft** to whoever will cover my airspace, or terminated the service and told the pilot where to call
- [ ] No tag is still assumed by me
- [ ] I announced on the `ATC` channel that I closed the position
