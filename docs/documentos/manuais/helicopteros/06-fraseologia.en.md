# Phraseology

## :material-numeric-0-box: How to use this page

The MCA 100-16 has no section of its own for helicopters[^2]. The helicopter-specific phraseology is in chapter 7 of the ICA 100-4[^1], and most of the examples come from it. Pilot transmissions appear in the green bubbles, on the left; controller transmissions in the blue bubbles, on the right. Replace the callsigns and names with those of your position.

Three words change the kind of movement, and the pilot needs to hear the right one (ICA 100-4, item 7.1)[^1]:

| Word | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| **Taxi** | Movement on the ground, along the taxiways. Preferred for wheeled helicopters |
| **Air-taxi** | Movement in the air, below 20 kt and in ground effect |
| **Air-transit** | Movement in the air, along an air-transit route, above 20 kt and below 100 ft |

## :material-numeric-1-box: Taxiing in the air

=== ":flag_br: Portuguese"

    </div>
    <div class="comms-atc">

    </div>

    </div>

    </div>

## :material-numeric-6-box: Report on a helicopter route

=== ":flag_br: Portuguese"
    <div class="comms-pilot">

    </div>
    <div class="comms-atc">
      **PR HEL**, autorizado cruzamento da CTR via REH ALFA, mantenha 1.500 pés, reporte vertical do ESTÁDIO.
    </div>
    <div class="comms-pilot">

    </div>

=== ":flag_gb: English"
    <div class="comms-pilot">
      Control, **PR HEL**, helicopter route ALFA, overhead the BRIDGE, 1,500 feet, northbound, to cross the control zone.
    </div>
    <div class="comms-atc">
      **PR HEL**, cleared to cross the control zone via helicopter route ALFA, maintain 1,500 feet, report overhead the STADIUM.
    </div>
    <div class="comms-pilot">

The route and point names are fictitious. Use those in the area's AIC.

## :material-numeric-7-box: Priority traffic

=== ":flag_br: Portuguese"
    <div class="comms-atc">
      **PT KLN**, Rádio Altamira, tráfego do PT MLO iniciou táxi realizando aeromédico, com prioridade para decolagem.
    </div>
    <div class="comms-pilot">
      **PT KLN**, ciente, cederá passagem ao aeromédico.
    </div>
    <div class="comms-atc">
      **PT OLC**, Rádio Altamira, tráfego é o helicóptero Guardião 08, a 500 pés, setor ECHO, realizando operação policial.
    </div>
    <div class="comms-pilot">
      **PT OLC**, ciente, atento ao tráfego.
    </div>

=== ":flag_gb: English"
    <div class="comms-atc">
      **PT KLN**, Altamira Radio, PT MLO traffic started taxiing for aeromedical service, with priority for take-off.
    </div>
    <div class="comms-pilot">
      **PT KLN**, roger, will give way to the aeromedical service.
    </div>
    <div class="comms-atc">
      **PT OLC**, Altamira Radio, traffic is the helicopter Guardião 08, at 500 feet, sector ECHO, performing police operation.
    </div>
    <div class="comms-pilot">
      **PT OLC**, roger and alert to traffic.
    </div>

Source: MCA 100-16, Art. 158, III, situações 1 e 3. Position: AFIS.

The MCA examples are for the RADIO, but the idea holds for the tower and the approach: say what the operation is and what priority it has, and the other pilot gives way.

## Phrases to avoid

| Avoid | Why | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Cleared to land" at a point the tower cannot see | The tower cannot vouch for the site | "Landing will be at your own risk [site], report on the ground" |
| "Taxi" to a skid-equipped helicopter | It cannot roll on the ground | "Air-taxi cleared via..." |
| "Cleared for take-off" without saying from where | The helicopter can depart from several points | "Cleared for take-off from present position" (or from the point) |
| "Contact..." while the helicopter is hovering | The single pilot has both hands busy | Transfer before it lifts, or relay |
