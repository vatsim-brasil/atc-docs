# Ground Control (GND)

## The role of GND

With a helicopter, GND does what it does with any aircraft: it clears movement on the manoeuvring area and keeps the helicopter away from other aircraft, vehicles and people. The difference is that there are three ways to move (ground taxi, air-taxi and air-transit) and that the helicopter often does not need GND at all, because it can take off from where it is.

## The premises

1. **Taxi is not mandatory.** Under the ICA 100-4, helicopter taxi "is not normally mandatory" (item 2.3.1)[^1]. If it can take off from the apron or helipad, the next step is with the tower, not with you. But if it moves on the manoeuvring area, it needs a clearance, like any aircraft (Art. 79 of the ICA 100-12)[^3].
2. **Prefer ground taxi.** For wheeled helicopters, ground taxi uses less fuel and minimises turbulence. Air-taxi may be needed, but blows harder (item 5.1.2.1)[^1].
3. **Air-taxi is along the taxiways.** Below 20 kt and in ground effect, a helicopter "may be cleared to air-taxi along the taxiways" (item 5.1.2.2)[^1].
4. **Air-transit is along air-transit routes.** Above 20 kt and below 100 ft, only where such routes exist (item 5.1.2.3)[^1].
5. **Keep helicopters apart and away from light aircraft.** Avoid instructions that make helicopters taxi close to each other, and consider the effect of turbulence on light aircraft arriving or departing (item 5.1.2.4)[^1].
6. **The helicopter should not overfly anyone.** In air-transit it avoids overflying aircraft, vehicles and people, and avoids air-taxiing where the downwash could damage aircraft or raise dust (items 2.3.3 and 2.3.4)[^1].

## The tasks

### ATC clearance

An IFR helicopter, or a VFR one with a flight plan departing controlled airspace, receives an ATC clearance like any other flight. Where no DEL is online, whoever covers that position issues it.

When clearing a VFR flight, confirm the **destination** and, if the terminal area has published REH, by which **REH or gate** the helicopter intends to leave. That helps the tower and APP know where it is going.

### Choosing the kind of movement

Ask yourself, in this order:

| Question | If yes |
| --- | --- |
| Can the helicopter take off from where it is, without crossing anything? | No taxi needed. Transfer to the tower, which clears the take-off from present position |
| Does it have wheels, and is the way to the departure point along taxiways? | Clear a ground **taxi**, like an aeroplane |
| Does it have skids, or has the pilot asked, and are there no light aircraft on the way? | Clear an **air-taxi** along the taxiway |
| Is there a published air-transit route, and is the destination far away? | Clear an **air-transit** along the route |

If the pilot does not say how they want to move, ask. On the network, hardly any helicopter pilot expects a long ground taxi.

### Runway crossings

A helicopter crossing a runway, rolling or in the air, needs a clearance for it, like any aircraft. If GND does not control the runway, coordinate with the tower or transfer the helicopter to it before the crossing.

### Frequency changes

Avoid telling a **single-pilot** helicopter that is hovering or air-taxiing to change frequency. At that moment both hands and both feet are busy. When possible, relay the next unit's instructions until it can change (item 5.1.2.5)[^1].

!!! note "Good practice"
    Transfer the helicopter to the tower **before** it lifts, while still on the ground. If it is already airborne, coordinate with the tower and let it talk to the helicopter on your frequency, or relay the take-off clearance.

## On the network

GND is an **S1** position under the GCAP, with a maximum range of 20 NM[^2]. In the sector package, helipads inside the aerodrome show up as aprons or stands. When a pilot asks to depart from a "helipad" you cannot find on the diagram, ask where it is and, if it is outside the manoeuvring area, leave the take-off to the tower.

With the tower offline, GND does **not** cover the tower: top-down runs from the top, and the tower is covered by APP or the centre (GCAP 4.6)[^2]. GND clears the movement up to the holding point and transfers to whoever is above.
