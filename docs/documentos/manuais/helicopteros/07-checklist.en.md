# Quick Checklist

## All ratings

- [ ] I know the difference between **taxi**, **air-taxi** and **air-transit**, and use the right word
- [ ] I keep hovering or air-taxiing helicopters **clear of light aircraft**
- [ ] I do not change the frequency of a single-pilot helicopter while it hovers
- [ ] I know that police, SAR and firefighting operations on the network are for VSOA flights only
- [ ] I know no pilot may demand priority, and that I may ask for an emergency to end

## Ground (S1)

- [ ] I asked how the helicopter wants to move, if it did not say
- [ ] If it can take off from where it is, I transferred it to the tower **before** it lifted
- [ ] I preferred ground taxi for wheeled helicopters near light aircraft
- [ ] Air-taxi is along the taxiways; air-transit only along a published route
- [ ] No runway crossing without coordinating with the tower

## Tower (S2)

- [ ] I said **where from** the helicopter is cleared to take off or land
- [ ] Point out of sight or outside the manoeuvring area: **at your own risk**, report on the ground
- [ ] The helicopter's path does not cross the final or take-off path of any runway in use
- [ ] Away from the runway, the helicopter ahead has left the landing and take-off area
- [ ] Simultaneous landings or take-offs only with points **60 m** or more apart and no conflicting paths
- [ ] Wake turbulence applied to a LIGHT helicopter behind a MEDIUM or HEAVY aeroplane
- [ ] I used a stationary hold over a visual point instead of long orbits

## Approach (S3)

- [ ] I opened the terminal page and know where the REH run, the altitudes by direction and the transfer points
- [ ] I know each segment's class: in C and D I control; in G I only inform
- [ ] Entry and crossing of a final or IFR path only with a clearance
- [ ] I gave traffic information between helicopters flying opposite ways and between helicopters and aeroplanes near the finals
- [ ] I coordinated with the tower before transferring at the published point
- [ ] Pilot without the chart: I pointed them to the terminal page or gave a direct route to a visual point, away from the finals

## Pocket reference

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Air-taxi | Below 20 kt, in ground effect |
| Air-transit | Above 20 kt, below 100 ft |
| Rotor downwash | About 3 rotor diameters |
| Helicopter VFR minima at the aerodrome | 600 ft ceiling, 1,500 m visibility |
| VFR visibility in controlled airspace | 3,000 m |
| Simultaneous landings | 60 m between the points and between the helicopters |
| Circuit without a tower | 500 ft, without crossing the finals |
| Minimum height over built-up areas | 500 ft above the highest obstacle within 600 m |
| REH | Corridor of about 1 NM; references on the right |
| No ATC online | Designated frequency or 122.800 |
| Take-off priority | Air ambulance ahead of SAR and public safety |
| Police, SAR and firefighting on the network | Approved VSOA only (CoC A13(b)) |
