# Glossary

## Clouds (amount)

- **FEW**: few
- **SCT**: scattered
- **BKN**: broken (defines ceiling)
- **OVC**: overcast (defines ceiling)

## Clouds (type)

- **CB**: cumulonimbus (thunderstorm/severe weather)
- **TCU**: towering cumulus (convective potential)

## Common phenomena

- **RA**: rain
- **DZ**: drizzle
- **TS**: thunderstorm
- **BR**: mist
- **FG**: fog
- **HZ**: haze
- **Prefixes**: `-` light, no sign moderate, `+` heavy

## Wind

- **VRB**: variable direction
- **G**: gusts
- **xxxVyyy**: directional variation

## Visibility

- **9999**: 10 km or more (in the convention used in the messages)

## Pressure

- **Q1016**: QNH 1016 hPa

    For extensive lists of aeronautical abbreviations, consult the eAIP (GEN) in the official references.
