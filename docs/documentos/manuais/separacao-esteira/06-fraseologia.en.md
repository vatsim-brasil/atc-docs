# Phraseology

## How to use this page

The examples below come from MCA 100-16. Pilot transmissions are marked with `*`. Replace the callsigns, levels and points with those of your traffic, without changing the structure of the phrase.

## RVSM status

| Portuguese | English |
| --- | --- |
| PT ART, confirme aprovação RVSM? | PT ART, confirm RVSM approved? |
| \*PT ART, afirmo RVSM. | \*PT ART, affirm RVSM. |
| \*PT ART, negativo RVSM. | \*PT ART, negative RVSM. |
| PT ART, negativo ingresso no espaço aéreo RVSM, mantenha FL 280. | PT ART, unable clearance into RVSM airspace, maintain flight level 280. |
| PT ART, negativo autorização para voo no espaço aéreo RVSM, mantenha FL 280. Autorização de ingresso no espaço aéreo RVSM prevista para (hora/fixo). | PT ART, unable clearance to fly into RVSM airspace, maintain flight level 280. Expect clearance to enter RVSM airspace at (time/fix). |
| \*PT ART, negativo RVSM devido a turbulência. | \*PT ART, unable RVSM due turbulence. |
| \*PT ART, negativo RVSM devido a equipamento. | \*PT ART, unable RVSM due equipment. |
| PT ART, reporte quando pronto para reassumir RVSM. | PT ART, report when able to resume RVSM. |
| \*PT ART, pronto para reassumir RVSM. | \*PT ART, ready to resume RVSM. |

Source: MCA 100-16[^1].

## On the runway

| Portuguese | English |
| --- | --- |
| TAM 3330, condições de partida imediata? | TAM 3330, are you ready for immediate departure? |
| TAM 3203, pista 09 esquerda, autorizado decolagem imediata, vento 095 graus, 10 kt. | TAM 3203, runway 09 left, cleared for immediate take-off, wind 095 degrees, 10 kt. |
| ONE 6340, pista 15, decole imediatamente ou livre a pista. | ONE 6340, runway 15, take-off immediately or vacate the runway. |
| GLO 1495, negativo, alinhe e mantenha devido a aeronave cruzando a pista. | GLO 1495, negative, line up and wait due aircraft crossing the runway. |
| GLO 1256, reporte avistando o Airbus na final. | GLO 1256, report the Airbus on final in sight. |
| \*GLO 1256, Airbus avistado. | \*GLO 1256, Airbus in sight. |
| GLO 1256, após o Airbus na final, alinhar e manter atrás do Airbus. | GLO 1256, behind Airbus on final, line up and wait behind. |
| TAM 3320, no solo aos 10, taxie via primeira interseção à direita, reporte pista livre. | TAM 3320, on the ground at 10, taxi via first intersection to the right, report runway vacated. |

Source: MCA 100-16[^6].

!!! note "Waiting for wake turbulence"
    MCA 100-16 has no specific phrase for holding a departure for wake turbulence. Use the "negative, line up and wait due" structure with the reason, and say how long is left. For example: **"GLO 1495, line up and wait, wake turbulence from the Boeing 777, departure in 1 minute."**
