# Decision: Straight-in final vs Joining the Downwind leg (TWR) + Full script (3 aircraft)

## Decision table — when to allow a Straight-in Final

Use this table as a practical rule **when there is no VAC** or when there is no local procedure that explicitly addresses the "straight-in".

!!! note "Operational principle"
    **Predictability > convenience**.  
    If the decision increases the risk of conflict with the circuit, **do not authorise a straight-in final**.

### Quick matrix

| Condition | Allow straight-in final? | Recommended action (TWR) | Notes |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Empty circuit / 1 well-spaced traffic | ✅ Yes | "straight-in approved, report final" | keep awareness of wind/runway occupancy |
| There is traffic established in the circuit (downwind/base) | ⚠️ It depends | prefer "join downwind leg" | straight-in only with clear separation |
| Traffic on base/short final | ❌ No | "remain outside / orbit / join downwind leg" | avoids convergence and "cut-in" |
| Performance mix (jet behind piston) | ❌ Prefer not | if authorised, increase spacing | reordering is usually better |
| Marginal visibility / low ceiling (degraded VMC) | ❌ No | standardise joining the downwind leg and reports | reduces surprises and loss of SA* |
| Pilot without traffic in sight (when "follow" would be needed) | ❌ No | resequence with extension/orbit | without visual contact, do not "fit it in" by eye |
| Aerodrome with likely obstacles/sensitive area | ❌ No | join downwind leg as per standard | without a VAC, do not assume safe routes |
| Runway may change (variable wind) | ❌ No | remain outside until the runway is stable | avoids traffic coming "the wrong way" |

!!! tip "SA = Situational Awareness"
    **SA** stands for **Situational Awareness** (in Portuguese, **Consciência Situacional**).

### Implementation rules (short and objective)

- Straight-in final only when you can guarantee:

  1) **separation** (time/lateral) from the circuit;  
  2) **zero conflict** with base/final;  
  3) **predictability** (no multiple simultaneous joinings).
- If you authorise a straight-in final at an aerodrome with traffic in the circuit:
  - state the **sequence** ("number 2 after…"),
  - require a **visual report** (if applicable),
  - use **extend downwind leg** or an **orbit** to create spacing.

## Full script — sequence of 3 aircraft (PT/EN)

### Scenario

- Runway in use: **RWY 16**
- Circuit: **left-hand**
- Aircraft:
  - **PT-ABC** (piston) — standard circuit 1000 ft AAL
  - **PR-JET** (jet) — high circuit 1500 ft AAL
  - **PR-HLX** (helicopter) — low circuit 500 ft AAL, dedicated route to the south

Objective: show a realistic radio sequence with **organisation, separation and mitigation of mixed traffic**.

!!! info "Focus of this script"
    What matters here is the **controller's decision**: in which order to place the aircraft and how to create spacing. The transmissions are written in abbreviated form for quick reading.

    On frequency, the unit is always **TORRE followed by the location name**, the registration is read letter by letter and numbers digit by digit. The complete phraseology, in Portuguese, English and Spanish, is in the [VFR Phraseology Manual](../fraseologia-voo-visual/index.en.md).

---

### 1) Piston joining and establishing in the circuit

=== ":flag_br: Portuguese"

    - **PILOTO**: "TORRE, PT-ABC, 5 milhas ao norte, 1500 pés, para pouso."
    - **TWR**: "PT-ABC, autorizado para o circuito de tráfego, pista 16, circuito à esquerda, vento ___, QNH ___, reporte perna do vento."
    - **PILOTO**: "Autorizado para o circuito de tráfego, pista 16, circuito à esquerda, QNH ___, reportará perna do vento, PT-ABC."

=== ":flag_gb: English"

    - **PILOT**: "TOWER, PT-ABC, 5 miles north, 1500 feet, inbound to land."
    - **TWR**: "PT-ABC, cleared to traffic pattern, runway 16, left-hand circuit, wind ___, QNH ___, report downwind leg."
    - **PILOT**: "Cleared to traffic pattern, runway 16, left-hand circuit, QNH ___, will report downwind leg, PT-ABC."

---

### 2) Jet approaching — keep separated from the piston

=== ":flag_br: Portuguese"

    - **PILOTO**: "TORRE, PR-JET, 8 milhas a leste, 2500 pés, para pouso."
    - **TWR**: "PR-JET, pista 16, circuito à esquerda, mantenha 1500 pés <abbr title="Above Aerodrome Level/Altitude Acima do Aeródromo">AAL</abbr>, ingresse na perna do vento, reporte na perna base."
    - **PILOTO**: "Circuito à esquerda, 1500 pés <abbr title="Above Aerodrome Level/Altitude Acima do Aeródromo">AAL</abbr>, reportarei na perna base, PR-JET."

=== ":flag_gb: English"

    - **PILOT**: "TOWER, PR-JET, 8 miles east, 2500 feet, inbound to land."
    - **TWR**: "PR-JET, runway 16, left-hand circuit, maintain 1500 feet <abbr title="Above Aerodrome Level">AAL</abbr>, join downwind leg 2 miles, report downwind leg."
    - **PILOT**: "Left-hand circuit, maintaining 1500 feet <abbr title="Above Aerodrome Level">AAL</abbr>, joining downwind leg, will report downwind, PR-JET."

---

### 3) Helicopter — low circuit/dedicated route without crossing final

=== ":flag_br: Portuguese"

    - **PILOTO**: "TORRE, PR-HLX, pátio sul, para decolagem e circuito."
    - **TWR**: "PR-HLX, autorizado decolagem, mantenha circuito baixo 500 pés <abbr title="Above Aerodrome Level/Altitude Acima do Aeródromo">AAL</abbr> ao sul, não cruze a final, reporte base."
    - **PILOTO**: "Autorizado decolagem, 500 pés <abbr title="Above Aerodrome Level/Altitude Acima do Aeródromo">AAL</abbr> ao sul, sem cruzar final, reportarei base, PR-HLX."

=== ":flag_gb: English"

    - **PILOT**: "TOWER, PR-HLX, south apron, ready for departure and circuits."
    - **TWR**: "PR-HLX, cleared for takeoff, maintain low circuit 500 feet <abbr title="Above Aerodrome Level">AAL</abbr> south of the aerodrome, do not cross final, report base."
    - **PILOT**: "Cleared for takeoff, 500 feet <abbr title="Above Aerodrome Level">AAL</abbr> south, will not cross final, will report base, PR-HLX."

---

### 4) Sequencing the piston traffic for landing

=== ":flag_br: Portuguese"

    - **PILOTO**: "PT-ABC, na perna do vento."
    - **TWR**: "PT-ABC, número 1, reporte na perna base."
    - **PILOTO**: "Número 1, reportarei base, PT-ABC."
    - **PILOTO**: "PT-ABC, na perna base."
    - **TWR**: "PT-ABC, pista 16, pouso autorizado, vento ___."
    - **PILOTO**: "Pouso autorizado, pista 16, PT-ABC."

=== ":flag_gb: English"

    - **PILOT**: "PT-ABC, downwind leg."
    - **TWR**: "PT-ABC, number 1, report base leg."
    - **PILOT**: "Number 1, will report base leg, PT-ABC."
    - **PILOT**: "PT-ABC, on base leg."
    - **TWR**: "PT-ABC, runway 16, cleared to land, wind ___."
    - **PILOT**: "Runway 16, cleared to land, PT-ABC."

---

### 5) Managing the jet to avoid closing up on the piston (spacing)

=== ":flag_br: Portuguese"

    - **PILOTO**: "PR-JET, na perna do vento."
    - **TWR**: "PR-JET, número 2 após PT-ABC, mantenha tráfego à vista, alongue perna do vento, aguarde autorização para ingresso na perna base."
    - **PILOTO**: "Número 2 após PT-ABC, tráfego à vista, vai alongar a perna do vento, aguardará autorização para a perna base, PR-JET."
    - **TWR** (quando aplicável): "PR-JET, gire base, reporte na final pista 16."
    - **PILOTO**: "Gira base, reportará na final pista 16, PR-JET."
    - **PILOTO**: "PR-JET, na final pista 16."
    - **TWR**: "PR-JET, pista 16, pouso autorizado, vento ___."
    - **PILOTO**: "Pouso autorizado, pista 16, PR-JET."

=== ":flag_gb: English"

    - **PILOT**: "PR-JET, downwind leg."
    - **TWR**: "PR-JET, number 2 after PT-ABC, report traffic in sight and follow, extend downwind leg, standby for further instructions for turning base."
    - **PILOT**: "Number 2 after PT-ABC, traffic in sight, extending downwind, waiting for base clearance, PR-JET."
    - **TWR** (when appropriate): "PR-JET, turn base and report on final runway 16."
    - **PILOT**: "Turning base and will report on final runway 16, PR-JET."
    - **PILOT**: "PR-JET on final runway 16"
    - **TWR**: "PR-JET, runway 16, cleared to land, wind ___."
    - **PILOT**: "Runway 16, cleared to land, PR-JET."

---

### 6) Helicopter integrating without crossing final

=== ":flag_br: Portuguese"

    - **PILOTO**: "PR-HLX, girando base (circuito baixo sul)."
    - **TWR**: "PR-HLX, autorizado aproximação/POUSO (conforme área designada), mantenha separado do eixo da pista, reporte no solo."
    - **PILOTO**: "Autorizado, manterei separado do eixo, reportarei no solo, PR-HLX."

=== ":flag_gb: English"

    - **PILOT**: "PR-HLX, base leg (low circuit south)."
    - **TWR**: "PR-HLX, cleared approach/landing (as assigned), remain clear of runway centerline, report on the ground."
    - **PILOT**: "Cleared, will remain clear of centerline, will report on the ground, PR-HLX."

!!! tip "Note"
    If you prefer to keep the helicopter using the "runway" (touch-and-go), rewrite it to integrate with the fixed-wing traffic only when the circuit is light.

---

## Final notes

This script demonstrates:

- **Altitude separation** between the piston (1000 ft), the jet (1500 ft) and the helicopter (500 ft)
- **Downwind extension** to create adequate spacing and avoid "closing up" on the traffic ahead
- **Dedicated helicopter route** keeping it off the fixed-wing traffic axis and without crossing final
- **Explicit sequencing** ("number 1", "number 2 after...") for clarity
- **Consistent reports** supporting situational awareness
- **Standardised phraseology** in PT/EN

!!! tip "Practical application"
    Use this script as a reference for training and standardising communications in circuit operations with multiple aircraft of different categories, especially when separation between aircraft of different performance needs to be managed.
