# Sequencing and safety (TWR)

## The TWR's objective in the circuit

Maintain a continuous flow with minimum complexity:

- separate by **time**, **altitude**, **lateral** means or **procedure**,
- reduce the mix of performance,
- maintain situational awareness.

## Controller tools (VFR circuit)

| Tool | Typical use | Note |
|---|---|---|
| "Follow traffic" | fit into the sequence | require "traffic in sight" |
| "Extend downwind" | increase spacing | reduce conflict on base/final |
| "Orbit" | hold without leaving the area | define side/altitude when necessary |
| "Remain outside the circuit" | saturation | define point + report |
| "Low approach / touch-and-go" | training | control runway occupancy |

## Visual separation: how to operate safely

- If you issue "**follow traffic**", the pilot must:
  - confirm **traffic in sight**,
  - maintain separation,
  - follow the instructed profile.
- If the pilot **does not have the traffic in sight**, the TWR must:
  - resequence (extend/orbit),
  - or apply separation by instruction and clear positioning.

## Go-around

Standard:

- treat it as a normal, expected manoeuvre.
- give a clear instruction: **heading**, **altitude**, **integration** (e.g. "climb to 1000 ft <abbr title="Above Aerodrome Level">AAL</abbr>, join downwind").

## Situations that require immediate action

| Situation | Recommended action |
|---|---|
| converging traffic on base | "extend downwind" / "orbit" / "do not cut base" |
| final occupied + short final | instruct a go-around or "continue" with caution and separation |
| mix of jet/piston aircraft | separate by altitude or reorder with extension |
| helicopter crossing final | hold and cross only with final clear |
