# Standards by category (altitudes, limits and restrictions)

When **there is no VAC/publication**, use conservative standards to reduce the mix of performance in the circuit.

## Altitude reference

Use **AAL** (Above Aerodrome Level), that is, altitude **above the aerodrome elevation**.

## Recommended altitudes (no publication)

| Category | Circuit altitude (AAL) | Operational notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Piston (SEP) | **1000 ft AAL** | common standard for light traffic |
| Jet / high performance | **1500 ft AAL** | reduces conflict with slow traffic; better energy and separation |
| Helicopters | **500 ft AAL** (or "low circuit") | prefer dedicated routes/areas when possible |

!!! note "Mixed traffic separation"
    If the traffic is mixed and there are no local procedures, the TWR must **separate by altitude and/or by the way it sequences**, avoiding "fitting a jet behind a piston" on short final.

## Lateral limits (circuit distance)

| Category | Downwind (lateral distance) | Comment |
|---|---|---|
| Piston (SEP) | **0.8 to 1.5 NM** | keeping the circuit compact reduces occupancy time |
| Jet / high performance | **1.5 to 3.0 NM** | more energy, larger turn radius |
| Helicopters | **0.3 to 0.8 NM** | keep clear of the fixed-wing traffic axis, when applicable |

## Recommended safety restrictions (no VAC)

- Avoid "**cutting the base**" when there is traffic on downwind/base.
- Prohibit "**straight-in**" if it would conflict with traffic in the circuit (unless the TWR can integrate it with separation).
- Helicopters: prefer a **dedicated route** (e.g. "via the apron/taxi line", "keeping to the edge of the aerodrome") so as not to cross the fixed-wing final.

## Helicopter integration (practical model)

When there is no published procedure:

- authorise helicopters to **operate below the circuit** and, when possible, **clear of the approach axis**.
- if they need to cross final, do so with **explicit coordination** and **with no traffic on short final**.

!!! tip "Local procedures"
    At aerodromes with heavy operations, the ideal is to create/use a published local procedure. Without one, standardise as much as possible through ATC instructions.
