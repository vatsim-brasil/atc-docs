#

## Overview

Welcome to the Traffic Circuit Manual, a practical and objective guide developed to help aviation professionals and enthusiasts understand and apply traffic circuit procedures at aerodromes.

This manual summarises the operation of the **standard (rectangular) traffic circuit** at the **TWR** position, focusing on **VFR traffic** when **there is no VAC chart published** for the aerodrome (or when the VAC is not available at the time).

## About this Manual

This manual was created in accordance with the topics related to standard phraseology in **MCA 100-16** [^1] and other official DECEA documents dealing with traffic circuit procedures and aerodrome operations.

It is extremely important to read the official documents regularly and consult the primary sources (AIP, aeronautical charts and DECEA publications).

[^1]: [**MCA 100-16 - Fraseologia de Tráfego Aéreo (Air Traffic Phraseology)**](https://publicacoes.decea.mil.br/publicacao/MCA-100-16): establishes the air traffic phraseology standards, complementing the provisions of **ICA-100-12**, "Regras do Ar" (Rules of the Air).

## Objective
Standardise the traffic flow around the aerodrome, facilitating:
- the sequence of landings/takeoffs,
- visual separation,
- the reduction of conflicts,
- consistent instructions and reports.

## Golden rule (information priority)
1. **VAC / AIP / aerodrome charts** (when published)
2. **NOTAM** and **local procedures**
3. Current **ATC instructions**
4. **Standard circuit** (this manual)

## Application
- **Controlled** aerodrome: TWR organises the traffic and authorises joining/leaving.
- **Uncontrolled** aerodrome: it is up to the pilot to maintain separation and follow published/locally accepted procedures.

!!! warning "Important"
    Recommended for simulation/training use. For real-world operations, always use the current official publications.

## How to use this manual
- Read **Standard circuit without VAC** and **Altitudes/profiles** first.
- Use the **PT/EN Phraseology** section as a quick reference card during the session.
- Use the **checklists** to standardise decisions at the TWR.

!!! tip "Scope and further reading"
    This manual covers **operation and decision-making at the TWR position**: circuit geometry, altitudes by category, sequencing, runway change and VAC reading.

    **Phraseology** has its own manual. If what you are looking for is how to speak, from start-up on the apron to closing the flight plan, in Portuguese, English and Spanish, go to the [VFR Phraseology Manual](../fraseologia-voo-visual/index.en.md).
