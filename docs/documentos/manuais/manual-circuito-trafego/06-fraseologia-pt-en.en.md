# Phraseology (PT/EN) — Traffic Circuit (TWR)

Below is a practical set of common circuit calls, in "catalogue" format for quick use.

!!! info "Complete reference"
    This page is a **quick reference card**, designed for the controller to have at hand during the session.

    The complete VFR phraseology, with an end-to-end flight in **Portuguese, English and Spanish**, covering Ground, Tower, Control, Information and Radio, is in the [VFR Phraseology Manual](../fraseologia-voo-visual/index.en.md).

!!! note "Example convention"
    - Callsign: **PT-ABC** (piston), **PR-JET** (jet), **PR-HLX** (helicopter)
    - Aerodrome: "TORRE"
    - Runway: RWY 16
    - Adjust to your situation (unit name, runway, QNH, etc.)

!!! warning "How this becomes real radiotelephony"
    The calls below are written in abbreviated form, for quick reading. On frequency:

    - the unit's callsign is always **TORRE followed by the location name** (Art. 37, MCA 100-16), never "TORRE" on its own;
    - the registration is read **letter by letter**, without a hyphen: PT-ABC becomes PAPA TANGO ALFA BRAVO CHARLIE;
    - numbers are read **digit by digit**: in Portuguese, pista 16 becomes pista UNO MEIA.

    Pronunciation details are in [VFR Concepts](../fraseologia-voo-visual/conceitos.en.md).

## 1) Passing runway/circuit information and joining the Downwind leg

=== ":flag_br: Portuguese"

    | Situation | Phraseology (TWR) | Note |
    |---|---|---|
    | Joining the circuit on the downwind leg | "PT-ABC, autorizado para o circuito de tráfego, pista 16, circuito à direita, vento 160 graus 08 nós, QNH 1013, reporte perna do vento." | If there is traffic, include information |
    | Traffic in sight and follow | "PT-ABC, tráfego à sua frente na perna do vento, confirme visual com o tráfego e siga o mesmo." | If negative, sequence again |

=== ":flag_gb: English"

    | Situation | Phraseology (TWR) | Observation |
    |---|---|---|
    | Circuit entry via downwind leg | "PT-ABC, cleared to traffic pattern, runway 16, right-hand circuit, wind 160 degrees 08 knots, QNH 1013, report downwind leg." | If traffic present, include information |
    | Traffic in sight and follow | "PT-ABC, traffic ahead on downwind leg, report in sight and follow." | If negative, sequence again |

## 2) Extend downwind leg / orbit / remain

=== ":flag_br: Portuguese"

    | Situation | Phraseology (TWR) | Note |
    |---|---|---|
    | Extend downwind leg | "PT-ABC, alongue perna do vento, aguarde instruções para girar base." | Helps spacing |
    | Orbit | "PT-ABC, efetue órbita na perna do vento, reporte completada." | If necessary, specify the side |
    | Remain outside the circuit | "PT-ABC, mantenha fora do circuito a oeste do aeródromo, 1500 pés <abbr title="Above Aerodrome Level/Altitude Acima do Aeródromo">AAL</abbr>, reporte pronto para ingressar." | Defines area + altitude |

=== ":flag_gb: English"

    | Situation | Phraseology (TWR) | Observation |
    |---|---|---|
    | Extend downwind leg | "PT-ABC, extend downwind leg, standby for further instructions for turning base." | Helps spacing |
    | Orbit | "PT-ABC, make one orbit on downwind leg, report completed." | If necessary, specify side |
    | Remain outside circuit | "PT-ABC, remain outside the circuit west of the aerodrome, 1500 feet <abbr title="Above Aerodrome Level">AAL</abbr>, report ready to join." | Defines area + altitude |

## 3) Landing / touch-and-go / low approach clearances

=== ":flag_br: Portuguese"

    | Situation | Phraseology (TWR) | Note |
    |---|---|---|
    | Clearing to land | "PT-ABC, pista 16, pouso autorizado, vento …" | Always include the wind |
    | Touch-and-go | "PT-ABC, pista 16, autorizado toque e arremetida." | Occupancy control |
    | Low approach | "PT-ABC, pista 16, autorizada passagem baixa, não toque, reporte passando a cabeceira." | Useful for training |

=== ":flag_gb: English"

    | Situation | Phraseology (TWR) | Observation |
    |---|---|---|
    | Landing clearance | "PT-ABC, runway 16, cleared to land, wind …" | Always include wind |
    | Touch and go | "PT-ABC, runway 16, cleared for touch and go." | Runway occupancy control |
    | Low approach | "PT-ABC, runway 16, cleared low approach, no touchdown, report passing threshold." | Useful for training |

## 4) Go-around

=== ":flag_br: Portuguese"

    | Situation | Phraseology (TWR) | Note |
    |---|---|---|
    | Go-around instruction | "PT-ABC, arremeta, mantenha proa da pista, suba para 1000 pés <abbr title="Above Aerodrome Level/Altitude Acima do Aeródromo">AAL</abbr>, ingresse na perna do vento." | Standardise heading + altitude |
    | Traffic on the runway | "PT-ABC, arremeta, pista ocupada." | Clarity and priority |

=== ":flag_gb: English"

    | Situation | Phraseology (TWR) | Observation |
    |---|---|---|
    | Go-around instruction | "PT-ABC, go around, maintain runway heading, climb to 1000 feet <abbr title="Above Aerodrome Level">AAL</abbr>, join downwind leg." | Standardize heading + altitude |
    | Traffic on runway | "PT-ABC, go around, runway occupied." | Clarity and priority |

## 5) Helicopters (low profile)

=== ":flag_br: Portuguese"

    | Situation | Phraseology (TWR) | Note |
    |---|---|---|
    | Low circuit/dedicated route | "PR-HLX, autorizado circuito baixo, mantenha 500 pés <abbr title="Above Aerodrome Level/Altitude Acima do Aeródromo">AAL</abbr> ao sul do aeródromo, reporte perna base." | Avoid crossing the fixed-wing final |
    | Crossing final (when safe) | "PR-HLX, cruze a final após o pouso do tráfego, reporte cruzando." | Only with final clear/safe |

=== ":flag_gb: English"

    | Situation | Phraseology (TWR) | Observation |
    |---|---|---|
    | Low circuit/dedicated route | "PR-HLX, cleared for low circuit, maintain 500 feet <abbr title="Above Aerodrome Level">AAL</abbr> south of the aerodrome, report base leg." | Avoid crossing fixed-wing final |
    | Cross final (when safe) | "PR-HLX, cross final after the landing traffic, report crossing." | Only with final clear/safe |

## 6) Jets / high performance (separation)

=== ":flag_br: Portuguese"

=== ":flag_gb: English"
