# Phraseology

## How to use this page

The examples come from Section VI of Chapter IV of MCA 100-16, dedicated to AFIS[^1]. Pilot transmissions are marked with `*`. The callsigns, frequencies and names are those used in the examples of the manual. Replace them with those of your position.

The RADIO callsign is the name of the location: **Rádio Altamira** in Portuguese, **Altamira Radio** in English.

## Initial call

| Portuguese | English |
| --- | --- |
| *Rádio Altamira, PT KST. | *Altamira Radio, PT KST. |
| PT KST, Rádio Altamira. | PT KST, Altamira Radio. |
| ONE 6344, Rádio Altamira, prossiga. | ONE 6344, Altamira Radio, go ahead. |

On final, "wind calm" comes on its own because there is no other traffic. With traffic, the traffic information comes first.

| PT EEA, Rádio Usiminas ciente. Informe alinhado para a partida. | PT EEA, Usiminas Radio roger. Report lined up for departure. |
| *PT EEA alinhado cabeceira zero cinco. | *PT EEA lined up runway zero five. |
| PT EEA, Rádio Usiminas ciente. Vento calmo. Informe fora do solo. | PT EEA, Usiminas Radio roger. Wind calm. Report airborne. |
| *PT EEA fora do solo aos zero dois. | *PT EEA airborne at zero two. |
| *PT EEA atinge e mantém nível zero quatro cinco, estimando Terminal Vitória aos quatro dois e Vitória aos cinco sete. | *PT EEA reaching and maintaining flight level zero four five, estimating Vitória Terminal at four two and Vitória at five seven. |
| PT EEA, Rádio Usiminas ciente, ingressando Terminal, chame Controle Vitória em uno uno nove decimal oito, caso negativo, Torre Vitória em uno uno oito decimal uno. | PT EEA, Usiminas Radio roger, entering Terminal, call Vitória Control one one nine decimal eight, if negative, Vitória Tower one one eight decimal one. |

"Entering runway" and "lined up" are pilot reports, not clearances. The RADIO answers with roger and requests the next report.

## What not to say

At the RADIO, control phrases become information phrases:

| Avoid (control) | Use (information) |
| --- | --- |
| Cleared to land runway 07. | Roger, wind calm. |
| Cleared for take-off. | Roger, wind calm. Report airborne. |
| Taxi to holding point runway 05. | Roger, report on holding point. |
| Hold position. | Known traffic is a Xingu on downwind leg runway zero five. |
| Number two, follow the Xingu. | Known traffic is a Xingu on downwind leg, ahead of you. |
| Climb and maintain FL 045. | (The RADIO does not assign levels. If there is a clearance from another unit, it is relayed, with the name of the unit that issued it.) |
