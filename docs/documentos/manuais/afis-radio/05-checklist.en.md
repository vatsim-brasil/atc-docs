# Quick Checklist

## Before opening the position

- [ ] I hold the **S2** rating and I am on the active Vatsim Brasil ATC Roster
- [ ] The `_R_TWR` position is in the package, with the callsign and frequency from the aerodrome page in the MOP
- [ ] I know the FIZ limits or, if there is no FIZ, that the service extends to 27 NM and below FL 145
- [ ] I know which TMA or CTR lies above or around the FIZ, and which approach or center is online
- [ ] I told whoever was covering the aerodrome top-down and asked about traffic already in the FIZ
- [ ] I have the aerodrome METAR, or the nearest one, and the NOTAM for aids out of service

## On every call

- [ ] I answered with **roger**, not with cleared
- [ ] I gave wind, altimeter setting, temperature and aerodrome conditions
- [ ] I gave the **known traffic**, or said there is no traffic reported
- [ ] I requested the next report, and on base leg or final the pilot reported the gear down and locked
- [ ] I left the choice of runway and spacing to the pilot

## On every IFR

- [ ] The clearance comes from approach or the center, never from me
- [ ] When relaying, I said **who issued it** and read the clearance as received
- [ ] I gave the IFR the VFR traffic in the circuit, and the VFR the IFR on the procedure
- [ ] I agreed with the control unit when the pilot changes frequency

## Before closing the position

- [ ] I told the approach or center that will cover the aerodrome
- [ ] I told the pilots on the frequency whom they will talk to, or that the aerodrome is left without an ATS unit
- [ ] No aircraft was left without reporting on the ground or without calling the next unit

## Pocket reference

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Network suffix | `_R_TWR` |
| Minimum rating | S2 |
| FIZ airspace | Class G |
| Area with no published FIZ | Movement area and Class G below FL 145, up to 27 NM |
| Standard answer to a report | Roger |
| Relay | "Cleared by the Center..." |
| No unit online | Designated frequency, or 122.800 (in the real world, FCA or 123.45) |
| Callsign | Location + Radio (Rádio Altamira in Portuguese) |
