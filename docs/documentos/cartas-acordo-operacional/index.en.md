# Letters of Agreement

**Letters of Agreement** (LOA) set out the procedures agreed between Vatsim Brasil and other organisations operating on the VATSIM network. They define each party's responsibilities, coordination and limits of action.

The terms of a letter apply to all Vatsim Brasil controllers and pilots once it has been approved and published.
